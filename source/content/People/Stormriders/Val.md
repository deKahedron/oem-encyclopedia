### Life
Val was a veteran of over half a dozen military campaigns, having served as a soldier and auxiliary unit. Eventually, she grew sick of being treated as a disposable drone and turned to the outlaw life out of spite, falling in with [[Waive Barin|Waive Barin's]] [[Stormrider Gang]].
### Personality
Val had a short temper and a crotchety manner. She had a deep loathing of nobility, and was greatly distrustful of [[Taeko Vostok]] when she joined the gang. Nevertheless, was humble, welcoming [[Becko]] and [[Clamour]] into the gang and recognising their skills in cooking and smithing that both surpassed her own.

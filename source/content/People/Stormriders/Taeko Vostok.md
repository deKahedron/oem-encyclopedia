### Early life
Taeko was the only child of [[Jerej Vostok|Count Jerej Vostok]] and [[Ulma Vostok|Countess Ulma Vostok]]. Her mother died while Taeko was still young, killed by the same werewolf that infected her father. In her mother's absence, her father found a way to spend time with Taeko y including her in his tinkering projects. Since he could not stand to touch silver, he entrusted Taeko with most of the intricate alloy-making, including teaching her the recipe for [[Acambarine|acambarine]].

When Taeko was in her twenties, her father told her about his infection.

In her thirties, Taeko spent some years studying at the [[University of Arcaxius]]. She showed talent with lightning magic and fulfilled her [[Contract of Contribution|contract of contribution]] by inventing a new method for extruding metal wire.

In the year 3426, the outlaw [[Waive Barin]] was arrested on Vostok lands. Taeko took pity on the bandit, and chose to release him before he faced her father's justice.
### Project Golden Blood
In the year 3430, Taeko accompanied her father to a secret council at the [[Weylin Damasil|Emperor's]] invitation. Here, the Vostoks were tasked to build intelligent constructed soldiers whose loyalties could not be bought by the [[Galigos Tinanbaria|Archduke]].

At the end of that year, Taeko and Jerej began work on what they dubbed [[Project Golden Blood]].

After a couple of early prototypes that served as a mechanical proof of concept, the Vostoks began to see potential in the project far beyond the creation of weapons. The pair invited [[Anasta Wulfhild]] to join the project to develop a language by which the constructs could be given identity.

Once this news reached the Emperor's court, [[Lusio Noven-Hai|Lord Noven]] accused the Vostoks of abandoning their mission and selling out the Emperor's secrets to an outsider. As punishment, he dispatched a force to take Castle Vosgar and arrest all three engineers.

Count Jerej held off the attackers, but was captured. Taeko and Anasta were able to escape with their most recent prototype. The two decided to part ways for safety. Anasta would search for new collaborators, while Taeko proposed to collect the funds and precious metals the project would need to continue.
### The Stormrider Gang
After she was forced to go on the run, Taeko was able to make contact with Waive Barin, asking to join his outlaw gang, the [[Stormrider Gang|Stormriders]].

On her first job with the gang, she met [[Yaykov]] and [[Maeve]] and took her first life. Over the following weeks, Taeko earned the gang's trust, as her powerful magic became their strongest asset. One day, after the death of [[Amos]], Waive proposed the game [[Hat (Game)|Hat]] to try to get Taeko to reveal her true motivations, but was calmed by her apparent lack of one. 

Eventually, the Stormriders robbed [[Kriv & Friends (party)|the outlaw Kriv Dilmerev and his companions]] after the latter had robbed an armoured convoy full of [[Magic Item|magical items]]. The hit appeared successful but, that night, while the Stormriders were carrying their spoils back to their hideout, half their number, including Taeko, were attacked by [[Kriv Dilmerev|Dilmerev]]. In the ambush, Taeko was shot through the mouth by [[Aurath|the tiefling Igna]] and only escaped after she was polymorphed into a moth by [[Ruark Emblethorn|Ruark the Fabulous]]. The injuries she sustained forever hampered her ability to speak, and made it dangerous for her to cast spells with verbal components.

In the following days, a squadron of knights sent by [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count Lathlin]] tracked the Stormriders to their hideout. During the attack, Taeko was captured and taken to a cell in [[Glaumdr Palace]].

Taeko was rescued from her imprisonment by the remainder of the Stormrider gang, after they had abandoned their leader Waive. While they escaped [[Highgale]] on the Count's pleasure barge, Taeko worked to create a placebo second soul for Maeve so that she could survive her next phase switch.
### Trouble at sea
After Maeve was originally saved, she was unable to sleep; instead she would completely shut down during the day. This took a great toll on her mental health.

Taeko tried to create an electrical second soul to fill the void of Dowe's death. The electrical being didn't work. It initially replicated behaviours and mannerisms of Maeve and Dowe, but over time became more repetitive, violent and volatile.

Eventually, the new Dowe dissociated from its body completely, extending its will into the ropes of the ship and imprisoned the Stormriders aboard their own hull. Over the following weeks, the raging construct captured three other vessels, roping them into a single phantom fleet.

Yaykov was able to evade capture long enough to contact his sister [[Yelena]], who arrived alongside the [[The Nameless Conspirators|Nameless Conspirators]] to rescue him and the other Stormriders. 
### Return to Arcaxius
After the Stormriders were saved and Maeve was slain, Taeko recovered Maeve's body from the wreck.

Upon boarding the [[The Silver Pelican|Silver Pelican]], Taeko discovered the true purpose of the expedition: to recruit her to continue her work on Project Golden Blood. Taeko staunchly refused, citing what she had witnessed happening to Maeve as proof that souls could not be created or simulated through magic. Additionally, she had no desire to see her and her father's legacy claimed by [[Fenbryte Industries]] as a patent. Yurtris Fenbryte teleported aboard the ship to continue the negotiation in person. In response to Taeko's refusal, she proposed that the price of the expedition ought to be covered by turning the Stormriders in to Count Lathlin.

In order to save her friends, Taeko agreed to join Fenbryte as a consultant. She bid farewell to her friends, admitting to them that Maeve's madness was a product of her own failure. In secret, she hoped that this would prevent them from trying to save her and getting themselves killed. Before leaving, Yaykov slipped Taeko a message on sheet of metal stolen from Ashes.

Taeko is fascinated by the find and questions Ashes directly about where she acquired the message, learning that the [[Caeran|Library]] was able to produce an alloy strikingly close to acambarine.

Taeko spent the return journey trying to become closer to the conspirators and learn more about them. Early on, she got them to play Hat with her.

After arriving at the University, Taeko confronted Anasta Wulfhild about selling out her family's work. Anasta was taken aback by the anger, defending that the project wouldn't exist at all without Fenbryte's backing.

For a time, Fenbryte still remained untrusting of Taeko, forbidding her from moving about the University without an escort. In order to earn her freedom from supervision, she stole [[Rädsla Furtch|Dr. Furtch's]] treatise about the workings of the brain from Sindri's workshop, telling Fenbryte that Sindri had been withholding the information that could prove crucial to the project.

Once she had free roam of the University grounds, Taeko decided to use Fenbryte's rivalry with [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]] to destroy both of them. She visited The Salvation to explain that [[Yurtris Fenbryte|The Plenty]] was trying to beat her to a source of immortality other than lichdom. She asked for help in sabotaging the project. The Salvation was able to draw Maeve's soul into a *Soul Cage* and give Taeko the two alloy books she had taken from [[Cthagas|Cthagas's]] body: Caeran's journal and spellbook.

After learning that the Nameless Conspirators were planning to delve into the depths of the Library to kill the [[The Librarian|Librarian]], she gifted Sindri the two books in the hope that she would have the change to speak with Caeran once the deed was done.

After Caeran's reactivation, Taeko contacted them in secret to coordinate the final steps of her plan.

At the grand reveal of the [[Indentured]], Taeko secretly swapped out [[Osvard Fastulsson|Osvard's]] *Soul Cage* with Maeve's and destroyed the [[Fenbryte's Formality|Formality contract]]. The Plenty herself caught onto the sabotage and was about to uncover the whole operation, so Taeko tried to activate the Indentured body herself. Her injuries failed her though, causing her *Lightning Bolt* to miscast and incapacitate her. Before Fenbryte could finish with Taeko, Ashes activated the machine, causing Maeve to reanimate and lash out at the spectators. As [[Zaledrix Almeyar|The Change]] evacuated [[Andrej Rusakov]], Maeve teleported herself and Taeko out of the chamber before [[Jaygo Coronal|The War]] could kill them.

While the Nameless Conspirators began to openly attack the liches, Taeko, Maeve and Caeran prepared all of the Indentured components they had been able to steal into nineteen complete bodies, hiding them throughout the Library so that they might collect the souls liberated by the destruction of the phylactery. Once they were done, Taeko and Caeran integrated a new pendular alternator into Maeve's metal frame, allowing a partition of Caeran themself to become Maeve's new daytime soul.
### The Voltscourge
After helping to create Maeve/Caeran, Taeko fell out with her friend over differences regarding the [[Indentured#Patented|Patented Indentured]]. Maeve/Caeran wished to help them find peace by gifting them the same dual-soul life that they had, meanwhile Taeko thought they could only be truly in control of their own choices if they could remember their past lives.

Taeko has since been on the run again, seeking out Patented Indentured wherever they may be and performing a delicate electro-surgical procedure on them to burn their Formality contracts without killing them. These Indentured are known as [[Indentured#Overvolted|Overvolted]].
### Personality
Taeko was always quite shy and stand-offish. After joining the Stormriders, she became more confident in her abilities, but learned how to manipulate others from how she was treated by Waive Barin. By the time of Maeve's downfall into madness, Taeko became very sensitive to the notion that she was a danger to anyone close to her.

She cared strongly about the few people she was close to, mainly her gangmates in the Stormriders. However, when interacting with strangers, she would often treat them as tools, hoping to use them before they tried to use her.

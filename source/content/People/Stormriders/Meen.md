### Life
During his teen years, Meen lived with a pod of [[Druid|druids]]. Eventually, the druids attempted to induct Meen into their order, which terrified her so much that she fled and never looked back. After wandering alone for some time, he eventually joined the Stormrider Gang under [[Waive Barin]].
### Personality
Meen has an amicable personality and a mostly optimistic outlook. However, he has a paranoid streak: she refuses to be left alone in the woods and is suspicious of trees.

Meen has never told anyone directly about what the druids wanted to do to him.

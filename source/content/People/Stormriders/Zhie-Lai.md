### Life
Zhie-Lai felt the calling of Bahamut shortly before joining [[Waive Barin|Waive Barin's]] Stormriders. She joined the gang as they were beginning to gain their revolutionary reputation.

Zhie suffered a serious wound on her left leg while escaping from [[Blue Lichen Point]] during the [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count's]] attack.
### Personality
Zhie-Lai is quiet and empathetic. She is aware of the awe that can be spread by cultivating an air of mysticism. As such, especially when in public, she speaks little and presents herself as a conduit of her god's mercy.

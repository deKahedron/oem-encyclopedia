### Life
Hao-Len was born to outlaw parents and has lived as a social outcast his whole life. He eventually joined the Stormrider Gang under [[Waive Barin]].

Hao-Len was killed by [[Kriv Dilmerev]] after the Stormriders stole a score from [[Kriv & Friends (party)|Dilmerev's gang]]. He was then resurrected by Waive Barin using the [[Ravenous Rose]]. Since his resurrection, he lived in constant pain, as his mortal wounds were stitched together with thorns.

Hao-Len was captured by [[Maeve]] alongside the rest of the Stormriders. When the gang was rescued by the [[The Nameless Conspirators|Nameless Conspirators]], Hao-Len's back was accidentally crushed by [[Osvard Fastulsson|Osvard]].
### Personality
Hao-Len has a very serious personality with a limited sense of humour. He was Waive Barin's most trusted enforcer, often tasked with the hardest role in any job.

Hao-Len initially had a strong dislike of [[Yaykov]], but over time grew to respect his new captain, especially as his trust in Waive began to falter.

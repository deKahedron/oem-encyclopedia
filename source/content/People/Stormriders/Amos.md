### Life
In his youth, Amos apprenticed to a hedge wizard, though he never learned any powerful magic. He worked many jobs throughout his life, but eventually fell down on his luck. Lacking any family, he joined the Stormrider Gang under [[Waive Barin]].

Due to his non-threatening appearance and sharp eyes, he was often assigned to scout and keep lookout.

Amos was killed by the bounty hunter [[Gracken Hast]] while keeping lookout for a job at [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count Lathlin's]] summer home.
### Personality
Amos was a long-winded man who lived for the drinks and chatter after a job. He was known for his tall tales, to the point that the game [[Hat (Game)|Hat]] was invented in his honour.

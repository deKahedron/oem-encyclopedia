### Life
As a [[Tiefling|tiefling]] born in [[Highgale]], Becko was thrust into slavery from birth. He was raised as a house servant and eventually found himself as an indentured manservant working in [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count Lathlin's]] summer home. In 3431, he was freed from his bondage by the Stormrider gang alongside [[Clamour]].

Becko remained with the Stormriders after this, serving as a new cook and accountant for the gang.

When the gang was held captive by [[Maeve]], Becko's experience dealing with mad tyrants was put to use deescalating Maeve's most violent breakdowns.
### Personality
Becko was a seemingly meek man. His brutal and traumatic formative experiences made him terrified of failure and prone to anxious sweating. However, despite his demeanour, he demonstrated immense courage on multiple occasions: while helping [[Jax]] and [[Meen]] infiltrate [[Glaumdr Palace]], the home of his abusers and while confronting the raging monster that Maeve was turning into, talking to her when the rest of gang wanted to kill or abandon her, and continuing to do so when she began threatening to harm the others.

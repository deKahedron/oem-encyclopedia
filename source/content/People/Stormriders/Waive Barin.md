### Youth
Waive grew up with his two fathers in a small fishing village in [[Hiamyth]]. As taxes increased and fishing yields remained small, Waive's fathers began smuggling goods across the [[Bay of Grief]] in order to make ends meet: one of them, a boatwright, built a small kiteboat for the other, a sailor, to fly across the waves at incredible speeds. Waive was taught to sail this boat, the *Stormrider*.
### Outlaw life
After Waive's parents were arrested, he fled to the streets with his friend [[Fizz]], turning to a life of crime and eventually forming the Stormrider Gang in honour of his father's boat.

In the year 3426, Waive was arrested on [[House Vostok|Vostok]] lands. [[Taeko Vostok]] took pity on the bandit, and chose to release him before he faced her father's justice.

Waive would eventually welcome Taeko into his gang, though he remained somewhat cynical of her intentions.

As the gang's luck began to turn sour, Waive's shortcomings began to show. His inability to assume his part of responsibility for the events or even slow down to mourn with his companions led to the [[Yaykov]] leading a mutiny against Waive.

The mutiny occurred in the immediate aftermath of Fizz's death and Waive accepting the [[Ravenous Rose]] as a gift from [[Aurath|Igna]]. When he was last seen, the Rose was rapidly consuming his left arm and vitality.
### Personality
As a leader of the Stormriders, Waive was charismatic and hopeful, though he had a tendency to focus too much on his companions' errors. His righteous rhetoric reflected his fantasies of making a difference in Hiamyth, though he made few acts of charity to the common people that didn't benefit the gang in some way.

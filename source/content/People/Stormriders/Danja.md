### Life
Danja was orphaned at a young age, being the child of revolutionary parents who died in a peasant revolt. She eventually joined the Stormrider Gang under [[Waive Barin]]. As one of the most senior members of the gang, she was often trusted with leading any group that Waive himself was not a part of.

Danja was killed by [[Aurath|Igna]] after the Stormriders stole a score from [[Kriv & Friends (party)|Kriv Dilmerev's gang]]. She was then resurrected by Waive Barin using the [[Ravenous Rose]]. Since her resurrection, she has lived in constant pain, as the arrow wound in her back was stitched together with thorns.
### Personality
Danja has a friendly if somewhat detatched personality. She enjoys reading ancient philosophy and has always had a very casual outlook on death.

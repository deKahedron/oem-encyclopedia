### Life
As a [[Tiefling|tiefling]] born in [[Highgale]], Clamour was thrust into slavery from birth. Due to her physical build and fire-resistant skin, she was trained as a smith for the army. In 3431, while performing forced labour at [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count Lathlin's]] summer home, she was freed from her bondage by the Stormrider gang alongside [[Becko]].

Clamour remained with the Stormriders after this, repairing their weaponry and working alongside [[Taeko Vostok]] to create magical alloys.

In helping the gang escape from Highgale, Clamour was seen by many biting in half a great chain that held open the marine portcullis to the city. This visceral, dramatic act of revolution became an icon of resistance among the tiefling population, and would eventually end in Clamour herself fighting for the freedom of her people.
### Personality
Clamour is a quiet, irritable woman. She dislikes being open about herself and acts defensively when questioned. However, when in silence or talking about work, she can be quite comfortable company.

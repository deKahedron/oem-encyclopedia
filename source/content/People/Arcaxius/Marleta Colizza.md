During her research, she discovered one of [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation's]] clones was acting as a spy around the university. Thinking the clone was tailing her personally, she killed it.

Out of spite, The Salvation sent several other clones after Marleta, driving her mad and forcing her to leave the University. Several years later, when Marleta was an old woman, [[V680-A]] tracked her down to her house in Hiamyth and killed her during the night.

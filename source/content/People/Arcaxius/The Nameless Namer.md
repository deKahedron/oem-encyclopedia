### Early Life
The Nameless Namer was born as Abakim and raised in a nomadic [[Elf#Eladrin|eladrin]] tribe. From their travels to the [[Feywild]], they learned to play games of [[True Name|names]] and music.

Abakim had always practiced his musical talent, becoming proficient with many string instruments. While staying in [[Rhyza]], Abakim studied musical theory and created the magical item *[[Abakim's Quartet]]*, a baton which could conjure a quartet of spectral instruments to harmonise with a player's melody.
### At the University
After leaving his tribe, Abakim began studying at the [[University of Arcaxius]], determined to prove his value despite the sneering of [[Arcane Magic#Wizards|Wizards]] toward [[Arcane Magic#Bards|Bards]].

Their persistence and tolerance for harsh criticism earned them the attention of the [[Wendallia Kirstein|professor of Onomancy]]. Abakim was one of the rare few students who weren't pushed away by the professor's methods. By the year 2910, it was unofficially recognised that Abakim would succeed the ancient professor after she died.

At one point, the wizard [[Gthumtreyk]] studied under The Salvation and, while he had become terrified of her and abandoned the course, he remained fond of Abakim, who had shown him more warmth.
### Surviving the Withering
After the death of [[Garthum Bleska|Professor Garthum Bleska]], Abakim was the first person to uncover that the Bleska had been murdered, by detecting the trace of a powerful necromantic spell in the [[Caeran|Library]], where the body had been found. Their warning was what allowed the resistance to form in time to try and stop the [[The Six Liches#The Clocktower Covenant|Clocktower Covenant]].

During the showdown in the Department of Chronurgy, as the battle was all but lost, Abakim was able to flee while holding the *Soul Cage* with the soul of [[Helem Thumlekh]]. While interrogating the captured soul, he was able to hide in the Library just long enough to improvise a unique [[Onomancy|onomantic]] ritual to cast off his name and identity, becoming the Nameless Namer. This allowed them to escape capture via [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation's]] *Gate* spell and thwart even Divination spells by [[Andrej Rusakov|The Truth]]. The Namer then escaped into the [[Astral Sea]], just barely avoiding disintegration by the freshly reanimated [[Leein Galvanis|The Joy]], who swore vengeance for his lost love, Professor Thumlekh.
### Exile
Before the *Soul Cage* ended, the Namer extracted Helem Thumlekh's true name and fed her soul to an astral dreadnought, destroying her forever.
As soon as they arrived in the Astral Sea, the Namer began single-mindedly plotting the downfall of the liches.
During this period, he contacted his former student Gthumtreyk, attempting to use him as an agent to build a network of enemies against the liches. He entrusted Gthumtreyk with the location of [[Caeran|Caeran's]] core, that the two might meet and form the first link of that alliance. However, while reaching the core, Gthumtreyk was tailed by the [[The Librarian|Librarian]]. Once the Librarian attacked him, the wizard panicked, assuming he had been caught in another cruel lesson by the Salvation. Fearing for his life and hoping to prevent Caeran from revealing him, he stole two of Caeran's central books and fled the University forever.


Despaired by the scope of the previous failure, the Namer searched the Astral Sea for other means of vengeance, including the [[Illithid|mind flayers]] and the [[Githyanki|gith]], but was let down at every turn. Eventually, it was he who was discovered, by the *Astral Projection* of the [[Kana'Lathen|Mother Stone Kana'Lathen]]. The Mother Stone became something of a mentor to the Namer, helping them rediscover peace through music and contemplation.

Over the centuries, the Namer came to accept the past, believing that they might never have revenge and accepting their own fortune at the second chance they had to survive that encounter. He began to make trips back to the [[Material Plane]], journeying under a myriad of disguises and identities. During one such trip, the Namer met and befriended [[Valarr]], who followed them around for a time and came to think of himself as their apprentice.

Once Valarr enrolled in Arcaxius, the Namer thought it best not to contact him again, lest their identity be uncovered and endanger them both. Nevertheless, the Namer kept an eye on Valarr, taking pride in his apprentice's accomplishments.
### Return to the fight
During the Summer of 3433, the Namer witnessed [[Andrej Rusakov|The Truth's]] [[Andrej Rusakov#Severance from the Weave|severance from the Weave]]. Despite having previously accepted that they would never have revenge, the Namer saw this as too great an opportunity to not resume action against the liches.

The Namer was curious about the adventurers he had seen escorting [[The Truth]] across the Astral Sea, but uncertain of their loyalties. In order to learn more about them, he began sending dreams and visions to the spirit-speaker [[Skrunceleclectos]], guiding him towards the University, The Salvation and the [[The Nameless Conspirators|Nameless Conspirators]].

Using the empty space left for Helem Thumlekh's soul, the Namer, with the help of the Mother Stone, was able to perform a ritual to bind himself to the liches' phylactery.

Because the ritual was still untested, the Namer remained secretive as he approached the University. He arrived the same night of [[Cthagas|Cthagas's]] murder. The sight of [[Ashes]], determined to kill The Salvation herself in revenge, assured the Namer that the conspirators could be trusted as fellow enemies of the liches.

The Namer accompanied the Nameless Conspirators on their mission to kill the Librarian and restore Caeran's control of the Library. During this expedition, the Namer shared The Salvation's true name with them. They spoke with [[Kana'Rhul|Nokwunn]] about meeting her mentor, but was unable to earn the monk's trust. Ashes, who had begun studying magic with Cthagas, was intrigued about the Namer's onomantic skills and received some tutoring from them.

Upon meeting the [[Fate Peddler]], the Namer traded away some of their luck in exchange for the confirmation that their ritual had successfully bound them to the phylactery.

After the Librarian was killed, The Namer was the first to ask Caeran about the nature of the phylactery, discovering that it was a minute of the day.

After the conspirators returned to the surface, the Namer joined them in watching [[Osvard Fastulsson|Osvard's]] fatal duel with [[Jaygo Coronal|The War]]. With a new confidence in their own immortality, even as Nokwunn and [[Marred Draymor|Marred]] joined the fight in open defiance of the liches, the Namer remained hidden.

After the fight was over, Osvard's body *Disintegrated* and the crowd scattered, the Namer approached the pile of dust and began casting *Valarr's Reversion*, his apprentice's own spell, to restore the body to a resurrectable state. During this casting, [[Leein Galvanis|The Joy]], who was arriving late to [[Zaledrix Almeyar|The Change's]] summons, recognised the Namer and, despite the best attempts of the conspirators, stunned and killed them out of revenge for Helem Thumlekh.
### Stolen lichdom
The Namer was reanimated four days later in a burst of magical light that shook the sky around the University. Their connection to the phylactery was not perfect, leaving them with a weakened magical ability. The Namer's return was witnessed by the liches, including The Salvation, who fled to her *Demiplane* in response, and The Joy, who was at once delirious and delighted that he might have more chances to torment his lover's killer.

During Ashes's birthday celebration, the Namer presented a plan to destroy the liches' phylactery by abusing his own soul's connection to it. The following day, as the first steps of this plan were being put in motion, The War, The Plenty and The Change attempted to call a truce with the Namer, welcoming him to their number on the condition that he disbands his allies. Before the Namer could answer the offer, Marred Draymor and [[Laubrey Lafay|Sir Laubrey Lafay]] attacked the liches. This created an opening for [[Helga]] to teleport the Namer, along with Ashes and Sindri, into the Tower of Change. From within the tower, the Namer cast *Tether Essence* to align the tower's distorted timescale with the minute that held his own soul. While Ashes and Sindri battled within the minute, the Namer withstood an assault by The Joy.
In the looped minute, Ashes and Sindri were able to confront the soul of The Change, eventually convincing them to surrender the phylactery and allow the liches to die.

After the [[The Broken Minute|breaking of the minute]], the Namer pressed the conspirators to hunt down the remaining four liches before they could escape. The first target to present himself was Leein Galvanis who, finally mortal again, wanted nothing more than to die while dragging the Namer to oblivion with him. The illusionist trapped the conspirators in a *Leein's Hall of Mirrors*, isolating the Namer, torturing them and eventually killing them with *Finger of Death*.

The conspirators spared no time to acknowledge their fallen ally before pursuing The War. It is unknown what happened to his body and possessions.
### Skills
The Nameless Namer was an extremely talented arcanist in several different fields, demonstrating skills without peer.

* **Naming.** The Namer was particularly skilled at elemental onomancy, knowing the names of many inanimate elements.
* **True Name Change.** When escaping the Clocktower Covenant, the Namer was able to change their true name in an instant using magic.
* **Magical Trace Detection.** The Namer showed extremely sensitive psychometric abilities. He was able to identify spells that had been cast long before, and was even able to trace the connection between Helem Thumlekh's soul and the phylactery using only her name and the memory of having held her *Soul Cage*.

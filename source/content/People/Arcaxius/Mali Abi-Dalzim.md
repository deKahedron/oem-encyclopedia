### Life
Mali was a professor at the time of the [[The Six Liches#The Withering|Withering]]. On that day, he was asked to substitute for Professor [[Jaygo Coronal]] in leading one team of the welcoming [[Chain Lightning (Sport)|Chain Lightning]] game.

After [[The Nameless Namer|Abakim]] discovered that professor [[Garthum Bleska|Bleska]] had been murdered, Mali ran to speak with Professor [[Sharna Harrow|Harrow]], but was attacked by the liches. Barely escaping with his life, he joined the fight against the [[The Six Liches#The Clocktower Covenant|Clocktower Covenant]].

During the ensuing battle, Mali was killed by [[Yurtris Fenbryte]] and [[Leein Galvanis]]. His soul was captured and used to reanimate [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]].

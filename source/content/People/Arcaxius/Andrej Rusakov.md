### Early Life
Andrej Rusakov grew up in the [[Hiamyth|mythian]] city of [[Dobruje]]. From an early age, he showed a great interest in [[Mundane Sciences|science]] and the [[Arcane Magic|arcane]]. When he was only seven, he was taken on by a court wizard as an apprentice.
### At the University
In 2898, Rusakov was invited to hold the title Professor of Divination at the University of Arcaxius.

With the vast resources available to him at the University, Rusakov spent countless hours studying the flow of [[Magic|magic]] and the [[The Weave|Weave]] itself, ambitiously hoping to devise a unifying theory that could explain where magic itself came from.

His lack of sleep and excessive use of divination magic for his observations caused him to develop [[Chronic Divination]], a condition which caused him to see things in the future, other locations or other planes unwillingly and without warning. As his condition worsened, it threatened to lead to his death or complete inability to continue practising magic safely.
### Ascension to lichdom
After he became a lich, little changed in The Truth's daily life. He remained fully focused on his investigations, killing himself to regenerate his body every time his disease became too much to handle.

However, with his mortality gone, he became much slower and safer in his research, conducting more repetitive experiments and waiting for perfect conditions for each one.
### Research into a Unifying Theory
Since before Andrej was born, the general understanding of magic had been that [[Arcane Magic|arcane magic]] followed laws entirely separate from [[Divine Magic|divine magic]] and psionics. Andrej had long believed this to be an incomplete understanding, and made it his life's ambition to discover the underlying mechanics that tied together all magic.

Using a [[Luruthi|luruthi]] [[Amber Spyglass|amber spyglass]], he coined the term [[Rusakov Particles]] to describe the variable-mass motes of potential that compose the Weave.

Building on the research of [[Thereminumpheros]], [[Babaaji]] and [[Cedia Hemn]], The Truth was able to reduce the relationship between a spell's power and the effort required to cast it down to a single equation. He published his findings in 3417, naming it [[The Laws of Magic#Rusakov's Law|Rusakov's Law]].

While Rusakov's Law was a great milestone, it still did not explain the origin of magic. The Truth wanted to explore this topic further, pushing his research into [[Ley Line|ley lines]] and the flow of the Weave. 
### Severance from the Weave
In 3433, The Truth's research assistant [[Rufus Thornwelle]] was killed as a sacrifice by the [[Yuan-ti|yuan-ti]] of [[Ssai-Sh'aa]]. Shortly afterwards, the Professor took on [[Cthagas]] as a new assistant. Cthagas, unlike Thornwelle, asked questions about The Truth's theories much more openly.

After initially being annoyed, Rusakov chose to discard the dead end he was working on and make some more observations. By studying the seemingly-random patterns of Rusakov Particles across the planes, The Truth was able to divine a faint but consistent trend of the particles away from a single point in the [[Astral Sea]]. *Astrally Projecting* himself, along with Cthagas, [[Ashes]], [[Wathop]], [[Nokwunn]] and [[Gwendolyn]] as an escort, he tracked this flow back to its origin point, a [[Sydic cluster|remnant of the oldest spell]]. Here, he was able to steal a glimpse at the fundamental inner workings of magic, but denied Cthagas a look.

What he saw at the Sydic cluster shattered his understanding of magic so wholly that it afflicted him with the only known case of [[Rusakov's Paradox]]. This event led him to being completely cut off from the Weave. He could no longer cast spells nor be affected by magic directly. Crucially, it also cut him off from his phylactery, rendering him a mortal human once again.
### Return to Teaching
At [[Zaledrix Almeyar|The Change's]] recommendation, Professor Rusakov tried to teach classes again. Unfortunately, his lack of conviction in what he was teaching was readily apparent to his students, who began to abandon his classes in droves. Before giving up entirely, Rusakov passed his tutorage over to [[Leein Galvanis|The Joy]].
### Injury
At [[Yurtris Fenbryte|The Plenty's]] special presentation of her new [[Indentured]] constructs, professor Rusakov suffered a deep laceration to the neck from the newly awaken [[Maeve]]. The ensuing trauma and blood loss left him in a coma until after the phylactery had been destroyed, and The Change, The Joy, [[Jaygo Coronal|The War]] and [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]] were all dead.
### Death
On the 3rd Godsday of Autumn, 3435, Andrej Rusakov died peacefully in his sleep.

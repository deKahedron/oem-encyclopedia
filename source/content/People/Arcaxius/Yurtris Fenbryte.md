Yurtris Fenbryte, The Plenty, was one of the [[The Six Liches|Six Liches]] of [[Arcaxius]] and the head of Fenbryte Industries. Before the [[The Six Liches#The Withering|Withering]], she was a professor at the University and held the position of Archmage of Artificery.

### Early Life
Fenbryte grew up as the only child of a powerful family with high-ranking members in several Nadirian guilds. At a young age, she began an apprenticeship under her aunt, a master of the Architects Guild. Propelled by both natural talent and her familial connections, she became a guild master herself in 2703.
### As Guild Master
As an architect, Yurtris was unlike most gnomes. In gnomish culture, the creative process is typically enjoyed as a deeply social and collaborative activity. Fenbryte, while still regularly collaborating with other masters, treated her apprentices and journeymen with little regard for their contributions, commanding and directing her teams more so than leading them.

Fenbryte was an early adopted of the *Sending* spell, using it to coordinate her investments and projects across the continent and react to global events faster than any of her competitors.
### At the University
In the year 2835, Yurtris Fenbryte was invited to the University of Arcaxius to teach artificing. While she accepted the title of Archmage, she did so on the condition that her personal lectures be very limited, allowing to continue devoting most of her time to growing her own company. It was in the environment of freedom that Arcaxius afforded her, away from the Nadirian guilds, that Fenbryte Industries took shape.
### As a lich
During the centuries that the liches ruled Arcaxius, Fenbryte Industries remained one of the main sources of paid work for any independent researchers studying there without a wealthy patron. 
### Influence on the Umaran Empire
During the rise and height of the [[Umaran Empire]], The Plenty and her company kept close relations with the Nadirian army. While they didn't create the designs, Fenbryte Industries was hired to assist in mass-production of many Nadirian civil projects and weapons, from [[Baculurb|baculurbs]] to [[Psychic Bell|psychic bells]].

Yurtris's main personal contribution to the Umaran Empire was [[Marhew]], a city carved from a plateau of marble using acid siphoned from [[Corrosil's Tear]]. Fenbryte herself was in Marhew during the [[Great Humbling]], and was killed by the chalican attack on her city.
### Project Golden Blood and exile
In Spring of 3432, [[Anasta Wulfhild]] contacted The Plenty for assistance in completing [[Project Golden Blood]]. The project, which aimed to build a versatile, programmable construct, had been aborted after the headquarters at [[Castle Vosgar]] was raided. With the project leader [[Jerej Vostok]] presumed dead, Wulfhild and [[Taeko Vostok]] went their separate ways for safety.

After meeting with Wulfhild to examine the prototype [[Clover]], Fenbryte saw the experiment as a way to one-up her rival, [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]], by beating her to her goal of creating a cleaner alternative path to immortality. Knowing that The Salvation was herself quite close to completing her own research, Fenbryte put every other project on hold and fully rebuilt her tower into a hub for building the first [[Indentured]].

As part of her new focus on Project Golden Blood, Fenbryte hired two of the greatest construct engineers from across the continent: [[Eiterox Lyren]] and [[Sindri Son of Brokken|Sindri, Son of Brokken]]. At Wulfhild's insistence, she also sent a crew of adventurers on her steamboat, [[The Silver Pelican]], to retrieve Taeko Vostok.

After Vostok was located aboard a ship with the rest of the [[Stormrider Gang|Stormrider gang]], she refused to continue working the project knowing that it had been taken over by Fenbryte. To force her participation, Yurtris threatened to turn the Stormriders over to the [[Galigos Tinanbaria|Archduke of Hiamyth]].

In the following weeks, Vostok gained some trust from Fenbryte by betraying Sindri, Son of Brokken and turning over [[Rädsla Furtch|Dr. Furtch's]] research papers to aid in the development of Heart Bulbs.

After the [[The Librarian|Librarian]] was killed, Yurtris Fenbryte planned a grand reveal of the first prototype [[Indentured]]. The experiment was intended to demonstrate the ability to control and command any soul that was placed into one of these constructs, with the soul of [[Osvard Fastulsson|Osvard]] being used as an example. However, the demonstration was sabotaged by Sindri, [[Ashes]] and Taeko, who broke the Formality Contract in the shell and swapped out Osvard's soul cage for [[Maeve|Maeve's]].

As the newly-rebuilt Maeve raged in the test chamber, [[Andrej Rusakov]] was seriously wounded.

After her [[The Broken Minute|phylactery]] was destroyed, The Plenty struck a deal for her own life with the [[The Nameless Conspirators|Nameless Conspirators]]. In return for allowing her to flee Arcaxius, she gave them a scroll of *Demiplane*, keyed to The Salvation's own sanctum. Before she left, however, Osvard assured her that her escape would last only as long as it took him to find her. Wearily, she acknowledged the threat.

Over the following two years, Fenbryte was on the move between all of her workhalls and factories across the continent, rarely more than one step ahead of Osvard and Sindri. During this time, she was focused entirely on finishing Project Golden Blood, in the hopes she could secure her own rebirth after Osvard caught up to her.
### Death
Around the time that the first stable Indentured were being produced by Fenbryte Industries, Andrej Rusakov died. Crestfallen at the loss of her friend, Yurtris suddenly felt very lonely and ready to pass on the torch to her constructed successors.

She sent an invitation to the Nameless Conspirators to battle her at all her strength in her factory in [[The Fumes]]. After destroying what resistance she was able to put forward, the adventurers cornered her in her office. Here, she proposed one final deal.

After the phylactery had been destroyed, Cthagas's soul was freed, and was captured by a *Soul Cage* within an Indentured prototype. Understanding that there was little she could do to save her own life, she asked the conspirators to sign a *Fenbryte's Formality* contract, agreeing to never again interfere with the operations of Fenbryte Industries, in exchange for the construct containing [[Cthagas|Cthagas's]] soul. The conspirators begrudgingly agreed. In her final moments, she stepped to the window for a smoke. While gazing out upon the yellow clouds of the valley below, Sindri's owl construct, which had been chasing her since the battle at Arcaxius, dropped the [[Impossible Geometry]] on her head from high in the sky, killing her instantly.
### Personality
Yurtris Fenbryte was a very callous woman, who understood life as a series of power plays and zero-sum exchanges.

While she was a favourite of her parents, she had a very difficult relationship with her descendants, having missed her son's funeral on the day of the [[The Six Liches#The Withering|Withering]].

She was very proud. She rarely had much use for the opinions of others unless she had requested them. Despite this, she often spared the effort to make her designs and buildings accessible, being amputee herself (her right arm had to be amputated at birth). She saw herself strongly reflected in her great-granddaughter [[Yetan Shkenning]], but could never understand why the latter would refuse to follow in her footsteps.

Yurtris clearly felt some discomfort in her undead form: she was rarely seen without her *Dusk-Tinted Glasses*, a pair of lenses which inhibited her [[Truesight]], allowing her to see the illusory body she presented to the world. Additionally, her smoking habit became much more constant during her time as a lich. Those close to her said that she did it to feel warmth within her body again.

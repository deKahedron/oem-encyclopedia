### Life
Before she was invited to hold the title of Archmage of Enchantment, Sharna worked as an investigator and interrogator for the [[Kastak]] city guard.

At the University, she was known for her grisly stories and cynical view of morality.

During the [[The Six Liches#The Withering|Withering]], she was one of the first archmages targeted by the [[The Six Liches#The Clocktower Covenant|Clocktower Covenant]], alongside Professor [[Sora Hiramo]].

Centuries later, [[Zaledrix Almeyar|The Change]] used Sharna's *Inquisitor's Dagger* to interrogate prisoners, including [[Sindri Son of Brokken|Sindri, Son of Brokken]].

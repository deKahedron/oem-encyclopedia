### Life
Ingritte was a professor at the time of the [[The Six Liches#The Withering|Withering]]. On that day, she welcomed new students by leading one team in an open game of [[Chain Lightning (Sport)|Chain Lightning]].

After [[The Nameless Namer|Abakim]] discovered the conspiracy afoot, Ingritte was one of the first people to take action against the liches.

During the showdown with the [[The Six Liches#The Clocktower Covenant|Clocktower Covenant]], Ingritte created a *Sphere of Annihilation* which disintegrated [[Helem Thumlekh]]. Later in the battle, Ingritte herself was slain by a *Disintegrate* from [[Jaygo Coronal|The War]], who captured her soul in a *Soul Cage*. Before she could be used to fuel a lich's return, [[Yorum Teris|Yorum Kell]] wrestled The War into the *Sphere of Annihilation*, destroying the cage and freeing Ingritte's soul.
### Legacy
While she was not the most skilled wizard ever, Ingritte Skårgard is remembered as one of the most powerful wizards of history by pure metrics. Her creation of the first *Sphere of Annihilation* was the highest-[[The Laws of Magic#Power (P)|power]] spell ever recorded, at 1.07 Gþ. She was suspected to have sorcerous blood in addition to her learned mastery of [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] formulae.

### Life
Diana Hexenwelt was taught from a young age how to ride the giant bees of [[Waxwick]]. She was an adventurer for a couple of years until she was hired as a hunter by the University of Arcaxius.

On the day of the [[The Six Liches#The Withering|Withering]], Diana attended a negotiation between professor [[Zaledrix Almeyar]] and a diplomat from [[Fuegil]]. After the meeting, Diana was told to take the day off.

After the assassinations of professors [[Sora Hiramo|Hiramo]] and [[Sharna Harrow|Harrow]], Diana joined the efforts to stop the [[The Six Liches#The Clocktower Covenant|Clocktower Covenant's]] plans, organising the evacuation of students and staff.

During the ensuing battle, Diana struggled to see through professor [[Leein Galvanis|Leein Galvanis's]] phantasms. After [[The Nameless Namer|Abakim]] escaped the scene, Diana remained as the last opposition to the now four liches. She yielded and her life was spared, but she was sworn to secrecy with a *[[Fenbryte's Formality]]* contract.

She retired back to her home town and started a family.

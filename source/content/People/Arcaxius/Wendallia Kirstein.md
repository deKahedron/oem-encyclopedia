### Early Life
Wendallia Kirstein was the third of four sisters born into a sylvan noble family. She grew up in her ancestral home at the [[The Empty Manor Upon a Hill]].

Largely ignored by her family, she was allowed to pursue magic at her leisure. She frequently met with wild [[Fey]], becoming fascinated with [[True Name|true names]] and [[Onomancy|onomancy]].
### Study at the University
In 2326, in her late nineties, Wendallia left home to take up study at the [[University of Arcaxius]]. At the time, the memory of [[Vecna]], the first lich, was still fresh. Wendallia's curiosity led her to discover Vecna's spellbook, the [[The Book of Vile Darkness|Book of Vile Darkness]]. She eventually turned this book in to the [[Caeran|Library]], which earned her much acclaim as a researcher, but not before she had studied it closely and performed a select few experiments of her own.

In the year 2547, she was granted the position of Archmage of Onomancy. As a professor, she was known to be extremely demanding, pushing away most of her students entirely. However, for those with thick enough skin to take her criticism, she was a vast source of knowledge.

In 2902, some of the other professors felt that Kirstein was too old to hold for someone without a clear successor, given the scarcity of onomancers. Begrudgingly, she took on the [[Bard|bard]] [[The Nameless Namer|Abakim]] as a direct apprentice. By the year 2910, Abakim was unofficially confirmed as her successor for the position of archmage.
### Ascension to lichdom

### As The Salvation
Shortly after becoming a lich, and in an attempt to make her true name as hidden as possible, The Salvation murdered her entire dynasty, erasing their name, heraldry and memory from as many places and minds as she could.

The Salvation spent most of her time pursuing immortality even beyond lichdom. Officially, this was so that the Six would be the last liches ever needed, and future wizards could pursue less destructive methods of prolonging their life. However, she also secretly feared betrayal by the other liches, and wanted to ensure she would never truly die, no matter what happened.

Her research eventually produced the Clone spell. While developing the spell, many of her prototypes failed and unexpectedly came alive. The Salvation kept several of the more able-bodied and mentally stable ones as servants. Of all these servants, the one that lasted the longest was [[Ashes]].

After [[Andrej Rusakov|The Truth's]] severance from the [[The Weave|Weave]], The Salvation became much more suspicious of her fellow liches, feeling that their pact was nearing its end. At this point, she tried to replace her servant with a [[V972-R|new clone]], since Ashes had formed too many external attachments. Ashes, with help from [[Cthagas]] and the sudden return of [[Yurtris Fenbryte|The Plenty]] to the University, was able to escape.

Over the following weeks, The Salvation unleashed a new clone servant, [[Helga|V973-H]], to infiltrate Ashes's friends and learn their true names and Ashes's location.

Shortly after this, the [[The Librarian|Librarian]] took complete control over the Library, destroying all of The Salvation's backup clone jars. This made The Salvation much more careful about losing her two remaining clones, Ashes and Helga.

Upon the return of Ashes's posse from their journey aboard [[The Silver Pelican]], The Salvation ordered Helga to earn Ashes's trust, which she then used to betray and murder Cthagas.

When Ashes confronted her, The Salvation said that Cthagas's soul was being consumed by her [[Phylactery|phylactery]], and could only be rescued if Ashes replaced him with more powerful soul. At this time, The Salvation was weighing which of her two clones would be a more suitable vessel for her possession, and wanted to test Ashes's natural arcane talents. To conduct this test, she entrusted Ashes with the Librarian's true name, promising that if Ashes killed it while pronouncing the name, Cthagas's soul would be freed.

When Ashes returned with the task completed, The Salvation handed her an empty soul cage enchanted with *Nystul's Magic Aura* to appear as though it contained Cthagas's soul. The Salvation's hope was to isolate Ashes by making her think that her friend wasn't returning because he didn't want to, rather than because he was trapped in the phylactery.

As the conflict between the [[The Nameless Conspirators|Nameless Conspirators]] and the liches reached a boiling point, The Salvation retreated to her personal *Demiplane* to wait out the outcome. She only intervened to try and prevent Ashes and [[Sindri Son of Brokken]] from destroying the phylactery. However, in her unwillingness to put herself at risk, she only travelled there via *Banishment*, and was returned to her *Demiplane* when her concentration was broken.

As the [[The Broken Minute|phylactery]] was destroyed, a wave of dispelling wave rippled over the University, deactivating most magical effects and killing Helga. 

After The Plenty sold out her fellow lich by gifting the Conspirators a scroll of *Demiplane*, The Salvation was attacked in her own sanctum. With her phylactery gone, only one of her clones remaining and her next batch far from ready, The Salvation saw only one way out, by possessing Ashes and wresting control of her body. During the fight, she locked [[Marred Draymor|Marred]] and [[Osvard Fastulsson|Osvard]] in *Forcecages* and killed Sindri with *Power Word: Kill* before attempting to weaken Ashes's resistance with *Power Word: Ruin*.

As Ashes killed her creator, her body drew in The Salvation's soul, as it had been designed to do. However, through the support of her friends, the sacrifices of the echoes of the other clones and a deal struck with the [[Fate Peddler]], Ashes was able to exorcise The Salvation, killing her for good.

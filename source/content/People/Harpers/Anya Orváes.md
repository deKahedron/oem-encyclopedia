### Adventuring career
Anya fled her home in her youth, having been shunned by her family for her tiefling nature.

She found community in the Harpers, becoming a well-known freelance adventurer, joining any assignment that caught her fancy, though she never remained long in a single adventuring party.
### Personality
By the time she joined the guild, Anya had severed her own horns off. She claims this was because the right one got in the way of her bowstring and leaving only the left was unbalanced. She likes painting the stumps of her horns.

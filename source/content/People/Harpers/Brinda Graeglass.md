### Adventuring career
Brinda was heralded as a sign of favour from [[Oghma]] since she was born. Because of her special standing, she was allowed to learn [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] [[Magic|magic]], despite living in a [[Sophism|sophic]] community.

As the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]] became more and more averse to the practice of magic, Brinda eventually felt that she was no longer welcome in her own home region.
### Personality
While at work, Brinda comes across as very stern and spares little time for levity. She enjoys calm, quiet pastimes like knitting and gardening.

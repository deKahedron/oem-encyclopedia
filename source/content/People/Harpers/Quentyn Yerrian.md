### Adventuring career
Quentyn was in a small group of [[Liengard|Liengardian]] adventurers for some years. Together, they slew a white dragon of [[Bastorin's Spawn]]. In 3401, he joined the Harpers.

In the year 3412, Quentyn subjected himself to the experiments of a [[Dwarf#Duergar|duergar]] smith, who wove a suit of [[Mithral|mithral]] mail onto the inside of Quentyn's skin. This made him nigh-impervious to slashing attacks, but made him quick to tire and slurred his speech.

At two different points in his career, he attempted to join the [[Candleguard]], first when it was under the leadership of [[Lily Hexenwelt]], then under [[Qaoatcazatl]]. He was rejected both times.
### Promotion to High Harper
Quentyn was elected High Harper to 3429, succeeding the 17-year-long term of his predecessor, [[Iva Hidderstonn|Joen Iva Hidderstonn]].
### Personality
Quentyn is a slow-speaking, shrewd man who feels deeply responsible for the safety of his underlings, despite the dangers of their line of work.

He has long had a fascination with the Candleguard. This may have started when he was bequeathed [[Erach Kuhn|Erach Kuhn's]] Belt of Giant Strength as his first reward from the Harpers guild.

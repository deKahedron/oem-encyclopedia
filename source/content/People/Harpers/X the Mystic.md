### Life
Very little is known about X's personal life. In the early 31st century, adventurers and travellers began finding scattered inscriptions of survival advice signed with an X.

Some years later, the being known as X the Mystic began appearing in dark, dangerous dungeons, exploring on his own accord and offering useful advice.

Many have suggested that X may be a [[Shambler|shambler]], the strange, seemingly unique traders that roam the [[Underdark]]. Why he alone among his kind seems to be able to travel the surface is unclear.

X was officially recruited to the Harpers in 3426. He rarely takes assignments with others, but has proven invaluable as a scout.
### Skills
X is able to meld into flat surfaces like a shadow, travelling silently. In the dark, he is all but invisible and intangible, except for his glowing violet eyes. With his eyes closed, he is undetectable by anything save [[Truesight]].

X is well-known for his encyclopaedic knowledge of common dungeon-dwelling monsters and the most effective ways to deal with them.
### X the Mystic's Rules of Dungeon Survival
1. "Trust a Flumph"
2. "Before you drink from a fountain or pool, toss a copper into it. It's a small price to pay for your life."
3. "Sometimes a chest is just a chest, but don't bet on it."
4. "No one carves statues of frightened warriors. If you see one, keep your eyes closed and your ears open."
5. *Many people swear that they have heard X tell them a rule number 5, but no one actually remembers what it is.*
6. "Keep a few gems in your pocket. A hungry xorn is a helpful xorn."
7. "Before opening a sarcophagus, light a torch."

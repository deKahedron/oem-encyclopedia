### Life
Quaal hatched just before the start of the [[The Hunger War|Hunger War]], and grew up in extreme poverty, collecting the few scraps of colourful materials he could find. Watching a few heroes turn the tide of the situation in his city, he became enamoured with the classic ideals of heroism.

Eventually, he decided to move to [[Newhearth]] to work for the Harpers, first by helping to fix gear as an apprentice tailor, then eventually becoming one of the guild's main outfitters.

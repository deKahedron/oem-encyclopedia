### Life
Rega remembers little of her life before [[Baphomet|Baphomet's]] death in 3430.

During the [[The Hunger War|Hunger War]], Rega was living in [[Korom Sul]], where she fought alongside many other minotaurs. During the aftermath of the battle, her discernment that the gnolls were going through an experience similar to her own led her to show them mercy.

Some years later, she met the Candleguard while helping an unsupervised settlement of gnolls fend off a tlincalli attack. [[Qaoatcazatl]] saw in Rega someone who was willing to ignore outside rules in order to do the right thing, and offered the minotaur the chance to join the most famous adventuring party on the continent.

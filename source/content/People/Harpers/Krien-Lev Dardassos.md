### Life
Krien-Lev trained as an alchemist's apprentice in [[Cal Chromis]]. He discovered that he was more interested in tinkering with the machines in the lab than learning the chemical formulas, and eventually moved to [[Newhearth]] to become an outfitter for the [[Harpers]]. On his journey there he faced some danger and got his first taste of battle, putting his weapons to the test.

For some years he served as an engineer and smith for the Harpers, inventing tools like the [[Spring-Activated Dispenser|S.A.D.]]. During this time, he would still find time to join field assignments. He also developed a rivalry with fellow Harper artificer [[Zanti Patrachia|Zanti the Bullet]].
### Candleguard
After the disaster at the [[Labyrinth Beyond Waking Sight]], High Harper [[Quentyn Yerrian]] wanted to put the Candleguard on a tighter leash. Despite [[Qaoatcazatl|Qaoatcazatl's]] complaints, Krien-Lev was assigned to join the team.

Krien-Lev has yet to find his place within his new party. The veterans largely distrust him, and only [[Rega Anganott]], who joined shortly after him, treats him with the same warmth as she does everyone else.

### Life
Yorum Teris grew up in [[Dartûm]] and was knighted in his youth after squiring for a knight of the [[Order of Marble]].

In the year 2910, he enrolled at the [[University of Arcaxius]]. By this time, he was already proficient in some simple [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] spells. In the days before the academic year began, he befriended the University's head of security [[Diana Hexenwelt]].

During the Withering, Yorum was the only student who joined the resistance against the [[The Six Liches#The Clocktower Covenant|Clocktower Covenant]]. During the ensuing battle, he was able to save [[Ingritte Skårgard|Ingritte Skårgard's]] soul from being consumed. Yorum was then killed when [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]] rose and slew him with his name.

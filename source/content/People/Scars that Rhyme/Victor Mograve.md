### Career
Victor co-owns [[Cassic and Mograve's Trove of Antiquities]] alongside [[Ormus Cassic]], inheriting the job from his mother.

For a long time, Victor has had a working business relationship with the [[Harpers]], identifying, appraising and selling the artefacts they found. In some cases, he would even count on the Harpers to recover specific items for his collection. However, since [[Quentyn Yerrian]] became High Harper, his relationship with the guild has soured. He continues to work with them, but his personal involvement with the guild has lessened.

### Life
Epidma grew up in a large family in [[The Fumes|the Fumes]]. Her father was a scout for a [[Fenbryte Industries]] mining team. She has several older siblings, at least one of whom fought for the [[Umaran Empire]] against the chalicans.

When she decided to join the [[Harpers]], her brother gifted her the pistol [[Plan C]], a modified flintlock, as a good luck charm.
### As an adventurer
Epidma banded together with an old friend and a travelling acquaintance, agreeing that they would all join the Harpers together. To test their resilience, they chose to make [[Mount Jeong-Yo#Pariah's Climb|Pariah's Climb]] up to [[Newhearth]]. Epidma's companions both died on the journey. When she herself was near the summit, she was attacked by a yeti. Using Plan C to push it off a ledge, the noise of the gunshot caused an avalanche which buried her under snow, from which she was rescued by [[Kridix Thundersoul]]. As thanks, she gifted him her brother's pistol.

Kridix and his companions helped Epidma reach Newhearth, where she promised them she would find a doctor before joining the Harpers.

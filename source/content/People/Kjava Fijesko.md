Kjava Fijesko was a princess of the Fijesko dynasty of [[Hiamyth]]. She was at the centre of the political upheaval that resulted in the recognition and legalisation of gender transition in Hiamyth and, subsequently, the [[Umaran Empire]].

### Succession crisis
Princess Kjava is most known for her role in legitimising trans rights in Hiamyth. As the oldest "male" child of [[Desmond Fijesko IV|King Desmond IV]], she was the heir to the throne. For as long as her father lived, she hid her identity, only coming out to her brother in secret.

After her father's death, the princess announced her identity publicly. To add a sense of credibility to the process, she renounced the throne, as the laws of succession dictated her younger brother would be ahead of any of his sisters.

Partly because his legitimacy as king hung on the legitimacy of his sister's transition and partly because of the closeness between the two, [[Sergej Fijesko|King Sergej II]] would end up passing many laws protecting and normalising all experiences across the gender spectrum.

However, these new laws created the potential for a new problem. A future transgender prince would shake up the line of succession, giving pretext for civil wars. In order to prevent this, a new decree was signed into law on the day of Princess Kjava's death, ratifying that succession would be determined exclusively by primogeniture, regardless of gender or sex.

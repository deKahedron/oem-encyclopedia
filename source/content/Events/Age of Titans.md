The Age of Titans was a period in the history of [[Oem]] when humanoid species lived in the shadow of titans, an assortment of unrelated, colossal monsters.

Humanoids have not always been the dominant species on the surface. Millennia ago, we did not share the continent with the monsters; they shared it with us.

For us, the smaller races, it was a time when our survival depended on the titan of our home not being too hungry, and on the one next door not killing him and taking his place.

Countless races were wiped out by the battles waged by these creatures; races we do not even remember. Some names survive to this day, with varying degrees of context: the [[Kraken]], the [[Tarrasque]], the [[The Thirst|Thirst]], D'ruuda, the [[Zaratan|Zaratans]], [[Tiamat|Tiamut]], [[Zuggtmoy]], the [[Time Eater]], the [[Nightfall Bird]], the [[Kana'Lathen|Mother Stone]], and [[Fomorian]].

Some humanoids survived by hiding from these tyrants, in distant planes or underground. Others pledged themselves to these godlike creatures, of their own volition or as slaves.

In the Late Age of Titans, only the [[Giant|giants]] and the [[Dragon|dragons]] still vied for power. The myths of the [[Fomorian's Crusade]] suggest that the giants were the first of these to fall. However, the dragons’ victory was short-lived. Their solitary nature led to endless internal struggles over territory, until their numbers were reduced to what we know today.

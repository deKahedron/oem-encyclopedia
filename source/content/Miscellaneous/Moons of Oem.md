The skies of [[Oem]] are home to four moons: yellow [[Mourna]], slate-blue [[Gannare]], pink [[Syril]] and the greenish [[Valthen]]. The first two are similar in size and orbital period (41 and 55 days respectively); Syril is smaller and slower, taking 90 days to complete its cycle; Valthen, with its 132-day cycle, is little more than a faint smudge on most nights.

Because of the amount of variables involved in the moons, no lunar [[Ostorian Calendar|calendars]] have ever gained much traction in Oem.

The presence of these four satellites has several effects on the physical world and on the people of Oem.
The tides are highly irregular. Although the average sea level does not vary drastically on most days, the difference in sea level between the two most extreme syzygies can exceed 10 metres in some regions.
In regards to [[Magic|magic]], the barriers between the planes are affected not by the position of the moons, but by their phases.
### Synchronies
The more full moons there are on a given night, the easier it is to access the [[Feywild]]. In extreme cases, such as a Bright Synchrony (a coincidence of four full moons), it is difficult to venture beyond civilised areas without ending up lost in the wilds of the Feywild, or coming face to face with curious fey creatures.

The Shadowfell is similarly affected by new moons. During a Dark Synchrony, it is said that even bonfires give off less light, darkened by the influence of that sinister plane.

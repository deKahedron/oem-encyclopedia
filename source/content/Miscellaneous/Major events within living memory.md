Given the extreme longevity of some species, many events that took place hundreds of years ago are still within the living memory of some people.

Depending on the age and location of a given character, it can be assumed that they experienced the following historical events in some way:

* Within the last decade
	* [[The Hunger War]]
	* [[Minotaur|Minotaurs]] and [[Gnoll|gnolls]] stopped being unquestionably violent monsters.
	* [[The Six Liches]] of [[Arcaxius]] were struck down and succeeded by the [[Twin Kings]].
	* The [[The Broken Minute|destruction of the minute]].
	* The [[Indentured]] are born.
	* The [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]] are devastated by [[The Draymorites|Draymorite]] cultists.
* Around 20 years ago
	* The first firearms began circulating.
	* [[Fuegil & the Manilands|The Manilands]] begin using the metric system.
	* Invention of the *Chest of Preserving*.
* Around 30 years ago
	* The [[Tungertine|Buried Wyrm]] kills thousands in the [[Underdark]] beneath [[Dûnar]].
* Around 70 years ago
	* The Manilands were formed
* Around 100 years ago
	* First sightings of the [[Chalican|chalicans]] and the [[Nadiria#The Great Humbling|fall of Nadiria]].
* Around 120 years ago
	* The [[Kana'Lathen|Mother Stone]] goes dormant.
	* Invention of the *Alchemy Jug*.
* Around 150 years ago
	* Rise of the [[Umaran Empire]].
	* Invention of the *Bag of Holding* and the *Teleportation Circle*.
* Around 200 years ago
	* The [[Harpers]] become the first international guild of adventurers.
* Around 300 years ago
	* The [[Kraken's Respite]].
	* The [[Crimson Fever]].
* Around 400 years ago
	* The first [[Dragonborn]] come into being.
* Around 500 years ago
	* The Six Liches take over the [[University of Arcaxius]].
	* The *Sending* spell is discovered.
* Around 700 years ago
	* [[Hel-Dhao]] lights the [[Eternal Ember]] and founds [[Newhearth]].

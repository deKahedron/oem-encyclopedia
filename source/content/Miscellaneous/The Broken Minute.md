Every day from 19:51 to 19:52, time appears to skip forwards a minute. In reality, time is still passing, but every creature on [[Oem]] is frozen and unable to perceive its passage.

The rule of thumb is that is something is considered a creature for the purpose of spells it doesn't perceive the minute, nor can it be affected in any way during the minute.
### Origin
The minute was originally bound as a [[Phylactery|phylactery]] by [[Zaledrix Almeyar]] and the [[The Six Liches|liches of Arcaxius]]. In 3433, [[The Nameless Conspirators|several enemies of the liches]] bound the [[Department of Chronurgy]] to the phylactery and then destroyed the tower's enchantments. In doing so, they inadvertently shattered the minute itself.

The Ostorian calendar is the main system of date-keeping in Oem. This calendar doesn't use months, instead dividing the 290-day solar year into 4 seasons of 72 days plus the two equinoxes.

Public holidays are not linked to a specific date in the year, but rather to a week, within which they are celebrated on the day of the week with which they are most closely associated out of the seven.
### Weekdays
* **Godsday** (*Divínedes*), used for religious celebrations.
* **Crownsday** (*Córones*), used for patriotic or governmental celebrations.
* **Bloodsday** (*Sánades*), used for familial celebrations.
* **Widsday** (*Téredes*), used for celebrations relating to harvests or the natural world.
* **Holdsday** (*Pósedes*), used for celebrations of material goods.
* **Goldsday** (*Órodes*), used for commercial festivals.
* **Soulsday** (*Álades*), used for honouring the dead.

Equinoxes (called Lumeq and Tenebreq in the Spring and Autumn respectively) do not count as days of the week, so if the day after a Godsday is an equinox, the day after that will be a Crownsday.

Dates in Oem are written by listing the ordinal number of weekday day within the season, the weekday, the season and the year. For example: 
4th Bloodsday of Summer, 3433 (the start date of the campaign [[The End of Magic]]).
### Current year
The current year, as of the end of [[The Fire (Campaign)|The Fire]], is 3436.
### Original year 1
The current year number is not based on the original "year 1" set by the [[Giant|giants]]. Rather, it is based on the number of years since the [[Banishment]], the event that ended the [[Age of Titans]]. It is unknown when the original year 1 was, but it was likely at least 5 thousand years before the Banishment.

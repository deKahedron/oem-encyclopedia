Felkin are a humanoid that emerged from the [[Shadowfell]] some centuries ago. Almost mechanical in their mannerisms and habits, they manage to coexist with other races by performing dull or repetitive tasks that others find tedious.

### Biology
Felkin have no skin on the top of their skulls, which often sport crests or horns running along their lengths. Perhaps more disturbing are their eyes, wide dark empty sockets with pinpricks of light floating ethereally within. The bones on their cheeks protrude downwards, covering the sides of their mouth, with only a bottom lip to cover all their teeth. The rest of their body is covered in skin that clings tight to their thick bones. Despite their almost undead appearance, they are deceptively strong and nimble.
### Not Quite Empty
The influence of the Shadowfell still lies heavily on the felkin. Compared to other humanoids, they experience an extremely shallow, though no less broad, emotional spectrum.

Felkin experience the emotions they evoke in others more powerfully than their own. This often leads them to think about people around them more than themselves, for better or worse.

This extroverted leaning often causes good-aligned felkin who live among other races to become guards or pursue the arts, and more evil-aligned individuals serve as torturers or interrogators: anything to allow them to provoke strong feelings in others without dealing with the prejudice so often directed their way.
### Origin
Much like how the first [[Elf|elves]] emerged from the [[Feywild]], the first felkin escaped from the Shadowfell during a [[Moons of Oem#Synchronies|Dark Synchrony]]. It is unknown whether the felkin descended from another humanoid species the way that elves did.
### Society
Felkin thrive in the [[Material Plane]] and have formed small communities at the periphery of many cities.

Unless they are trying to appeal to the tastes of another folk, they rarely construct anything more ornamented or impractical than necessary.

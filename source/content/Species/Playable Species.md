This is a list of all the playable species in the [[Oem]] setting. If you want to play anything else, consult the Dungeon Master first.
* [[Aarakocra]]. Avian humanoids resembling eagles or parrots.
* [[Aasimar]]. Humanoids of any species blessed with celestial power.
* [[Bugbear]]. Large, hairy and stealthy goblinoids.
* [[Centaur]]. Horse-human hybrids who live in herds.
* [[Dragonborn]]. An artificial race of draconian humanoids, born from a *Wish*.
* [[Dwarf]]. Hardy, stout and skilled people who live mostly underground.
* [[Elf]]. Extremely long-lived descendants of human Fey refugees.
* [[Felkin]]. Gaunt natives of the Shadowfell with skull faces who empathise greatly but feel little.
* [[Firbolg]]. Large guardians of the wild who hide from more metropolitan folk.
* [[Gallivespian]]. Minute humanoids with venomous spurs on their feet.
* [[Genasi]]. Humanoids of any species blessed with elemental power.
* [[Gith]]. Pilgrims of a distant plane, all but extinct on Oem.
* [[Giff]]. Hippo-faced warriors descended from Maruk experiments.
* [[Gnome]]. Clever, collaborative little tricksters who built the largest empire in living memory.
* [[Goblin]]. One of the most populous and misrepresented peoples on the continent.
* [[Goliath]]. Towering giant-kin who live in the high mountains and tundras.
* [[Halfling]]. Small, courageous folk who can find a home among any civilization.
* [[Hobgoblin]]. A species that live and die by the chains of command of their 5 great legions.
* [[Human]]. Numerous and study folk, known for religiously aligning with other great powers.
* [[Indentured]]. Automatons built to recycle humanoid souls into never-ending servitude.
* [[Kenku]]. Corvid humanoids who can imitate any sound, cursed to have no voice of their own.
* [[Kobold]]. Small draconic creatures who naturally flock around the territories of true dragons.
* [[Lizardfolk]]. Pragmatic reptilian humanoids who live in swamps of elemental Earth.
* [[Luruthi]]. Moth-like acolytes of the sun and stars.
* [[Minotaur]]. A race recently freed from the binding ferocity of their demon overlord.
* [[Orc]]. Burly folk fuelled by passion and rage, riven in two by a godly divorce.
* [[Othraxi]]. Six-legged pangolin kin who hide under sand and foliage alike.
* [[Owlin]]. Silent, winged humanoids, descended from the intelligent owls of the Feywild.
* [[Panserbjørn]]. Massive, armoured polar bears, skilled in metalwork and truthful as death.
* [[Perennial]]. Sentient plants reborn from the frightened soul of a dead druid.
* [[Rakin]]. Humanoid raccoons who prosper in the wake of other, more careless, folk.
* [[Tabaxi]]. Feline humanoids who get along better with other people than among themselves.
* [[Tiefling]]. Humanoids of any species with infernal power in their bloodline.
* [[Triton]]. Aquatic humanoids of the cold southern seas.

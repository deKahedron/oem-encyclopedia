### Biology
True dragons have four legs and two wings. Their skin is covered in colourful scales.

Dragons come in a wide variety of colours, each with other associated characteristics. However, they are all just different phenotypes of a single species.

Dragons may be inherently magical creatures, but they have existed for so long that they have become ingrained in the very nature of the world. Their biology is completely unaffected by dispelling effects and anti-magic fields.
##### Reproduction
Dragons are hermaphroditic and oviparous. They lay eggs in clutches. A dragon egg is about half a meter tall and weighs close to 30 kilograms. The number of eggs in a clutch varies based on the age and size of the mother.

Chromatic dragons are spawned from metallic parents and metallic dragons from chromatic parents. This phenomenon is known as the *Excisive Cycle*.
##### Breath weapons
Dragons are able to produce powerful effects from their breath. The effects they can produce depend on their colour. Chromatic dragons can only produce destructive blasts, but metallic dragons can also create other effects.
##### Hoards
Dragons grow continually throughout their lives. In order to be able to fly, they sympathetically offload a part of their weight into their hoard. A dragon's hoard is a collection of all its possessions: gold, jewels, plundered relics, magic items and even the land they exert their influence over. The greater the value of a dragon's hoard, the larger they are able to grow without losing the ability to fly.

Most dragons eventually die from outgrowing their hoards. First they lose their flight. Then, as they become more sluggish, they lose the ability to effectively protect their hoard from thieves. Once their hoard becomes too small to support them at all, they collapse and die of exhaustion.

The largest known dragon is [[Qilakestis]], who is at least 3000 years old. Because of this, it is unknown whether there is a true limit to how old a dragon can become.
##### Regional effects
A dragon passively exerts a magical influence over the territory it claims. Over time, these lands warp and adapt to the dragon's presence: the weather changes, different species flourish or fail, springs and rivers can dry up or expand.

The migration or death of an adult dragon can have devastating environmental effects, as ecosystems buckle under the sudden changes that befall them.
### Number
The number of dragons has been relatively stable since the end of the [[Age of Titans]]. At current, there are approximately half a dozen ancient dragons, around a dozen adult dragons, some thirty young dragons and around two hundred wyrmlings.
### Origin
Dragons are much older than any humanoid races. It is unknown whether they were naturally evolved or born from some kind of magic.

It is said that the *Excisive Cycle* began with the [[Excision of Tiamut|excision of Tiamut]]. During this event, the one head of the four-headed dragon goddess [[Tiamat|Tiamut]] rebelled against the others, separating from them and becoming [[Bahamut]], the god of metallic dragons. From the stump of the lost head, two new heads grew, and Tiamut's colours became distinct, each remaining head solidifying into one hue.

Some [[Human#Shou|shou]] scholars claim the *Cycle* as evidence that Bahamut will one day re-join Tiamat, bringing about a new age of dragon dominion.
### Chromatic dragons
Chromatic dragons (red, blue, black, green and white) are much more violent and territorial than their metallic kin. They rarely cooperate with any other species except in dynamics of worship and submission.
### Metallic dragons
Metallic dragons (gold, silver, bronze, copper and brass), while much more civil in their interactions with other species, are still incredibly proud. They gladly accept the service and aid lesser creatures and even acknowledge the value of dealing and coexisting with them.

Metallic dragons are able to shape-shift into humanoid form and often develop entire lives and personas for themselves as a way of keeping a low profile and learning from others.

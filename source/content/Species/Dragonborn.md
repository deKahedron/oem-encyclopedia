### Biology
Dragonborn are significantly larger and heavier than [[Human|humans]]. Their bodies are covered in hard scales in one of the 10 colours shared by true dragons. Unlike dragons, their colour has little influence on an individual dragonborn's temperament.

Dragonborn can have horns, crests or other distinctive features of true dragons. However, these don't always align with the colour of their scales. For example, a gold dragonborn might have the characteristic single nose horn of a blue dragon. Dragonborn almost always inherit the scale colour of one of their parents at random.

Dragonborn are oviparous. They lay clutches of 2-5 eggs.
### Culture and society
For the purpose of inheritance, the eldest sibling of a clutch is determined not by the first to hatch, but by the first to produce their breath weapon.

Dragonborn that never produce a breath weapon are known as *unborn*.

Dragonborn are not a numerous species. Most of them live in a few cities in [[Dûnar]].
### Origin
In the year 3073, the dragon [[Tungertine]] was temporarily banished from the [[Material Plane]]. In his absence, the people of the city of [[Cal Payit]], who had previously worshipped the dragon as a god, raided his lair and stole his hoard. For their betrayal, Tungertine *Wished* upon them a mighty curse, transforming them into the first dragonborn, so that their loyalty might never again be called into question.

It is unknown whether Tungertine himself cast the *Wish* spell that created the dragonborn.

### Biology
Giants a single-generation species; they have never been known to reproduce. A dying giant can enter a dormant state, transforming into a part of the world around them: stone giants turn into great stone cliffs, hill giants into mounds of earth, frost giants into glaciers, etc.

A dormant giant can remain in this state for centuries of even millennia. They do not need to eat and are unidentifiable by all but the most trained eye, or by other giants.

It is not known what exactly causes a giant to reawaken and resume its corporeal form. However, the longer a giant has been dormant, the less it seems to remember of the events of the past once it awakens.
### Origin
There are no records of a time before the giants existed. The oldest surviving texts in any language come from the giants of the [[Age of Titans]].
### Ostoria
During the Age of Titans, the giants ruled a vast, interplanar empire called Ostoria. Ostoria stood for at least some thousands of years, covering all of known Oem, the [[City of Brass]], [[Avernus]] and parts of [[Ysgard]].

Ostoria's height did not last long, however. The legend of *[[Fomorian's Crusade]]* tells the tale of how the giants' empire was brought low after losing its hero-king [[Fomorian]]. As the giants were abandoned by their gods, they fell to melancholy and disorder, allowing their ancient enemies, the [[Dragon|dragons]], to tear them down during the [[Thousand Years War]].

Most of Ostoria has been completely lost to time. The grandest monuments of the stone giants, the flying cities of the cloud giants and the greatest weapons of the fire giants have all been eroded into sand, crashed into the ocean or stolen away into private collections across the planes.

Some remnants still linger, deep in the [[Underdark]] or in sealed vaults. Some ruins have been repurposed, the shadows of their original shapes barely discernible through the work of later masons and architects. The cities of [[Stormcross]] and [[Highgale]] are some of the most prominent examples of this.
### The Ordning *(El Estamento)*
Giant society is organised in a linear caste system, called the Ordning. Storm giants rank the highest, followed by cloud giants, fire giants, frost giants, stone giants, hill giants and finally giantkin such as ettins and [[Ogre|ogres]]. All non-giants are considered beneath the Ordning, and undeserving of any respect until they prove themselves otherwise.

Within each race of giants, different qualities are taken into consideration when determining who outranks who in the Ordning. Storm giants value omens that surround them, cloud giants value ostentatious wealth, fire giants skill at craft, frost giants brute strength, stone giants artistry and hill giants value only raw size.

All giants are expected to show reverence to anyone higher up than them in the Ordning.
##### Maat and Maug
Two concepts encompass the morality of giants: *maat* (pronounced *mott*) and *maug* (pronounced *mog*). Maat encompasses all that is right and proper, maug refers to anything awry, out of place or inadequate. Maat and maug are relative terms, they define an action being appropriate to the person or creature performing it. For example, a stone giant destroying a sacred sculpture would be extremely maug. A frost giant performing the same action might be praised as committing maat, if the destruction is considered a sufficiently impressive feat of strength.
### Giants in the current age
In the current age, the few giants who remain alive and active are mostly solitary creatures. There are two main exceptions:
* The city of [[Skyte]], in western [[Alheuffikh]]. An underground metropolis built by stone giants, one part of which is open and welcoming to humanoid races.
* The City of Brass on the [[Elemental Planes|Elemental Plane of Fire]]. This place is still home to many fire giants, though many of them resent that they are no longer the sole rulers of that place.

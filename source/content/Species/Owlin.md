### Biology
Owlin, like their cousins the [[Aarakocra]], have quite weak legs but very strong upper limbs, which serve as both wings and arms. About three-quarters of the way along each of their wings, they have two fingers and an opposable thumb. Whilst their hands are capable of most delicate movements that other humanoids can do, most aarakocra can't perform certain actions, such as wield ranged or two-handed weapons or perform the somatic components of spells, while flying.  
Their downy feathers allow them to fly much more quietly than the Aarakocra, if slower.
### Names
Owlin typically have a name and a surname. Their surname describes a physical trait of their. An owlin inherits their surname based on which of their parents' named traits is more pronounced in them.

Names: Hemlen, Ilifer, Kilimara, Urnea, Ysila, Zinnia
Surnames: Blots, Patches, Rings, Snowy

### Biology
They have pointed ears and are typically smaller and lighter than humans.

Elves do not need to sleep, instead entering a semi-conscious trance-like state to rest their body and mind. While they can sleep if they choose to, they cannot dream.

Despite being a separate species from humans, most elves are closely related enough to be able to reproduce with them. However, [[Half-Elf|human-elf hybrids]] are themselves sterile, except for those who inherit the blessing of the [[Myth King]].
### Origin
During the Age of Titans, humanoids searched for anywhere they could take refuge from the giant monsters that roamed the face of the continent. Many humans wound up in the Feywild.

Over the centuries after the [[Banishment]], some of the descendants of those humans returned to the [[Material Plane]]. Due to the way the Feywild distorts time, some of those who returned were, biologically, many thousands of years removed from their ancestors who had fled.

Elves who are the first of their line to return from the Feywild are called *lostlings*. Lostlings can vary wildly in how long their ancestors have spent in the Feywild and, as such, how biologically elvish they are. Some are close enough to humans to be considered half-elves.
## Sub-species
### High Elf
The high elves are an ethnic group native to the deserts of [[Alheuffikh]]. They typically have olive-brown skin and black hair and strikingly colourful eyes.

High elves speak [[Elvish]] and have an artocratic culture, where art of any kind is considered an act of worship to creation. Most of the high elf population lives in [[Rhyza]].
##### High elf names
First names: Daegon, Helem, Lanouf, Lurin, Olga, Yorah, Zaledrix
Surnames: Al-Hamsa, Almeyar, En-Rachsa, Thumlekh, Zamazeera
*When translated into other languages, some high elves choose to separate the preposition at the start of their surnames with a dash, while others don't.*
### Wood Elf
Wood elves are native to the deep forests of [[Hiamyth]] and [[Dûnar]]. They typically have pale, creamy skin and colourful hair in pastel, floral hues.

Alongside the [[Sylvan]] language, wood elves brought with them the complicated court dynamics of the Feywild. Their society is feudal, mostly residing in or around large arboreal palaces in the woods and dominated by intrigue and ever-changing fashions. Wood elves are known for their fascinating, impractical weaponry and unusual physical performances.
##### Wood elf names
First names: Alvar, Claudia, Ethel, Ethna, Gwendolyn, Marko, Timo, Wendallia
Surnames: Kankenfritz, Kirstein, Messerholdt, Naedalhauff, Summinburg
### Drow
Drow, also known as *dark elves*, are elves native to the [[Underdark]]. They have white hair and dark, iridescent skin in desaturated hues ranging from blue to purple.

Drow don't have a full language of their own, instead typically speaking some variant of [[Undercommon]]. The drow of each different Underdark region are often closely allied with other underground species, whether they be civil or feral. They are known for domesticating incredibly dangerous monsters, from grells to giant spiders.

In [[Dûnar]] and [[Nadiria]], the drow colonies resorted to worshipping the [[Lolth|Spider Queen Lolth]], a goddess of secrets whose ethos helped these populations resist the invading [[Umaran Empire]]. This cultural shift made them very aggressive toward outsiders.
##### Drow names
First names: Akôrdia, Celiem, Desterio, Ornim, Ta'rakiss
Surnames: Glamora, Naïlo, Siruell
### Sea Elf
Sea elves are a distant subspecies of elf that are adapted to live a fully aquatic life in the warm, shallow reefs of the [[Veleayan Sea]].

Sea elf settlements are few and far between. They communicate amongst themselves in [[Aquan]]. 
##### Sea elf names
Names: Cthagas, Cthil, Gthumtreyk, Htenga, Kfaarqo, Ktregar, Pkaachu, Pretgerd, Qhsen, Rhaeychu, Tgrath, Xjari, Xkogjer, Xrathas
### Eladrin
Eladrin are the elves whose lineages have diverged the furthest from humans, taking on more extreme aspects of the Feywild. Some of them change appearance based on the season or their own strong emotions. They have longer ears than other elves, slightly longer lifespans and even read as [[Fey]] to most Divination magic.

In the [[Lfaeria Desert]], nomadic tribes of eladrin, called [[Mirage-walkers|mirage-walkers]], travel freely between the Feywild and the Material Plane.
##### Eladrin names
Nicknames: Brightstring, Redfeather
Names: Abakim, Yanec
Eladrin typically precede their single-word name with a nickname or epithet.

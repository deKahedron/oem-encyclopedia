### Biology
Wyverns are clearly distinguishable from true [[Dragon|dragons]] by their number of limbs. Wyverns only have two frontal wings and two hind legs.

Also unlike true dragons, wyverns are gonochoric.

Wyverns are not sapient, but they are more intelligent than most animals.
### Origin
Wyverns are said to have once been servants or allies of the true dragons, whose loyalty was won over by [[Fomorian]]. This earned them the name "wyvern", which means "traitor" in [[Draconic]].

However, wyverns show no innate opposition to living alongside other dragons, provided they are adequately trained.
### Breeds
All breeds of wyvern are a single species capable of reproducing, despite the extreme physical variety between them.
#### Common wyvern
The common or ruddy wyvern is around 6 metres long with a similar wingspan. They are known for their fierce tempers and extremely venomous tail stinger.
#### Domestic wyvern
Domestic wyverns (also known as colt drakes) are around 4 metres long. They are bred for their docile temperament and speed. Domestic wyverns can be ridden by small humanoids for scouting and delivering messages.

Domestic wyverns that hatch in the same nest as a true dragon's egg acquire certain physical traits associated with that dragon type. Wealthy breeders will pay great fortunes to be able to cross wyverns with different features to hatch truly unique and dazzling scale patterns, horn structures or shapes.
#### Greater wyvern
The greater wyvern is a cross-breed between the common and domestic wyverns. They are larger and much harder to tame and handle than the domestic wyvern, but they are fierce in battle and capable of carrying Medium riders.
#### Shadow wyvern
The shadow wyvern is one of few naturally magical wyverns, bred for hunting in the [[Lfaeria Desert]]. They are the smallest wyvern breed, only around a metre long. They are invisible while in direct sunlight, recognisable only by their shadow, which helps them avoid larger aerial predators.
#### Cloud wyvern
Cloud wyverns (also known as sky whales) were the largest known breed of wyvern, though they are believed to be extinct. The largest skeletons found have been over 24 metres long. They had comparatively small wings relative to their body size, but their hind legs were also shaped like wings. They lived their entire lives airborne and were incapable of walking.

They had extremely large bellies, presumably filled with light gasses that allowed them to float gently, much like [[Dandelion Kow|dandelion kows]], and survive by filter-feeding. [[Giant|Cloud giants]] bred these beasts in colossal flocks to tug their flying cities.

### Biology
All dwarves grow beards from around the age of 5.

While they are known to be a primarily subterranean people, dwarves have several physical adaptions that allow them to thrive both on the surface and below ground.

Their eyes are very sensitive, but they have a third, semi-transparent eyelid that they can lower to reduce their exposure to bright light.

Dwarves have a strong grip reflex, something they evolved to help them cling to safety when treacherous ground crumbles beneath them or causes them to slip in the deep tunnels of the [[Underdark]].

Because the traditional dwarven diet is deficient in calcium, many dwarves, especially older ones, develop tooth decay. Their powerful stomachs are equipped to deal with this though, sporting strong muscles that act as a gizzard. The expression "eating rocks" is an insult used by dwarves to call each other old. Their stomachs also afford them a certain resistance to toxins in their food.
### Origin
While dwarves are a naturally evolved species, the reason for their longevity is still debated. Some believe it to be a blessing from their [[Divine Magic#Gods|gods]] or a form of [[Divine Magic#Heroics|heroics]] derived from their hardy nature.

The myth of [[Fomorian]] states that the [[Giant]] prince created dwarves after being inspired by the diligence and efficiency of the azers he encountered on the [[Elemental Planes|elemental plane of fire]].
### Culture
Dwarf culture is very egalitarian between genders, but strictly classist. A dwarf commoner may rise to gain renown for their craft, but never for their person, unless their clan is risen to nobility.

In dwarven culture, a Kelli is a travelling warrior who journeys in search of monsters to slay or other problems to solve. Kelli are expected to be given food and lodging whenever they require it. A Kelli lives outside of the usual class system, as each of them must forswear their clan to join a knightly order.
##### Beards
Beard grooming is very important to dwarves. Beard length can denote seniority, but fashions change often and the most respectable dwarves will trim and style their beards with great care for each occasion.
### Duergar
Duergar are a race of dwarves that live deep in the Underdark and are very rarely seen on the surface. They lack the third eyelid that allows other dwarves to tolerate sunlight, but have much better senses in the dark.
### Names
Like all words in [[Dwarvish]], dwarf names have two-syllables. However, unlike other words in the language, the penultimate syllable is stressed instead of the last one.

Dwarven nobles have surnames of their own, but other dwarves will use the name of one of their parents as a surname, connected with the word Chetta (meaning "child of").

Names: Brokken, Ewda, Garthum, Jaspa, Jerej, Lohum, Sindri, Taeko
Surnames: Bleska, Lyren, Tîlon, Vostok

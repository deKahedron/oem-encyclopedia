Luruthi are a species of moth-like humanoids. They live mostly in the southern [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]] and [[Alheuffikh]].

### Biology
Luruthi have four limbs and four wings, marking them as distinctly magical in origin, rather than descended from common insects.

Luruthi bodies are covered in an exoskeleton. Their arms and legs are long and thin. They have long, wispy antennae on their head, above large compound eyes. Their torso is covered in a short coat of fuzz, with much more fluffy tufts of hair around their head.
### Life cycle
Luruthi lay their eggs underground, in the soft soil of the forests they inhabit. A newly hatched luruthi is a 6-inch copper-coloured larva. Their four limbs clearly set them apart from common insects, on the rare occasions that they are seen by outsiders. Most commonly though, they remain underground entirely for almost a full century.

When a larva is fully grown (around three feet long), it emerges from the ground and climbs a tall tree to begin metamorphosis. This process takes around 24 hours, at the end of which a shining, brightly coloured luruthi emerges, fully capable of flight and bursting with an energy to match its immense appetite.
![307](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/16ROjrtGtTRh_hajkPyDlpWWC0h7J20vb)
The newly emerged luruthi nymph is an agile flyer full of wonder at the surface world. They retain some memory of their time as larvae, though they seldom enjoy reflecting on such times.

After barely a few years as nymphs, a luruthi grows too large and too slow to support their own weight in flight. These fully grown adults live for several centuries, though they are infertile from very early on in their adulthood. As their coloration fades, sharp patterns become more defined on their wings, which become limp and silky from atrophy.

### Isolation
Luruthi villages are typically built high up in the canopies of trees. Their houses usually have a section with an open roof, better to see the open sky. The celestial bodies are central to their culture, as adult luruthi find mystical guidance in the observation of their movements.

Because of this, it is not uncommon for adventurers and commoners alike to seek out luruthi settlements for advice and to have their fortunes read.

Luruthi are very reticent to reveal the locations of their breeding grounds to outsiders, for fear of their cocoons being poached for the creation of [[Dawnweave]] and [[Duskweave]].
### Religion
The luruthi worship of the astral bodies naturally extends to their pantheon of [[Divine Magic#Gods|gods]]. Their two main deities are the sun goddess [[Tathira]] and [[Yarinele]], the oldest of their moon gods. These two, along with many other minor gods of the moons and stars, offer advice and foresight to the luruthi.

The progeny of Tathira and Yarinele is [[Jajaro]], the storm god. In stark contrast to his parents, Jajaro holds domain over lies, uncertainty and freedom of choice. He is often worshipped by younger luruthi and the rare adventurers who leave their homes. Despite his antagonistic nature, Jajaro remains an important figure within the pantheon; presented just as often as a necessary contrast to the others as he is as an adversary.
### Names
Since luruthi spend so many years of their early life completely disconnected from their biological family, they have no surnames. Their names are long, single words of 5 to 7 syllables which are typically created as an elaborate sylvan portmanteau of their fate, as foretold in the stars. When interacting with members of other races, most Luruthi will use a shortened version of their full name.

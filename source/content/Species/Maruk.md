The maruk were a humanoid species that lived in Alheuffikh until the end of the [[Age of Titans]].
### Physiology
The maruk had a typical humanoid frame with two arms and two legs. Their entire body was covered in a layer of sparse tan fuzz. They had a long neck and a slightly pointed, round head. They had a pair of articulated mandibles to the sides of their mouth, small teeth and flat noses. Sixteen tiny eyes were arranged in clusters of four along ridges on their brow and cheek. Above their eyes, they had two groups of long whiskers and flowy crest of long hair, like a receding mohawk.

The maruk used [[Druidic Magic|druidic magic]] to alter their forms. An adult maruk could be sealed into a clay pod with silk and druidic script and, over the course of several weeks of hibernation, change in size, shape or even grow additional appendages or organs.
### Society
Like most humanoids during the Age of Titans, the maruk lived in fear of the great titans that ruled the world. Eventually, it seems they formed some sort of alliance with a great magical force that allowed them to become the first druids and fight back the [[The Thirst|Thirst]], a caustic, vaporous superorganism.

These new druidic powers allowed them to adapt their physical forms and open portals to the [[Elemental Planes|elemental planes]]. From these portals, the maruk conjured new rivers with which they were able to conquer the [[Lfaeria Desert|Lfaeria desert]].

Five maruk cities have been uncovered:
* The site known as *[[Maruk Emn]]*, which appears to have been a central trade and transport nexus.
* The four holy cities, each located at the end of one of the four artificial rivers: [[Annara]], [[Fara'kess]], [[Liy'yur]] and [[Sai'ta]].
### Religion
The maruk had four main [[Divine Magic#Gods|gods]]:
* [[Souiek]], the Protector God of the Wind
* [[Ta'ux]], the Saviour God of Rivers
* [[Larna]], Impassive God of the Sun
* [[Ifriko]], Deceitful God of Dunes
### Downfall
As the maruk became more dependent on their artificial rivers for irrigation, transport and war, the god Ta'ux became more prominent within the pantheon. Feeling like her god and the traditional maruk way of life had been abandoned, a [[High Priestess of Souiek|high priestess of Souiek]] wished to see Ta'ux's way diminished. This wish was overheard by the [[Ahamkara|ahamkara]] [[Kyore of Three Pains]], who granted it by closing off all of the maruk's elemental water portals.

With their rivers suddenly gone, the maruk were unable to adapt in time. They fell to infighting and starvation, becoming entirely extinct within a year. The desert sands reclaimed the once-lush oases they had created, and their cities were buried.
### Legacy
While the maruk themselves went extinct, many of their bio-spliced creations live on, let loose to become entirely new species. Some of these hybrids include tlincalli, manticores, harpies and the [[Giff]].

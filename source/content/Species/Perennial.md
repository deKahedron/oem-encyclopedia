### Biology
A perennial’s body is made mostly of wood. Their veins run green with sap; their skin is made of bark; leaves sprout from their heads in place of hair.

They are capable of photosynthesis and can live entirely without food, provided they have enough sun and water.

They do, however, have some humanoid-like organs, such as eyes and a mouth, allowing them to interact with the world in much the way a humanoid would.

Depending on the type of plant a perennial is reincarnated as, their lifespan may be as short as 1 year or as long as several centuries. Either way, they begin their lives at the same point of maturity that they died at in their past life but continue to mature at a rate proportional to their new lifespan.
### Origin
Occasionally, when a druid dies near an area of magical vegetation with strong druidic power, their soul returns to the earth in quite a literal sense. As the body decomposes and is absorbed by the nearby plant-life, the spores in the druid’s bloodstream transfer an imprint of their consciousness and natural magic into one such plant. This causes the soul of the druid to be reincarnated into the now awakened plant.

Despite re-growing from the soul of a past creature, a perennial retains no memory from their previous life. However, their personality may be similar to their previous incarnation, retaining notable character traits, quirks and even physical features.

Though the [[Egregore|egregore]] spores carried by the druid are passed on to the perennial, they can remain inactive unless reawakened by contact with other druids. Even then, unless the individual perennial chooses to return to the druidic collective, the spores hold no control over them and typically only manifest by allowing the perennial to understand the [[Druidic (Language)|secret language of the druids]].
### Quirks
Many perennials have been observed to enjoy smoking.
### Names
Since perennials appear sporadically and don't have a unified culture, it is common for them to be given nicknames by other intelligent creatures they meet.

Names: Oakenbrow, Old Sandalwood, Nelly Vin'ard, The Weeping Woman, Wathop.

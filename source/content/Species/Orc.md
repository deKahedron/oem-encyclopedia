### Biology
Orcs are larger than [[Human|humans]], though they have slightly longer bodies and arms relative to their legs. Their skin ranges from green to grey.

Adult orcs have large tusks. Males have tusks that jut upward from their lower jaw, much like those of wild boars. Females have tusks on their upper jaw that protrude forward, like those of elephants.

One of the most unique traits of orcs is their extreme genetic compatibility. Orcs of both sexes are able to successfully reproduce with most other humanoid species, producing fertile [[Half-Orc|half-orc]] children. It is debated whether this ability is natural or originates from a divine blessing.
### Origin
Orcs are a naturally evolved species.
### The Divine Annulment
Orcish culture is split in two due to a historic rift between their two parent deities, [[Gruumsh]] and [[Luthic]]. In the year 2309, the [[Myth King]] offered a peace treaty with the orcs of [[Lykaea]]. The mother god Luthic thought this an excellent opportunity to spread her children across the world and bring other species into her dynasty. The father god Gruumsh refused to allow his subjects to willingly submit themselves to another ruler.

And so the gods themselves split apart, ending the mythological marriage as the two deities continued to watch over those orcs that followed each of their mandates.
### Orcs of Gruumsh
The orcs of Gruumsh are, like their patron god, proud and temperamental. A nomadic culture of raiders who descend from the mountains to take what they can from weaker peoples before retreating back to their caves to repopulate.

The orcs of Gruumsh are nomadic. After their separation from the orcs of Luthic, they spread across the continent to [[Hiamyth]], [[Dûnar]] and [[Liengard]].

In the year 2885, the orcs of Liengard were conquered and expelled from the region by the [[Hobgoblin#Siika|legionless hobgoblins]]. They since migrated south into the northern [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]].
### Orcs of Luthic
The orcs of Luthic are much more peaceful and collaborative than their counterparts. They live mainly in Hiamyth, and welcome most all other peoples into their cities regardless of species. Thse orcs are even known to coexist with [[Kobold|kobolds]], [[Goblin|goblins]], [[Ogre|ogres]] and other commonly-feared races. 

Luthic's people are a matriarchal society; the most prolific mothers are worshipped as priestesses of the Cave-Mother.
##### Sex and queer discrimination
Luthican society places great cultural value on sex, especially reproductive sex. Because of this, their society tends to be quite homophobic and acephobic but trans inclusive. Since the value is placed on reproductive compatibility, a gay orc is more likely to be accepted if they are trans or partnered with a trans lover of the same gender.
### Names
Orcs proudly bear surnames that identify their lineage. Gruumsh orc surnames are clan names, granting some level of equality to all members of the clan. Luthic orcs carry the name of their matriarch as a surname. The matriarchs themselves are often referred to by their first name alone.

Names: Araiga, Brenck, Errach, Naaka, Ricca, Urra, Valarr, Yudith, Yuna
Gruumsh clan names: Ko'varr, Kuhn, Lo´ktar

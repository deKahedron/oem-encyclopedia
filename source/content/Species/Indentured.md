### Shackled anew
Pitched to investors as a chance at immortality without the distastefulness of lichdom, the [[Project Golden Blood|Golden Blood project]] was of special interest to [[Yurtris Fenbryte]] as a way of one-upping her colleague and long-time rival [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]].

But this was not the end of her ambitions. For Fenbryte Industries, the average worker is cheap, overworked and easy to replace in the case of an untimely accident or death. Constructs can work day and night with no need for direct oversight, but, without souls, they cannot cast magic of their own, a necessary skill for producing much of Fenbryte's merchandise.

Furthermore, certain specialists are uniquely talented enough to warrant keeping around.

These unfortunate souls were sealed into ageless machine bodies. Held in their mind-cages is enough of their personality to maintain their usefulness and creativity, all the while a [[Fenbryte's Formality]] scroll compels them to continue their labour, forever deprived of the freedom of sleep.
### Intricate construction
Indentured are no mere constructs. Locked inside each individual’s head is a silver structure that contains a humanoid soul in a manner similar to a *Soul Cage* spell. Connected to the soul is a tightly-wound Personality-Defining Reasoning scroll. This scroll is a collection of questions and answers covering topics of decision-making, morality and character in enough detail as to replicate the thinking patterns of particular humanoid.

Typically, an indentured’s soul corresponds to the person their scroll defines. When it does not, the personality can become eroded and strained over time.
### Echoes of flesh
On a deep level, the soul and mind that were once a humanoid recoil at the feeling of being encased in a steel coffin. To help the subjects avoid the feeling that they should be dead, certain precautions were taken to ensure this new form of life feels familiar to the person within.

Their metal bones and mechanisms are encased in wood; the covering of organic material helps the soul find rest. A mix of alchemical compounds pumped though the body keep the wood fresh and allow it to mend itself over time.

Many involuntary needs and impulses were also included in their construction. The upper torso periodically rises and falls in a motion similar to breathing. Indentured feel the need to eat and drink, although such sustenance is not directly processed by the body.

It is unusual for indentured to have a different arrangement of appendages than their former selves, even though such augments are mechanically possible.
### The rare free few
The creation of the indentured was supposed to be a terrible triumph of techno-feudalism, a milestone to place Fenbryte Industries firmly at the head of a new industrial age.

However, amid the chaos of the release event, which coincided with the downfall of the [[The Six Liches|liches]] of [[Arcaxius]] and the sudden closing of the main Fenbryte Ind. headquarters, two anomalous offshoots were born.

[[Taeko Vostok]], a disgruntled former collaborator of the Golden Blood project, conspired with the independent wizard [[Maeve]] and the sentient library [[Caeran]] to release the first batch of prototypes from their servitude.

After their initial endeavour, Taeko and the newly merged Maeve/Caeran disagreed on the best method for bestowing freedom to the indentured.

Taeko believed in retaining as much of the original person’s essence as possible. She devised a method of using an electrical current to burn out the Formality contract without destroying the personality scroll beside it. While effective, the process is dangerous, and often leaves the subject with traumatic responses and the haunting feeling of being dead.

Maeve/Caeran iterated upon the very process that Maeve once used to preserve her own life in an artificial body. By binding a second soul into the construct, the two personalities are able to take turns shouldering the psychological load. By virtue of being a new lifeform, the resulting dividual is also exempt from the terms of the *Formality* contract.
### Patented
Most indentured have the same body model, mass-produced by Fenbryte Industries to serve as an eternal worker. Individual units may have slight differences to reflect their original species, but they are generic and undetailed.

### Custom order
A custom-modelled frame with hand-sculpted details, commissioned to allow for a specific person to exist in an immortal body. Even these models include a *Formality* contract, though the terms are a lot less restricted; limited mostly to preventing hostility towards Fenbryte Industries.

### Overvolted
A patented indentured that has been freed from their *Formality* scroll by dangerously overloading their mind-box with lightning. This causes them to have strange flashbacks to their previous life. Their personalities have a tendency to be volatile and unpredictable.
### Pendular
An indentured that has been freed from servitude and saved from madness by the addition of a second soul into their body. Pendular constructs have a soul cage, which contains the original soul when it is not in use, as well as a [[Soul Coin|soul coin]], from which the second soul is drawn. A clockwork mechanism controls the alternation between the two souls on a regular interval.

Pendular indentured often modify their shells and adorn themselves in styles that suit each of their souls.

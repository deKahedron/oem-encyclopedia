**Aarakocra** are a 4-limbed avian species native to the western mountain ranges of the [[Oem]]. Since the [[Great Humbling]], their numbers have dwindled significantly, and they now mostly reside in the region known as [[The Fumes]].

### Biology
Aarakocra, like their cousins the [[Owlin]], have quite weak legs but very strong upper limbs, which serve as both wings and arms. About three-quarters of the way along each of their wings, they have two fingers and an opposable thumb. Whilst their hands are capable of most delicate movements that other humanoids can do, most aarakocra can't perform certain actions, such as wield ranged or two-handed weapons or perform the somatic components of spells, while flying.

Egregore is a psychic fungus that grows in the corpses of sapient creatures. 
### Effects
Inhaling egregore spores causes them to briefly live within a sentient creature, though they die after about an hour.

A creature that inhales egregore has disadvantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws for 1 hour, and has telepathy to a range of 30 feet for the same duration. This telepathy can only be used on other creatures that are under the effects of egregore.
### Connection to druids
Druids are connected by a network of egregore, but the egregore itself is not the mind influencing the actions of druids. It merely acts as a conduit that connects the druids to each other and the central druidic consciousness.

Magic refers to the ability of some creatures to produce supernatural effects in the world in defiance of the laws of [[Mundane Sciences|mundane science]].
### Types of magic
Magic can be classified by its source, by its nature or by its effect.
#### Types of magic by source
* [[Arcane Magic]]
* [[Divine Magic]]
* [[Druidic Magic]]
* [[Blood Magic]]
* [[Psionics]]
#### Types of magic by nature
* Spells: Discrete, recognisable instances of a magically effect that can be reliably reproduced.
* Suffusion: Magic that is renewably released by an [[Magic Item|item]] or location that was imbued by a separate magical effect.
* Magical Biology: Magic that is produced by the organs of a living or once-living creature.
#### Types of magic by effect
Also known as the classic schools of magic, these categories sort magic by how it affects the world around it.
* Abjuration
* Conjuration
* Divination
* Enchantment
* Evocation
* Illusion
* Necromancy
* Transmutation

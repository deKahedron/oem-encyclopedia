Arcane magic is a type of [[Magic|magic]] that is governed by the understanding of a series of rules and individual adherence to these rules.
### Wizards
The most representative users of arcane magic are wizards, who study the mathematical-ontological [[The Laws of Magic|laws of magic]] and codify their intentions into an [[Arcane Language|formal scripted language]].
### Bards
Bards wield magic through a more intense medium than wizards, using artistic expression to more powerfully convey their intent and emotions. Their understanding of the mechanics of the arcane is typically more surface-level, but they are more adept at feeling the result of a spell. As a result, they are able to use their arcane prowess to case [[Divine Magic|divine]] or even [[Druidic Magic|druidic]] magic.

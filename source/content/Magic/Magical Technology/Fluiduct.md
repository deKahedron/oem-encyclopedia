The fluiducts were a work of [[Nadiria|nadirian]] civil engineering, a series of gigantic, floating rings of stone that conducted water through them. From a distance, they appeared like rivers trussed up and held in a pillory.

Occasionally, a great tree or landmark building would be used to support one of these rings.

The fluiducts were used mainly to control the flow of rivers in defiance of natural terrain formations, depriving access to drinking water outside of the nadirian cities.

The fluiducts were also used to transport goods and people between cities.
### Environmental effects
The concentration of water into these out-of-reach ducts caused irreparable damage to the nadirian environment. Some water would spill from the fluiducts, allowing vegetation to thrive in their wake. Everywhere else, the land dried up into a near-desert clime, causing the extinction of countless species.

Since the destruction of the fluiducts, great amounts of water were dropped onto the dry land. Much of it, still tainted by the magic of the [[Nadiria#The Great Humbling|Great Humbling]] evaporated, forming erratic magical clouds that still curse the region to this day.

The rest was left to return to the low lands, creating shallow marshlands and small forests.

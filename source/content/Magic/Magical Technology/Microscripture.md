Microscripture is a technique for creating magic items by writing extremely small [[Arcane Magic#Runes|runes]].

During the [[Age of Titans]], the humanoid races that fell under the domain of the [[Giant|giants]] discovered that their smaller stature allowed them to scribe much smaller and more precise runes. This principle carries through to the modern day: the sharp eyes and dextrous hands of [[Gnome|gnomes]] are famous for producing the most delicate runecraft.

To further increase the precision of the labour, the *Enlarge/Reduce* spell can be cast on the object being worked on, making it larger while the scribes work. The required spellcasting makes this process, called microscripture, more costly, and requires the scribes to be fast, as well as careful, to be more efficient with the spell usage.

The efficiency of the *Enlarge/Reduce* spell can be increased by first casting or carving as many parts as possible in one piece, joined by thin wire, so that they count as a single object. Then, during the brief duration of the spell, many workers gather around the piece to each carve the runes of a different part.

The greatest rune carver artificers will use their spell-storing item to maximize the amount of time they can keep their work enlarged.

Black ivory is a [[Magical Materials|magical material]] made from [[Dragon|dragon]] bone. It is hard but less brittle than bone and is highly receptive to elemental [[Magic|magic]].

It is often used to create arrows, polearms, wands and staves. Certain methods for preparing the material make it flexible enough to use in the construction of composite bows. Weapons and foci made with black ivory take well to elemental enchantments.

To make black ivory, the dragon bone needs to be treated in very intense processes which vary based on the type of dragon the bone came from. These processes can take up to a year, and certain parts of the procedure need to be performed during certain seasons.

Enactine is a [[Magical Materials|magical metal]]. It is an incredibly rare, psionically reactive alloy, invented by the [[Gith|gith]], that appears to break [[Pewell’s law]].

When an item of enactine is created, it becomes psionically imprinted with its shape. If the item is then destroyed, a psionic creature need simply touch a piece of it and remember the imprinted shape to restore the broken item.

[[Loçta Kwalish]], discovered the enchantment is placed not on the physical item, but on the idea itself. So long as the idea is preserved, the magic suffers no degradation.

###### *Desconcierba*
Bewildermint is a turquoise herb with long leaves that end in a small hook. Its taste is at once sweet and spicy, with a strong but brief burn like mustard or wasabi. It grows deep in temperate forests, in places where the barrier to the [[Feywild]] is thin.

When this herb is mixed into certain potions, it produces more potent but shorter-lasting effects.

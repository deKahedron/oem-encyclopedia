Sallihhka, (also known as *Pulp Bronze* or *Bronzewood*) is a [[Magical Materials|magical construction process]] used by the [[Thri-Kreen]] to make weapons and tools.

The thri-kreen are naturally able to produce a thin, paper-like substance by chewing wood pulp, much like wasps. By laminating dozens of layers of this paper and holistically imprinting the structure with its desired shape, the end result is an incredibly hard material.

Despite having evolved entirely separate from any [[Gith|gith]] influence, the similarities in the forging processes make it behave somewhat like [[Enactine|enactine]]. Wielding a sallihhka item is a unique experience: because the idea of the item is psychically imbued within itself, the item "knows" what it is supposed to be and how it is supposed to be used. Using it for anything else can cause a nauseating sensation.

Sallihhka items are famously easy to enchant, and have been known to spontaneously acquire [[Magic|magical]] properties in response to very intense thoughts and feelings of their wielders.

While they are slightly waterproof, sallihhka tools can soften and break if fully submerged in water. Because the intended shape is imbued within the material, sallihhka cannot be recycled into anything else.

A creature with a Wisdom score of less than 13 has disadvantage on all attacks made with a sallihhka weapon.

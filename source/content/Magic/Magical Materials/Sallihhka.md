Sallihhka, (also known as *Pulp Bronze* or *Bronzewood*) is a [[Magical Materials|magical construction process]] used by the [[Thri-Kreen]] to make weapons and tools.

The thri-kreen are naturally able to produce a thin, paper-like substance by chewing wood pulp, much like wasps. By laminating dozens of layers of this paper and holistically imprinting the structure with its desired shape, the end result is an incredibly hard material.

Despite having evolved entirely separate from any [[Gith|gith]] influence, the material behaves somewhat like [[Enactine|enactine]].

Sallihhka items are famously easy to enchant, and sometimes spontaneously acquire [[Magic|magical]] properties in response to very intense thoughts and feelings of their wielders. While it is slightly waterproof, sallihhka tools can soften and break if fully submerged in water.

Wielding an sallihhka item is a unique experience: the idea of the item is psychically imbued within itself; the material cannot be recycled into anything other than what it was first intended to be, and using it for anything else can cause a nauseating sensation. 

A creature with a Wisdom score of less than 13 has disadvantage on all attacks made with a sallihhka weapon.

Divine magic is a type of [[Magic|magic]] that is sourced from an adherence to strong cultural values.
### Gods
A god is a personification of a set of cultural values and practices. Most gods exist within a pantheon, which divides up the total magical power provided by the pantheon's followers among those who most strongly represent those values.
### Oaths
A paladin who swears a divine oath doesn't necessarily gain their magic from a specific god or pantheon, but rather from a more universal form of divine magic, sourced from the global belief in strong moral convictions.
### Heroics
Heroics is a little-understood form of divine magic that passively affects certain creatures at all times. It is the magical effect responsible for the increased durability and physical prowess of creatures that are feared, respected or well-known. Because of this phenomenon, powerful heroes, villains and nobles are able to survive being hacked at with swords to a greater extent than ordinary people.

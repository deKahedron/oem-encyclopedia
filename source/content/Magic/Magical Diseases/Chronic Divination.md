Chronic divination is a [[Magical Disease|magical disease]] that causes unpredictable hallucinations of true images.

Excessive use of divination magic can cause the brain to develop a kind of dependence on it, leading to involuntary relapses of its effects. In most cases, these magical effects occur involuntarily, without draining the patient’s magical energy.

The most common form of this condition is Regular Chronic Divination. In these cases, episodes occur at predictable times, either at fixed hours or in relation to celestial movements.

Volatile Chronic Divination is a rarer, but far more unpredictable variant of the disease, both in terms of when episodes occur and the symptoms they present. Furthermore, the symptoms seem to become more common as the condition progresses. These symptoms can vary widely, such as seeing windows into other dimensions, perceiving the world with a slight temporal shift, or feeling as though your senses have been transported to another point in space.

Enchantment Self-replication is a [[Magical Disease|magical disease]] that causes the patient to continually reapply an enchantment spell to themselves.

Due to the ethical and legal restrictions on experimental enchantment magic, it has not been uncommon for researchers to perform trials on themselves. There have been many unfortunate accidents caused by this method of research. This is one such accident that has occurred often enough to be itself studied. A subject becomes enraptured in their own spell, which in addition to any other adverse effects, causes the subject to cast the spell on themselves again before it runs out.

Depending on the duration and side effects, this can cause as little as a daily drain on the wizard's stamina to preventing them from sleeping, constantly reapplying the spell, to performing a single inane action dictated by the enchantment over and over again.

Patients recovering from this disease are recommended to engage in mundane pastimes for 5 to 10 days, to allow the mind to regain its composure.

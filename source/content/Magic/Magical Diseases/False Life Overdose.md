False Life Overdose is a [[Magical Disease|magical disease]] that causes the patient to stop feeling pain, hunger, or thirst.

A common ailment among warlocks who take borrowed power for granted, a dependence on *False Life* can cause the body to become desensitised to its own shortcomings. Patients report not feeling hungry despite wasting away from not eating; suddenly choking for breath which they didn't realise they were holding, or even dying from common fever which their body never reacted to.

Licking salt after casting has been recommended as a way to remind the body of thirst, and subsequently its other needs.

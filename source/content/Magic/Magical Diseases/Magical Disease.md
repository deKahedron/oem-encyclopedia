Throughout history, hundreds of mages have pushed the boundaries of known [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] science. All too many of the finest among them perished at a young age, due to accidents and a lack of regard for caution.

Of course, many of them were the first to experience such tragedies.
Personally, it's the repeated incidents that I find far more tragic, as they demonstrate our inability as a community to learn from the mistakes of others.
Sometimes it seems to be a flaw in our nature to ignore the achievements of our predecessors in order as we reinvent the wheel just to prove ourselves their equals.

In the hope that we may one day overcome this intellectual epidemic, I have devoted my career to the study of magically induced ailments, as well as their proper treatments and potential cures. All this with the aim of ensuring that the mages of the future may lead long lives in good health, both mental and physical.

May we all learn to learn better.

-Introduction to the book [[The Mage's Handbook to Not Getting Yourself Killed in Horrible Ways]] by the [[Blood Magic#Thaumatics|thaumaticist]] [[Kerron Mortem]].

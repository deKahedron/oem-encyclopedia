###### 6th-level Enchantment (Onomancy)
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Class:** Wizard

You speak a word of power that incites crippling self-doubt in the mind of one creature you can see within range. If the target has 50 hit points or fewer, it is wracked by floundering incompetence. Otherwise, the spell has no effect on it. A target is also unaffected if it is immune to being charmed. While affected by this spell, the target automatically fails any attack roll or ability check that it doesn't make with advantage. The creature can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a successful save, the effect ends.

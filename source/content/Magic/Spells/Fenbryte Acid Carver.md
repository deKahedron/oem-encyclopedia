###### 5th-level Conjuration
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of acid)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
**Class:** Artificer

You conjure a floating stream of acid that lasts for the duration. The stream starts as a 5-foot sphere. As a bonus action, you can move the source of the stream up to 30 feet. The shape of the stream changes to a 5-foot wide line connecting the previous position of the source to the new position. It retains this shape until the start of your next turn, at which point it becomes a 5-foot sphere centered on the point you moved it to. A creature that passes through the stream or starts its turn there must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 6d6 points of acid damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. The stream instantly dissolves objects of organic material and stone, but not metal, unless the object is being worn or carried by a creature.

###### 6th-level Divination
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a small model of a perfect polyhedron, worth at least 50 gp)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Class:** Wizard

You invoke the axioms of the Weave to scorn beings that do not abide by the laws of the universe. One aberration or a creature native to a plane of existence other than the one you are on must make an Intelligence saving throw or take 6d6+26 psychic damage and be stunned for one round. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't stunned.

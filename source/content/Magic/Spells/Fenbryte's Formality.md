###### 5th-level Enchantment
**Casting Time:** 1 hour
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S, M (special watermarked parchment and rare inks, worth 150 gp, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Until dispelled or fulfilled
**Class:** Artificer

You inscribe a piece of parchment with a contract detailing a specific service or course of action that a creature must carry out or refrain from. The contract is long-worded and difficult to read. You can choose to obfuscate any of the details of the contract in smallprint. A creature that reads the contract must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature is unaware of any of the details hidden in the smallprint. A creature that is not Charmed that signs the contract becomes Charmed by you for the duration of the spell. After signing, the creature becomes aware of the full details of the contract, if it was not previously. While the creature is charmed by you, it takes 5d10 psychic damage each time it breaches the instructions in the contract, but no more than once each day. This damage increases by 1d10 each time the creature receives it. You can end the spell early by using an action to touch the contract and end it. A remove curse, greater restoration, or dispel magic spell cast on the contract itself also ends it.

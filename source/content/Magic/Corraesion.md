Corraesion is the process through which a non-demon creature becomes a [[Demon|demon]]. A corraeded creature is able to gain power by playing to the rules of the [[Abyss]] and is even able to claim a layer of the Abyss as their own.

Many demon lords, such as [[Orcus]], [[Zuggtmoy]], [[Ta'ux]] and [[Lolth]] were once other creature types before corraeding.

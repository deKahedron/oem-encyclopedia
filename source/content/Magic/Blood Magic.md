Blood magic is a type of [[Magic|magic]] that is sourced from the lifeforce or biological organs of living things. Blood magic can be used to detect living creatures, enhance a user's physical strength or even steal magical traits from another creature.

Despite the name, blood magic does not strictly require blood to function. Any creature that is biological can be syphoned to fuel blood magic.

Blood magic is understudied compared to other forms of magic, due to the strong social taboo surrounding it in most cultures. Most currently known applications of blood magic are much more wild and chaotic than [[Arcane Magic|arcane]], [[Divine Magic|divine]] or even [[Druidic Magic|druidic]] magic, where the blood is used purely as fuel.
### Crafting magic items
The most common form of blood magic is the creation of [[Magic Item|magic items]] from the harvested bodies of magical creatures. The sacrifice of the body part or the creature's life empowers a magical trace that evokes the nature of the living monster.
### Lycanthropy
Lycanthropy is a curse that causes one creature to transform into the shape of another under certain circumstances. While the victim is transformed, some or all of their personality is overwritten by the curse, often causing them to act like a wild monster.

Most lycanthropes are transformed by conditions completely beyond their control, such as the phases of the [[Moons of Oem|moons]]. However, through intense physical and mental fortitude, some are able to wrest back control over not only their transformations, but their behaviour while transformed.
### Thaumatics
Thaumatics is a very light form of blood magic that studies the relation between physical health and magical prowess. Some wizards practice thaumatic exercises to empower their spells through physical exertion.

Black Harbour is the capital city of [[Fuegil & the Manilands]]. It is built around the Bay of Ùlgher.

While the [[Glamoran Reformists|reformists]] took the capital within weeks of the start of the [[The Glamoran Civil War|revolution]], pockets of [[Sacrum Sophia|orthodox loyalists]] are still present. This bloody turf war has flipped districts back and forth between sides so many times that their inhabitants can scarcely remember who runs them.
### The Old City
Black Harbour was first populated by [[Fuegil|Gillish]] humans, serving as port and a coal mine, but otherwise not much of an urban nucleus. When the [[Umaran Empire]] conquered the Manilands, the [[Nadiria|Nadirians]] placed a high demand on Black Harbour’s coal supplies to power their industrial smelting and glass manufacturing. The mines beneath the city became an immense network of tunnels connecting every major district.
### Holy District
This district contains most of the city's largest monuments. The Three Spires (the Abstractasia, the Mereola and the Nihilihin) are visible from every part of this district; every major street framing one or more of them.

This district is currently under the control of reformist fanatics who worship the arcane.

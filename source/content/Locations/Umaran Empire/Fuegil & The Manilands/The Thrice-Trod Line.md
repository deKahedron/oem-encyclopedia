###### *La Trimarchita*

The Thrice-Trod Line is a region in the south-eastern [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]].

Marched flat and picked clean of life and riches by the advances of countless armies, this land is nevertheless a safe one.

The meagre country provides little of value for aggressive actors or wild monsters, so the few hardy citizens of the region need only wage battle against their own farms, where every sprouting plant is a small miracle.

The population are mostly [[Halfling|halflings]] and [[Kenku|kenku]].
### [[Providence]]
In the wake of the [[The Glamoran Civil War|Glamoran Civil War]], a new city has sprouted around a miraculous clear stream in the middle of the Line.

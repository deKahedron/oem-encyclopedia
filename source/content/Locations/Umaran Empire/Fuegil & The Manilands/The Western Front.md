The Western Front is a region in the extreme south-west of the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]].

Of the many fronts on which the Manilands has been slowly fighting back the chalicans, the campaign to retake the southern shores has been the most successful. Thanks to the logistical support from [[Black Harbour]] and a lower resistance from the enemy, the campaign advanced so much further West than the other fronts that it simply became known as the Western Front.

When the [[The Glamoran Civil War|revolution]] became violent, both sides sought the support of the western commanders. As each of the seasoned leaders joined the civil war, taking their best soldiers with them, the garrison decayed into a shell of its former self.

As the [[Chalican|chalicans]] retook their [[Chalican Hive|hive]] in the Gahaerins, the imperial bastion in the west of that mountain range was cut off from its supply route. The few remaining soldiers have retreated to the fort at the base of [[Kana'Lathen|Kana’Lathen, the Mother Stone.]]

The native ogres have become less agreeable since their rations from the capital stopped arriving. Perytons hunt anyone who walks too close to a ledge; basilisks stalk the tunnels and manticores and troglodytes search for easy pickings in the narrow mountain passes.

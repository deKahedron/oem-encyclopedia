Oirun's Blood is a forest in the south-western [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]]. The veil to the [[Feywild]] was once very thin in the region, which is now teeming with fey creatures.

Around the course of the river Sarestol grows a forest of tall sequoias and a dense underbrush of white flowers. In between the flower patches are pits of treacherous red quicksand.

Several small villages of [[Luruthi|luruthi]] live amongst the treetops, which they share with a population of [[Owlin|owlin]]. Since both races are mostly nocturnal and good at hiding, the forest appears completely wild during the day.

This also results in the forest being safer to traverse at night; many monsters hide among the dappled shadows of the trees to strike at unsuspecting travellers. The deadliest of these are displacer beasts, but the flowers hide meenlocks, redcaps and flowering mounds, while stirges, dryads and ankylosauri fill a more passive role within the ecosystem.

### The Myth of Oirun
The forest is said to be named after the ancient trickster Oirun, who sought to marry the daughter of the ancient king of Grekghen. In an attempt to convince the king that he was a rich conqueror, he tried to cut the hair of a korred with the king's own golden [[Scissor sword|scissor sword]]. The korred took the blade and shoved it down Oirun's throat, supposedly staining the soil of the forest red with his blood. The king's daughter, Cassaya, covered Oirun's body with a torn handkerchief out of disrespect. From these shreds, the myth tells, grew the mounds of white flowers that cover the forest's underbrush.

### Zinnia's Village
[[Zinnia]] was raised in a small treetop village in Oirun's Blood. The townsfolk had a strong religious community that worshipped [[Bargas]].

### Giant Menhir
On the eastern side of the forest stands a menhir with the following message carved in [[Giant (Language)|Giant]]: 
"Lo, Fomorian's promise. May the offspring of this forest find the hearts of the dragon queen."
A korred (rumoured to be the same korred for whom the region is named) has taken up residence within the stone.

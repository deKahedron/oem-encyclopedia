The Pale Blot is a dense swamp in the middle of the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]], west of [[Nadiria]]. The region owes its colour to the abundance of kaolin clay in its soil and water.

Since time immemorial, this place has been suffused with the magic of [[Elemental|elemental]] earth. The smell of clay overpowers the expected odours of a bog, curious chwingas watch outsiders and [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] geomancers weave wards to keep their villages above water.

Adventurers who step within the borders of the Blot should be prepared to face a wide variety of creatures, such as [[Bullywug|bullywugs]], catoblepas, froghemoths, galeb duhrs, geonids, hydras, khargras, mud hulks, shambling mounds and [[Troll|trolls]].

### Locksilver Manor
During the heyday of the [[Umaran Empire]], the Locksilver family tried to build an enormous manorhouse on the outskirts of the swamp, quarried from a nearby deposit of [[Zaratan|Zaratan's]] scutes. However, the elemental magic reclaimed the living stone, and the many-storied building sank. Now, only the top floors are even visible from the outside, and the buried rooms of the mansion are home to countless elemental monsters, benign and otherwise.

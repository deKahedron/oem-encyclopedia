A great raft of soaked, rotting lumber that clogs the many rivers of [[Fuegil]], creating the swamplands to the north. The land was once the site of a colossal battle between the Empire of [[Giant#Ostoria|Ostoria]] and the Dragon Lords, ending in a resounding defeat for the giants.

Hundreds of hill giants lie dormant or dead beneath the piles of wooden refuse and trolls haunt the mists.

The entire region is steeped in decay and stagnation. The wet smell of rotting wood hangs over everything, as slow waters are gradually contaminated in pools and then filtered in streams. Small swamp fires are common in the region, their blurred glows providing anonymity to the will-o-wisps that haunt the misty air.

Locals believe the name comes from how thoroughly the dam has transformed the surrounding biomes. In truth, the name originally came from the number of dead giants there.

The thick swampy air provides light obscurement to anything further than 15 feet and heavy obscurement to anything beyond 30 feet.

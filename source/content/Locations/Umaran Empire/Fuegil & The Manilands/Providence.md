###### *Nuevo Milagro*

Providence is a city in the [[The Thrice-Trod Line|Thrice-Trod Line]]. 

It was built in the ruins of the [[Nadiria|nadirian]] outpost city of Amphorica, after a copper [[Dragon|dragon]] known as the [[Young Miracle]] began to lair in the caves beneath the ruin.

The outer wall of the city is decorated with shards of white pottery that once covered the water tower at the centre of Amphorica.

The structure of the old water tower has also been repurposed; a giant clock has been installed in its place, visible from every corner of the city. This clock seems to count appears unaffected by the [[The Broken Minute|Broken Minute]].
### Population
The city is ruled by the reformist Marchioness Catrina Edelweiss, who instated a policy of taking in all survivors, providing them and their families with food in exchange for extreme labour building up her city.

Most of the city's population are [[Halfling|halflings]] and [[Kenku|kenku]], like the rest of the surrounding region, but also [[Kobold|kobolds]], who appear in droves wherever there are dragons.
### Sacrum attack
In the year 3436, the city was stormed by an army of [[Sacrum Sophia|orthodox sohpists]], [[Humble Order]] and [[The Honed Legion|Honed Legion]] led by Cognizant Siriotte. The all-seeing eyes of [[Oghma]] were channelled by the attackers. However, [[Lolth]], [[Bargas]], [[Alti]] and [[Milil]] manifested miracles of their own in defence of the city.

In the end, the attackers were successful in destroying much of the city, including the sacrilegious clocktower. The Young Miracle fled its lair, wounded. However, due to the efforts of [[The Ember Pack]] and the divine intervention of the [[Sophism#God Gods|Aspects of Oghma]].

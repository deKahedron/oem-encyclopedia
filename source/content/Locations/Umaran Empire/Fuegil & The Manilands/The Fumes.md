The Fumes is a region in the southern Manilands, upriver from [[The Culvert|the Culvert]]. 

Downriver from one of the largest [[Chalican|chalican]] [[Chalican Hive|hives]], a seemingly endless flow of fluorine residue mixes with the waters of the river Barklis, combusting in a yellow flame that produces oxygen and heat. For decades, the region was considered lost and uninhabitable; a permanent reminder of the impact of the chalican invasion.

However, in their rise to power, the [[Glamoran Reformists|glamorans]] hired the aid of [[Fenbryte Industries]] to reclaim the area. Fenbryte built a force of clay golems around the entrances to the chalican hive, absorbing the acidic fluoride from the air, rendering it breathable again. Since then, the [[Aarakocra|aarakocra]] settlements in the region have become bastions of reformist support.

However, golems are expensive, so outside of inhabited areas the air is still rich enough in hydrogen fluoride to support a massive population of oozes.

The extra oxygen produced by the fluorine reaction allows local insects to grow to huge sizes. [[Gallivespians]] use the area for breeding their riding [[Wyvernfly|dragonflies]].

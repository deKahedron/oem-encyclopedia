###### *La Traína*

The Trawl is a region in the southern [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]]. It is a long, extremely flat beach, littered with jagged rocks. The beach is just high enough that, at highest tide, the sea line advances a full half a mile in some places. When the tide retreats, the rocks and tide pools catch junk, treasures and fish from the sea.

Supporters of the orthodox cause can find refuge in the underwater protectorates of [[Vaaveliv]], Aahliscar and Pashlunder. The [[Triton|tritons]] that live here aren’t so much loyal to the [[Sacrum Sophia|Sacrum]] as they are against the [[Glamoran Reformists|reformists]]; they blame the civil war for distracting the efforts against the [[Chalican|chalicans]], whose return heralds the poisoning of their seas.

The tritons as a whole are not directly vassals to the [[Umaran Empire|Empire]], but these three outposts were granted to the [[Ornim Summerion|Grand Marquis]] command as an alliance treaty against the chalicans.

In the wake of the tritons’ waning, the local sahuagin population has grown out of control.

### Vaaveliv
Vaaveliv is one of the three triton towns near the coast of the Trawl. Unlike the other two, which are entirely beneath the sea, Vaaveliv is partly on the surface, build around a large blue hole. The tritons here coexist with a native [[Human|human]] population.

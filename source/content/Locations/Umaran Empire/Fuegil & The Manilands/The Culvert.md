###### *El Desagüe*

The Culvert is a valley in the southern [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]], on the edge of [[The Scab|the Scab]].

Over the century since the [[Great Humbling]], the increased acidity of the river Barklis caused it to carve out a great canyon where once there was only a plain. As [[Chalican|chalican]] activity near the river’s source waxed and then waned, the river carved out a wider and then narrower berth, resulting in a valley with a near-circular cross-section.

The top of the valley is still very narrow, so at any given time of day only a sliver of the interior is ever lit.

The inside of the tunnel has spawned an odd combination of aquatic, superficial and subterranean plant-life, all of it hardy enough to survive the low light and high fluoride content in the water. The [[Wyvernfly|luminous dragonflies]] that the [[Gallivespian|gallivespians]] imported to [[The Fumes|the Fumes]] have made their way downriver and are now thriving while controlling the rampant mosquito population.

With their main water source poisoned and inaccessible, most of the native [[Centaur|centaur]] herds had to migrate to other lands, mostly in [[Dûnar]] or the northern Manilands.

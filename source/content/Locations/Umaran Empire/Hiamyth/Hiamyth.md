Hiamyth is the easternmost province in the [[Umaran Empire]]. It spans the land between the [[Shadowy Mountains]] and the [[Guong Range]]. The region existed as a single kingdom long before it was annexed and is filled with the crumbling remnants of empires old and new.

### History
Hiamyth was formed in the wake of the conquests of the [[Myth King]], a legendary figure who united modern Hiamyth with a combination of military conquest, diplomacy and [[Divine Magic]]. Before the Myth King, these lands were known as the [[Second Ostorian Empire]], [[Siara]], the [[Court of Thread]], the [[Court of Flame]] and the [[Court of Flowers]].

Hiamyth is built upon the ruins of both the original [[Giant#Ostoria|Ostoria]] and dozens of ancestral [[Dragon|dragon]] hoards. Throughout Mythian history, there has been tension between the cultural descendants of the [[Giant|giants]] and dragons. The [[Mythian|Mythian language]] is a descendant of both [[Giant (Language)|Giant]] and [[Draconic]].

Following the [[War of the Cliffs]], Hiamyth was in a weakened state. As the Umaran Empire continued to expand, [[Dmitrij Fijesko|King Dmitrij VII]] attempted to keep the [[Nadiria|Nadirians]] out, but was soundly defeated in three disastrous battles.
In 3292, when Nadirian flying machines besieged his palace, King Dmitrij was killed attempting to flee. In his place, the Nadirian sympathiser [[Faralda Tinanbaria]] was installed as duchess.

After the [[Great Humbling|fall of Nadiria]] in 3337, the the empire's capital was relocated to the Mythian city of [[Stormcross]], as far from the [[Chalican|chalican]] invaders as possible. In the years since, the Mythian nobility have claimed large swathes of power away from the imperial crown, even elevating the title of the ruler from Duke to Archduke. Within his own lands, the current [[Galigos Tinanbaria|Archduke]] is more powerful than the [[Weylin Damasil|Emperor]] in most respects.
### Demographics
[[Human#Damaran|Damaran]] humans are the most populous ethnic group in Hiamyth, followed by [[Human#Shou|shou]] humans. Together, they make up around 70% of the humanoid population in most towns and cities.

Other species make up the majority groups in certain regions: [[Goliath|goliaths]] and [[Aarakocra|aarakocra]] in the Guong Range or [[Orc|orcs]] in the Shadowy Mountains,

The [[Elf#Wood Elf|wood elves]] of the sylvan courts rule a very large surface area but intermingle little with the other peoples, including their own serfs.

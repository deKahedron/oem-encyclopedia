The Umaran Empire is the largest territory in [[Oem]] ruled by a single monarch. At [[Ostorian Calendar#Current Year|present]], the Empire covers most of the south of the continent, including the provinces of [[Nadiria]], [[Dûnar]], [[Hiamyth]], and [[Fuegil & the Manilands]].

### History
The [[Gnome|gnomes]] of [[Nadiria]] have long been pioneers in [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] and technological advancement. However, their foreign policy had always been rather cautious and secretive; their machines were rarely sold, but rather leased.

In the mid 33rd century, the [[Fourth War of the Underdark]] (3257-3266), the [[War of the Cliffs]] (3268-3272) and the [[War of the Spine]] (3280), were all soundly resolved by one of the two sides hiring Nadirian artillery. These demonstrations made it clear, by the end of the century, that any armed conflict would be decided by Nadirian intervention.

As a side effect, the factions that had been on the losing side of these wars were forced to adopt guerrilla tactics to defend what territory they had left, especially the southern [[Elf#Drow|drow]] and the [[Goblin|goblins]] of Dûnar.

The empire was formally established on the 6th Crownsday of Autumn, 3291, when [[Salya Umar]] changed her title from Queen to Empress.

For nearly fifty years, the empire provided its vassal states with a slow and controlled improvement of their civil infrastructure, using arcane infusions and mechanisms. The provinces, however, were never allowed anywhere near the levels of Nadirian technology. These secrets were kept closely by the Nadirian guild masters.

Throughout the empire's heyday, it maintained a strong relationship with the [[University of Arcaxius]], fostered by [[Fenbryte Industries]] and the constant flow of new Nadirian students to the University.
### Chalican invasion
The first [[Chalican|chalicans]] were spotted on the western coasts of Oem in 3336. These strange creatures showed no interest in trade or communicating in any clear way. When they began insistently extracting resources from the lands they were occupying, they were forcefully repelled.

The following year, the chalicans returned. In one night, they destroyed every Nadirian metropolis, turning the region into the wasteland of volatile magic that it remains today. Imperial historians dubbed the event as the Tragedy of 3337. However, it is much more commonly remembered as the [[Great Humbling]], a name given by the [[Sacrum Sophia|sophics]].

Having lost the secret designs that once bound the empire, the nations that form it are still nominally united. In practice, however, the empire is split in two blocks: the Eastern Empire, composed of Dûnar and Hiamyth, and the Western Empire, composed of the Manilands.
### Modern day
Since the fall of Nadiria and the Umaran dynasty, the empire has been ruled by [[Weylin Damasil|Emperor Weylin Damasil]] from the new imperial capital, the mythian city of [[Stormcross]]. Due to the separation of powers and relative provincial sovereignty established during the empire's expansion, the Emperor now lives in a kingdom where he arguably has less power than the ruler of Hiamyth, [[Galigos Tinanbaria|Archduke Galigos Tinanbaria]].

The imperial family has little support from the common people. Among the longer-lived races, the Nadirians are still remembered as harbingers of mass-destruction, and the Great Humbling is held in superstitious regard as a form of divine punishment upon the gnomes for their hubris.

Many noble houses also favour the Archduke over the Emperor, especially [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count Aristarchos Lathlin]] of [[Highgale]]. Only a few families, such as the [[House Vostok|Vostoks]] and the [[Martha Krünne|Baronness Martha Krünne]] of [[Beltijna]] are openly loyal to the Emperor.

However, the continued existence of the empire is in no small part thanks to the Archduke, who enjoys an increased influence in other provinces than he would have without a centralised government to manipulate.
### Noble ranks
* Emperor. Dictates international laws and controls the imperial forces, such as the [[Imperial Rangers]] and [[Imperial Navy]].
* Marquis/Marchioness. A temporary ruler appointed by the Emperor to oversee a region during a time of war.
* Duke/Duchess. Ruler of an imperial province.
* Count/Countess. Hereditary ruler of a city or castle and its vassal towns.
* Baron/Baroness. Elected ruler of a city or castle and its vassal towns. The level of suffrage by which any given barony choses its leader varies greatly.

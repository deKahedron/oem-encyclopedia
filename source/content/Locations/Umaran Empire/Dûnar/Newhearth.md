### Founding
Newhearth was founded [[Hel-Dhao]] and the [[Platinum Pariahs]] in 2792, at the end of their long exodus from northern [[Hiamyth]]. According to legend, once they reached the top of Mount Jeong-Yo, Hel-Dhao plunged her sword into the ground, lighting a great bonfire that enveloped the peak of the mountain in warmth without harming any of her people. This fire, which still burns today, is known as the Eternal Ember.

For several centuries, Newhearth remained a small town with deep cultural significance to followers of [[Bahamut]] but little economic influence, isolated as it was.
### Arrival of the Harpers
When the Harpers relocated their headquarters to Newhearth in 3263, the entire town's way of life was upended. The constant presence of extremely wealthy adventurers made the town a hub of commerce in rare and expensive goods.

As part of this integration, a deal was struck with the High Priesthood of the town, guaranteeing the locals would have access to far lower prices on essential goods and limiting the area of the town that could be inhabited by members of the guild.

In 3294, the Harpers built the [[Tower of Sheathed Blades]] to serve as their new guildhall. In exchange, a new portal to the [[Elemental Planes|Elemental Plane of Water]] was opened, granting clean water for all in the town. As a side effect, the constant flow of water from the mountaintop increased the build-up of snow on the mountainside, further increasing the risk of avalanches on [[Mount Jeong-Yo#Pariah's Climb|Pariah's Climb]].
### Eternal Ember
From a distance, Newhearth appears to be completely engulfed in a slow-burning flame which illuminates the mountain peak. As one approaches the town itself, the flame appears to fade to a faint warm glow.

Stepping within the town's threshold immediately fills one with warmth, contrasting the biting chill of the outside mountain.

### Culture
[[Dwarf|Dwarvish]] culture is ubiquitous all across Dûnar. Their role as the great ambassadors between the surface and the Underdark have made turned their cavernous cities into trade hubs and given them access to crafting techniques from many other cultures.

Since its annexation into the Umaran Empire, Dûnar has seen very little large-scale conflict on the surface. This has allowed it to become the largest exporter of food in the Empire.

However, the are still hostilities between the forces of the Empire and some underground resistance groups. Some [[Elf#Drow|drow]] who resisted conquest by the Empire have resorted to guerrilla tactics in a bid to force the dwarves out of their ancestral caves.

The [[Centaur|centaurs]] who live in Dûnar migrated there after the [[Chalican|chalican]] invasion of their home in the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]]. They have taken up residence on the great planes and are thriving.

### Pre-empire
Nadiria was first formed by a militant dynasty of [[Gnome#Rock gnome|rock gnomes]], who conquered their surrounding lands through a combination of magical military technology and exploitative deals.

In the 33rd century, the Nadirians' technological superiority allowed them to become the de-facto arbiters of most of the continent, a rulership that was eventually formalised as the Umaran Empire.
### Umaran Empire
As Nadiria grew into the Umaran Empire, the extraction of resources from the imperial provinces allowed the Nadirians to build their cities into magical monuments. The scale and complexity of the magic employed in these works has not been seen before or since.

The cities made use of [[Fluiduct|fluiducts]] for transport and irrigation as well as for distributing clean water.

This society was, however, extremely unequal. Due to the prevalence of constructs, the vast majority of the population were no longer needed to tend the land nor defend it. In turn, any peasants without magical talent were reduced to little more than batteries, living in squalor and paid only in food, while donating their mental energy into the cities' [[Baculurb|baculurbs]].

Only those with natural magical talent could hope for a chance at social mobility. Families would remain in the city and continue to raise children in the faint hope that one of them might display such gifts and be able to elevate their kin to a life of luxury.
### The Great Humbling
In 3337, the great monument-cities of Nadiria were all attacked by [[Chalican|chalicans]]. The attack was carried out with a chemical weapon known as [[Chalican Catalyst|chalican catalyst]], which can supercharge magical effects and cause them to escape their creators' control. When this catalyst came into contact with the thousands of constructs, latent spells and ambient magical effects, especially the [[Arcanimbus|arcanimbi]], the cities were enveloped in cataclysmic magical chain reactions.

The eight largest cities were all hit in the same night, but all the smaller towns in the region were also attacked in similar ways over the following weeks.

*Unknown to the people of Oem, this attack was actually a joint effort between the chalicans and the [[Druid|druids]], who were responsible for infiltrating the jars of catalyst into the cities.*
### Wasteland
Since the Great Humbling, the surface of Nadiria has been turned into a wasteland of wild magic. The entire region has both visible and hidden magical dangers, but they are concentrated most around the old population centres.

Many such dangers are simply dormant spells awaiting a valid target, but the sheer number of magical interactions caused by the chalican attacks and Nadirian experimentation have left bizarre, seemingly broken magical effects in the environment, wildlife and even weather.

Of the few humanoids that still reside in the region, most are nomadic scavengers who search the safer ruins for valuable artefacts and anomalies. Some are mutated survivors from the fall, either unable or unwilling to leave the site of their fall.

Within the ruined cities, many constructs and works of civil engineering still function. These devices can be activated by inserting [[Electrum|electrum]] pieces into them, a remnant of the Nadirians ever-extractive relationship with their populace.

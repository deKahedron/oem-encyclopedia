Qilan is a region in the north of [[Oem]]. It borders [[Liengard]]. The entire region is a volcanic range dominated by the colossal dragon [[Qilakestis|Qilakestis, the God-Fire]].

Having claimed the entire region as his own, Qilakestis's hoard includes all of the gold that flows in the magma of the volcanoes, allowing him to grow to the size of a mountain, far too large to fly.

The clash between Qilakestis's regional effects and those of [[Bastorin's Spawn]] to the south creates the [[Wyrmwind]], a perpetual gale that ravages the bays to the southeast and northwest.

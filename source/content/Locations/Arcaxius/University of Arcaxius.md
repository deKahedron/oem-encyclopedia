The Sovereign University of Arcaxius is an independent [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] academic institution in [[Arcaxius]]. It is the largest organization of magical study in [[Oem]]. The University also serves as the diplomatic capital of Arcaxius.

### Free research programme
Since its founding, the University has maintained some form of contract that allows for the free usage of the University's resources, on the condition that the researchers results are also added to the Library's collection.

When the liches took power, this contract became a written document called a [[Contract of Contribution]]. This contract required the signee to remain within the grounds of the University until they had created a spell or another piece of research of significant enough value.
### Classes
The University hosts a number of archmage positions, mages who are given additional funding and space within the University grounds in exchange for teaching courses on their specialty. These classes ceased during the reign of the [[The Six Liches|Six Liches]], but resumed in a more limited form after their deaths. 
### History
#### Founding
In 1813, [[Caeran]], one of the greatest wizards in history, began building the [[Caeran#Library|Library]] underground in the middle of the [[Fintazza Jungle]]. The Library, a colossal arcane supercomputer modelled after Caeran's personality, continued to build and expand itself several centuries past Caeran's death, until it was nearly a kilometre across and over 100 metres deep.

In the year 2256, [[Xrathas]] founded the University above Caeran's Library. The University was announced as a haven for spellcasters to question, research and experiment freely without worry of political or religious interference.
#### Vecna's Rise
The first 50 years of the University were an optimistic period of boundless testing of magical limits. The first generation of students and collaborators who studied under and alongside of Xrathas became a close-knit community. A conclave of the most powerful and senior wizards at the University, called the [[Circle of Eight]], proclaimed their independence from other kingdoms and declared the surrounding jungle property of the University.

In the year 2310, [[Vecna]], a member of the Circle of Eight, underwent a dark ritual to become the first lich. After some debate, the rest of the Circle decided that Vecna's experiments had gone too far and should be stopped. Vecna in turn declared that the Circle had abandoned the University's stated goal of allowing for unfettered magical research. He abandoned the conclave and murdered Xrathas.

Vecna was destroyed by the Circle in 2314. Since there were no survivors, the details of the confrontation, as well as the fate of Vecna's spellbook, the [[The Book of Vile Darkness|Book of Vile Darkness]], were unknown.
#### Silent Age
The centuries following the fall of Vecna were underscored by the fear of creating another Vecna. Over the course of five hundred years, research continued at a steady pace and many new spells were created and refined, but very few major advancements were made.

Sometime before the year 2572, a beholder manifested in the Library. The beholder came to be known as the [[The Librarian|Librarian]], and slowly began taking over Caeran's territory.
#### The Six Liches
Around the time that Vecna's ascension was fading from living memory, [[The Six Liches|six new liches]] overtook the University. These liches ruled for a further five hundred years. During the liches' reign, researchers were still allowed to use the facilities for their own ends, but organised classes were cancelled.

The University's outward diplomacy in this era was mostly based on selling services in exchange for natural resources. During this era, [[Fenbryte Industries]] also held its headquarters within the University.
#### The Twin Kings
After the fall of the liches, the University was claimed by [[Marred Draymor]] as his seat of power, crowning himself and his brother [[Aki Draymor|Aki]] as kings of the University. Since his rise, King Marred has reopened the gates of the University, allowing total freedom among researchers and paying great sums from the liches' treasury to bring in divine experts for the first time in centuries.

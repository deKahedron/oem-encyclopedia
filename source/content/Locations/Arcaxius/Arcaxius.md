### Environment
Most of Arcaxius is covered by the [[Fintazza Jungle]], a dense rainforest with little sapient life. Near the border with [[Alheuffikh]], the forest becomes more sparse and dry, while the constant onslaught of the [[Wyrmwind]] renders the northern coast desolate.
### The Fine Lanes
The Fintazza Jungle is scored by three perfectly straight roads that connect the University to the three neighbouring regions.
* **[[Xrathas|Xrathas's]] Lane.** Connects to [[Alheuffikh]]. Named after the first professor of the University.
* **[[Bigby|Bigby's]] Lane.** Connects to [[Nadiria]].
* **[[Tenser|Tenser's]] Lane.** Connects to the northern [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]]. Formerly named [[Acererak|Acererak's]] Lane.
### The University
The most powerful settlement in Arcaxius is the [[University of Arcaxius|Sovereign University of Arcaxius]]. This academic institution was formed around [[Caeran|Caeran's]] colossal Library construct, and exists as a locus for magical practice and experimentation far away from other meddling laws.

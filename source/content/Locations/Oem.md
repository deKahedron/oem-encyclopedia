**Oem** is an isolated continent surrounded on all sides by perilous oceans, located on its planet's southern hemisphere.

In the current day, several distinct sovereign territories cover the surface of the continent:
# [[Umaran Empire|The Umaran Empire]]
The Umaran Empire is a crumbling collection of provinces once unified by the technological might of [[Nadiria]]. These provinces are [[Hiamyth]], [[Dûnar]], [[Nadiria]] and [[Fuegil & the Manilands]].
# [[Union of Alheuffikh]]
The arid lands of [[Alheuffikh]] are home to many different cultures separated by vast swathes of desert. [[The Eleven Great Cities]] hold a peaceful alliance of trade and wartime collaboration.
# [[Arcaxius]]
In the centre of the colossal [[Fintazza Jungle]], the [[University of Arcaxius|Sovereign University of Arcaxius]] welcomes researchers and mages of all sorts who wish to study or gain power away from the interests of other nations.
# [[Liengard]]
Despite its tropical latitude, the tundras of [[Liengard]] remain a frozen wasteland, due to the millennial influence of a [[Bastorin's Spawn|dynasty of white dragons]].
# [[Qilan]]
A volcanic peninsula to the north of Liengard, completely overrun by the territorial influence of the red [[Dragon|dragon]] [[Qilakestis]].
# [[The Disputed Lands]]
After the fall of [[Nadiria]] in the [[Nadiria#The Great Humbling|Great Humbling]], The western coast of the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]] was conquered by invading [[Chalican|chalicans]].

The Raincage is an arena in the Flowing District of [[Rhyza]]. It is the holy site of the demigod [[The Water Dancer]].

The arena has a large circular floor of polished stone. Five levels of seats are arranged around the edge, and the entire monument is enveloped in a mesh of metal grapevines. The ambient magic of the site maintains the air within the cage cool and slightly humid.

The arena is used for sports events, artistic demonstrations, high-profile duels and executions.

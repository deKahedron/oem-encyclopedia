The Easel of Sand is a tower on the western outskirts of [[Rhyza]]. It is the holy site of the demigod [[Diamond Eyes]].

The tower has very little on its lower floors save for a staircase up to the top. The top floor has a wide glass window that spans almost half of the tower's round wall, overlooking the [[Lfaeria Desert]].

An underground passage connects the tower to the inside of the city.

When Diamond Eyes was mantled by Salma Um-wettikh, a lift was added to the tower so that she wouldn't need to use the stairs.

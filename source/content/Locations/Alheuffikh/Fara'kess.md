Fara'kess was an ancient [[Maruk|maruk]] city and the holy site of [[Ifriko]]. 

[[Kyore of Three Pains]] was imprisoned here for millennia, until she was slain by the [[Norman Brothers]]. The [[Soul of Dune]] was hidden within Kyore's prison palace.

After the disappearance of the artificial rivers, many maruk fled here to hide from their enemies. Eventually, their desired were consumed by Kyore.

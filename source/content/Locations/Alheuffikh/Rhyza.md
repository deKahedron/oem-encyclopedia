Rhyza is the largest city in [[Alheuffikh]] and the cultural centre of the [[Elf#High Elf|high elves]]. It is located on the eastern coast of the region, in the Bay of Lapis, at the mouth of the river Alethn.

### Government
Rhyza is governed by the Nine Viziers, a group of four representatives from powerful families and five elected representatives who vote on matters of internal and external policy.
### Demographics
Rhyza has a population of around 180.000 people. Despite being known as a high elven city, elves only represent around 15% of the population. The rest is mostly made up of [[Human|humans]], [[Half-Elf|half-elves]], [[Goblin|goblins]] and, most recently, [[Minotaur|minotaurs]].

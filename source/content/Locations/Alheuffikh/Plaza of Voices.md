The Plaza of Voices is a square in the Sonorous District of [[Rhyza]]. It is the holy site of the demigod [[The Golden Chord]].

The acoustics of the square are designed to carry sound clearly or resonate with harmonic reverberations, depending on where the source of the sound is located.

Aside from the Golden Chord's daily performances, the square is used for public announcements, political and philosophical debate and live music or theatre.

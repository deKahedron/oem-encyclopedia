Sai'ta was an ancient [[Maruk|maruk]] city and the holy site of [[Souiek]].

Since the high priestess of Souiek rejected [[Ta'ux]], the city was completely cut off from her rivers; even when these rivers returned to the world after the awakening at [[Maruk Emn]].

Only once the high priestess called out to Ta'ux in supplication, begging for aid in her time of need, did the waters finally break back into the city.
### High Priestess
The city housed the last living maruk in history: the very priestess who had doomed her race. Her wish for repentance had kept her alive and in mourning.

The Solarium is a structure on the northern outskirts of [[Rhyza]]. It is the holy site of the demigod [[The Steel Poet]].

The structure is built as a pit in the ground with a roof made of lenses and mirrors that focus sunlight within the main chamber, raising the temperature so high that the Poet can twist and shape metal with their bare hands.

An underground passage connects the forge to the inside of the city.

Since the disappearance of the last Steel Poet in the 27th century, the Solarium has remained unlit and covered up with a tarpaulin sheet so that it can be safely maintained.

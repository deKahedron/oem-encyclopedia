The Cold Archive is a massive library in the Ornate District of [[Rhyza]]. It is the holy site of the demigod [[Bloodquill]].

The library is built in an ascending spiral design with extremely thick walls. Two-thirds of the building are underground which, along with the funnel shape of the walls, helps to keep the building cool and dry. The interior is well-lit with white walls, to allow for easy reading, and every wall is adorned with intricate calligraphy that serves for decoration and orientation.

Aside from books and scrolls, the Archive is also used to preserve ancient artefacts and other delicate or sensitive items.

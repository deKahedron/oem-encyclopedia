The northern coast of Alheuffikh is meets the [[Veleayan Sea]].

The region is very arid. The [[Lfaeria Desert]] covers most of the east and south of the land, and most of the rest is a covered in vast savannah.

The region is governed by the [[Union of Alheuffikh]].

Species native to the region include [[Elf#High Elf|high elves]], [[Elf#Eladrin|eladrin]], [[Tabaxi|tabaxi]], [[Gnoll|gnolls]], [[Gallivespian|gallivespians]], [[Giff|giff]], solar [[Luruthi|luruthi]], [[Tortle|tortles]], [[Othraxi|othraxi]] and the extinct [[Maruk|maruk]].

Since the return of the maruk river god [[Ta'ux]], four ancient rivers have been restored across the dry lands, causing a sudden boom of wildlife.

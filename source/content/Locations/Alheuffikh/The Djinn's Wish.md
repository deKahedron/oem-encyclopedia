The Djinn's Wish is a cabaret in [[Rhyza]], founded in 3432 by [[The Bastard Bard]]. The venue hosts travelling shows and independent musicians and playwrights, as well as offering food.

The walls of the showroom are decorated with artistic depictions of the exploits of the [[Norman Brothers]].

The Wish is currently run by Yora Al-aenil. Her brother Daegon works the kitchen and [[Eurel the Pianist]] is hosts the guest performers. The retired gunsmith [[T-Bag|Theodore Toolsbag]] resides in the Wish, and his son [[Justin Toolsbag|Justin]] helps wherever he's needed.

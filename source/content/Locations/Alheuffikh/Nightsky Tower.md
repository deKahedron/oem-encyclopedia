Nightsky Tower is a building in the Luminous District of [[Rhyza]]. It is the holy site of the demigod [[Redstar]].

The tower is the tallest structure in the city. The whole design of the tower is built to work as a gigantic arcane focus; the tower's peak is crowned with an obelisk of red quartz that allows the Redstar to extend the range of their spells all the way outside the city.

The outside of the tower is coated in a special enamel that gives the tower a dark opacity when in direct sunlight but a shimmering red glow in starlight.

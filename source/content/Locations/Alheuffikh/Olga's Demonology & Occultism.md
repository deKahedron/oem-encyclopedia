Olga's Demonology & Occultism is the residence of [[Olga Zamazeera]], the retired deathwalker of the [[Candleguard]] and champion of the [[Raven Queen]]. From her house in the Sandways of [[Rhyza]], Olga provides spellcasting services, miracles and advice. She has is also in the process of offloading her collection of dark artefacts to upstanding people, in the hopes that not too many of them will fall into the wrong hands once she dies.

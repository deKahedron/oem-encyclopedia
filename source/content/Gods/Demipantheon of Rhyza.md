[[Elf#High Elf|High Elf]] religion recognises a pantheon of Greater Gods, who are credited with the creation of the natural planes of existence. These include gods such as [[Corellon Larethian]], [[Angharradh]], [[Sehanine]] and [[Sashelas]], who they share with the other elven cultures.

While these deities are worshipped on special occasions, the Young Gods of the Demipantheon of Rhyza receive far more focus as aspirational figures.

Unlike the Greater Gods, whose origin is usually attributed to the cosmic beginnings of the multiverse, the Young Gods were mortals; [[Elf|elves]] or [[Half-Elf|half-elves]] who ascended to divinity through talent and dedication to their creative crafts.

The demigods are no less mortal than any other elf. Each time one of them dies, the most outstanding artist in the corresponding craft is eligible to mantle the identity of the fallen demigod.

An ascended demigod acquires the knowledge and skill of all their past incarnations, and some believe that the personality of their avatar is supplanted or intermingled with those of all these previous artists. 

Not all of the Demipantheon is always manifested. This is partly because the artistic trends of each era favour some disciplines over others. Additionally, certain historical anomalies have meant that a position has remained vacant for longer than usual.

In the [[Sophism|sophic]] pantheon, tortoise-shaped Kelim is the patron god of oral tradition, folklore, and farming techniques.

Kelim is worshipped by farmers and shepherds, who often keep tortoises around storehouses and flock animals to honor the god as well as scare away owlbears. It is said that so long as the great oral traditions are kept safe, the tortoise god will protect his people.

Kelim's most common symbol is the Bent Rod, representing a shillelagh staff. Hand-made shillelaghs are used as a symbol of Kelim

Souiek was the ancient [[Maruk|maruk]] [[Divine Magic#Gods|god]] of wind and the home. Souiek was represented as a hybrid of an elephant, a honey badger and a lion.

Souiek was an all-gender being, fiercely loyal and protective of its family.

A [[High Priestess of Souiek|priestess of Souiek]] was responsible for the wish that ousted [[Ta'ux]] from the maruk pantheon and led to the downfall of the maruk.

Ifriko was the ancient [[Maruk|maruk]] [[Divine Magic#Gods|god]] of the dunes and mirages. He was represented as a hybrid of a scorpion, a snake and a leopard.

Ifriko was a deceiver god who helped the maruk evade predators during the [[Age of Titans]].

Due to the way the [[Norman Brothers]] described the appearance of [[Kyore of Three Pains]] and the fact that she was trapped in [[Fara'kess]], Ifriko's holy city, [[Olga Zamazeera]] posited that the original appearance of Ifriko may suggest that the maruk originally worshipped Kyore herself as a god.

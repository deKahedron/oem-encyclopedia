Bahamut is one of the two [[Dragon|dragon]] [[Divine Magic#Gods|gods]], alongside [[Tiamat]].
### Description
Bahamut is a [[Divine Magic#Pure gods|pure god]]. He is typically represented as a long, serpent-like dragon with platinum scales and no arms, legs or wings.
### Origin
Bahamut was once one of the heads of Tiamut, a four-headed dragon with pearlescent platinum scales. During an event known as the [[Excision of Tiamut|Excision]], Bahamut turned on the other heads and abandoned them, taking with him Tiamut's metallic shine and leaving behind the reflected colours. From the stump, two more heads grew, and what remained became Tiamat.
### Domains
As one of only two gods in his pantheon, Bahamut reflects one half of a vast set of complementary domains. The main dualities are the following:
* Tiamat is multiple, Bahamut is individual.
* Tiamat is chromatic, Bahamut is metallic.
* Tiamat always was, Bahamut was born.
* Tiamat is natural, Bahamut is self-defined.
* Tiamat is female, Bahamut is male.
* Tiamat is imprisoned, Bahamut escaped.
* Tiamat is incarnate, Bahamut is a pure god.
* Tiamat is territorial, Bahamut is welcoming.
* Tiamat is cataclysmic, Bahamut is subtle.
* Tiamat inspires joy, Bahamut inspires melancholy.
* Tiamat is of the sky, Bahamut is of the sea.

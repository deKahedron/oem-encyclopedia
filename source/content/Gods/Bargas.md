Bargas is a god of kinship in the [[Sophism|sophic]] pantheon takes the form of a great grey wolfhound. Statuettes and etchings of Bargas symbolize the mutual knowledge of each other that all friends and family have. They are typically found in family portraits, as well as the entrances to inns, taverns and other such communal buildings.

Full body representations of him always have one foot off the ground, representing how these relationships are ever-changing and in motion.

Temples to Bargas are built in a circular shape, with a space for a fire pit in the middle. Acolytes of Bargas will often carry a steel lighter shaped in the likeness of the striding dog.

When an acolyte of Bargas takes a vow of silence to preserve a valued connection, they carry with them a piece of coal, symbolizing a relationship that does not currently burn but still exists. When the vow is broken, that piece of coal is returned to the flames. [[Zinnia]] once took such a vow of silence, to protect her treasonous father from having his shame known to their community.

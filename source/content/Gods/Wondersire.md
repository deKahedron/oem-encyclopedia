Wondersire (known in [[Elvish]] as Yatas'salaf) is the [[Elf#High Elf|high elf]] demigod of architecture. Wondersire built the great monuments and temple-homes of the rest of the [[Demipantheon of Rhyza|Demipantheon]].

The most recent avatar of this demigod died recently, in 3426, and the next incarnation has not yet come forth.

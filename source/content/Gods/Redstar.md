The Redstar (known in [[Elvish]] as *Najma'ara*) is the [[Elf#High Elf|high elf]] demigod of arcana and archmage of the [[Nightsky Tower]].

The last great wizard to wear this mantle was the [[Half-Elf]] [[Josslyn Qui-vaer]], who died over 300 years ago. There were many brilliant pretenders to the position, but for unknown reasons, the spirit of Redstar didn't manifest in any of them until the death of [[Zaledrix Almeyar]] in 3433.

The current Redstar is [[Lanouf Al-hamsa]].

Zail, the goddess of Lost Knowledge, is the psychopomp deity of the [[Sophism|sophic]] pantheon. She takes the form of a grey homing pigeon. When a creature dies, it is said that she visits them in their last moments to collect all the knowledge that will be lost with the individual’s death. As she returns home with this knowledge, she brings with her the soul of the departed, to be judged and sent to the outer planes.

Zail is worshipped by all those who witness death in their day-to-day, from soldiers to doctors to gravediggers.

Her symbols are a pigeon’s head and a wrapped paper scroll. Gravediggers in her service often wear a bird-shaped mask.

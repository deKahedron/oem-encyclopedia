Tiamat is one of the two [[Dragon|dragon]] [[Divine Magic#Gods|gods]], alongside [[Bahamut]].
### Description
Tiamat is an [[Divine Magic#Incarnate gods|incarnate god]]. Her physical form is that of a gargantuan dragon with multi-coloured scales in the five colours of the [[Dragon#Chromatic dragons|chromatic dragons]]. She has five heads, one shaped and coloured like each of the five chromatic dragon types.

Tiamat's five heads share one mind, though she speaks through one head or another to express different emotions and thoughts.
### Origin
During the early [[Age of Titans]], Tiamut was a four-headed dragon with pearlescent platinum scales that reflected all the colours of the rainbow. During an event known as the [[Excision of Tiamut|Excision]], one of her heads turned on the others and abandoned them, taking with it Tiamut's metallic shine and becoming Bahamut. From the stump, two more heads grew, and what remained became Tiamat.

It is unknown whether Tiamut ever existed as a [[Divine Magic#Pure gods|pure god]] before incarnating into her physical form.
### Imprisonment
Near the end of the [[Thousand Years War]], Tiamat was imprisoned on [[Avernus]] by [[Fomorian]]. She resides there still, though whether or not she wishes to leave is unknown. 
### Domains
As one of only two gods in her pantheon, Tiamat reflects one half of a vast set of complementary domains.
* Tiamat is multiple, Bahamut is individual.
* Tiamat is chromatic, Bahamut is metallic.
* Tiamat always was, Bahamut was born.
* Tiamat is natural, Bahamut is self-defined.
* Tiamat is female, Bahamut is male.
* Tiamat is imprisoned, Bahamut escaped.
* Tiamat is incarnate, Bahamut is a pure god.
* Tiamat is territorial, Bahamut is welcoming.
* Tiamat is cataclysmic, Bahamut is subtle.
* Tiamat inspires joy, Bahamut inspires melancholy.
* Tiamat is of the sky, Bahamut is of the sea.

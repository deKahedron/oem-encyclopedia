Ta'ux was the ancient [[Maruk|maruk]] [[Divine Magic#Gods|god]] of rivers, healing and war. Ta'ux was represented as a hybrid of a mantis, a crane and a zebra.

The maruk Ta'ux interpreted Ta'ux as a benevolent conqueror, who spread her influence by claiming dead lands of the desert with her rivers.
### Rise in maruk culture
As the artificial rivers became the core of maruk societal growth, Ta'ux was pushed to the forefront of her pantheon. Maruk culture then changed to promote Ta'ux's values of conquest through healing.
### Downfall
After the [[High Priestess of Souiek|high priestess of Souiek]] wished to see Ta'ux's influence diminished, Ta'ux was cast out of the pantheon. In a desperate attempt to cling to existence before she faded entirely, she [[Corraesion|corraeded]] into a [[Demon|demon]] and manifested in the 107th layer of the [[Abyss]], the [[The Abyss#The Sea of Screams|Sea of Screams]].
### Demon Lord of Conquest
Over the millennia, Ta'ux found great success as a demon lord. She rose to become the most powerful being in the Sea of Screams. Mantling the title of Demon Lord of Conquest, she bound her power to an ever-growing need to claim powerful victories. 

By the year 3430, she had conquered and successfully held a further three layers of the Abyss. Many powerful demons owed their fealty to Ta'ux, including at least one balor, one [[Quarthatan|quarthatan]], the succubus [[Aurath]], the marilith Solokkoth Aruvin-Yaak and the glabrezu Ral'kensaveeta Kos.

When the demon lord [[Baphomet]] was slain in 3430, Ta'ux made threw her lot into the fray to claim the [[The Abyss#The Endless Maze|Endless Maze]]. She waged war across the Abyss for two years, forcing [[Demogorgon]] and [[Kostchtchie]] to retreat back to their thrones. By 3432, only she and [[Yeenoghu]] remained as contenders to claim Baphomet's realm.
### The Hunger War
As the conflict between Yeenoghu and Ta'ux escalated, the Lord of Hunger called his [[Gnoll|gnoll]] servants to action, wreaking havoc across Dûnar and especially Alheuffikh. Ta'ux, lacking a similar force of followers on the [[Material Plane]], dispatched Aurath to open a portal through which she could bring her armies.

Using the stolen [[Demonomicon of Iggwilv]], Aurath travelled to [[Maruk Emn]] and reactivated the ancient portal gate that the maruk once used to connect to the [[Elemental Planes|Elemental Plane of Water]], corrupting it to instead create a gate to the Abyss.

Shortly after Ta'ux's forces began to cross, the [[Norman Brothers]] were able to sabotage the ritual, switching the portal back to the Plane of Water and reopening the ancient rivers.

With only a portion of her army on the Material Plane, Ta'ux split her attention between battling Yeenoghu and consolidating power from the other maruk cities. 

At Ta'ux's holy city of [[Annara]], her lieutenants failed to retrieve [[Conquering Waters]], a magical glaive forged in her honour.
### Return to divinity
When [[Adala Nindlor]] wished to [[Kyore of Three Pains]] to undo all of the [[Ahamkara|ahamkara's]] previous wishes, Ta'ux was suddenly returned to her status as a god and faded to the [[Outer Planes]].

As a god with no followers, her power rapidly began to dwindle. Adala Nindlor, using Conquering Waters as a conduit, began to commune with Ta'ux, learning the story of her rise and fall. Nindlor, seeing herself reflected in the river god, offered herself as Ta'ux's champion in the battle against Yeenoghu's [[Wendigo]].
### Rebirth as the gnoll god of redemption
After Yeenoghu was defeated in [[Rhyza]], the gnolls that had been under his direct control were suddenly freed. Many of them were aimless, searching for spiritual guidance. In these gnolls, Ta'ux found new worshippers who could sustain her existence.

Since her adoption by the liberated gnolls, Ta'ux has been somewhat redefined, acquiring more canine features.

In the autumn of 3434, roughly a year since the fall of Yeenoghu, Adala Nindlor returned her glaive to the waters of the river Alethn, parting ways with Ta'ux.

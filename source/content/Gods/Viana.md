Viana is the god of Magic in the [[Sophism|sophic]] pantheon. Bat-formed Viana was revered just as much as any other aspect of [[Oghma]], until the [[Great Humbling|Humbling]] of 3337 shook the spiritual zeitgeist of [[Fuegil]] into the arcane-fearing society it is today.

Private shrines to the Red Bat can still be found in gnomish households outside of the Manilands, but within this region, her worship usually leads to ostracization, followed by an investigation by the [[Humble Order]].

 The perched bat and the crescent moon are both symbols of faith to this diminished deity.

### Founding
The gang was formed by the two young thieves [[Waive Barin]] and [[Fizz]]. The name Stormriders came from a kiteboat of the same name built by one of Barin's fathers, used for smuggling across the [[Bay of Grief]].
### Expansion
In the last years of their activity, the Stormriders started building support among the common folk of [[Hiamyth]]. The gang leaders, as well as the dragon priestess [[Zhie-Lai|Sister Zhie]], would make a show of giving gold and supplies to the people of towns near their hideouts.

This in turn protected them against being turned in by those same commoners and allowed them to make more daring heists.

This increase in ambition grew further when the gang recruited [[Taeko Vostok]], a powerful wizard with inside knowledge of the mythian nobility.
### Downfall
After three successive raids against [[Aristarchos Lathlin|Count Lathlin]] of [[Highgale]], the gang suffered two heavy blows.

The [[Kriv & Friends (party)|outlaw band led by Kriv Dilmerev]] attacked the Stormriders during the night in revenge for a previous theft. The ambush killed or maimed around half the gang.

At the same time, Waive Barin was experimenting with the [[Ravenous Rose]], gifted to him by [[Aurath|Igna]], of Dilmerev's band. He was able to resurrect two of his fallen companions, and took them both to chase down Dilmerev's band.

Before their leader returned, the Stormriders were also attacked by agents of Count Lathlin at their hideout. Taeko Vostok was taken captive and [[Maeve|Dowe]] was killed, but the rest of the gang escaped with a captive priest.

When the escapees reunited with Waive, [[Yaykov]] led a mutiny against him, in response to the leadership decisions that led to the gang's situation and under the suspicion that Waive had tipped the Count off in the hopes of shedding the wounded gang members.

After rescuing Taeko and resurrecting [[Maeve]], the gang escaped Highgale in the count's pleasure barge, fleeing to the calmer waters of the [[Veleayan Sea]].
### Trapped at sea
After Maeve was originally saved, she was unable to sleep; instead she would completely shut down during the day. This took a great toll on her mental health.

Taeko Vostok tried to create an electrical second soul to fill the void of Dowe's death. The electrical being didn't work. It initially replicated behaviours and mannerisms of Maeve and Dowe, but over time became more repetitive, violent and volatile.

Eventually, the new Dowe dissociated from its body completely, extending its will into the ropes of the ship and imprisoned the Stormriders aboard their own hull. Over the following weeks, the raging construct captured three other vessels, roping them into a single phantom fleet.

Yaykov was able to evade capture long enough to contact his sister [[Yelena]], who arrived alongside the [[The Nameless Conspirators|Nameless Conspirators]] to rescue him and the other Stormriders.

[[Yurtris Fenbryte|The Plenty]], who had organised the rescue mission, then extorted the Stormriders, threatening to turn them back in to the Count if Taeko Vostok did not join her project. Taeko accepted the offer, taking Maeve's body with her.
### Current whereabouts
The current whereabouts of the Stormriders, now led by Captain Yaykov, are unknown.
### Notable heists
* **Robbery on Vostok land.** One of Waive and Fizz's first jobs, and their first encounter with Taeko Vostok, who released them before they faced harsher punishment.
* **Jail-block Shock.** Taeko's first job with the gang was a mission to break Fizz out of Rimech Jail. Maeve and Yaykov, prisoners at the same prison, were recruited into the gang.
* **Post-Heist.** A robbery of the [[Tramore]] couriers' office. Maeve and Yaykov's first trial in the gang, which produced both the magical gem that awakened Yaykov's psionic abilities and the information about the [[War of the Hunt]].
* **Summer Shack Smash-and-Grab.** The gang raided Count Lathlin's empty summer house, freeing recruiting the tiefling former slaves [[Clamour]] and [[Becko]]. During the escape, Amos was killed by the bounty hunter [[Gracken Hast]]. After killing Hast, the gang looted his lightning-powered gear.
* **Forge Forgery.** Hoping to replicate Hast's gear in preparation for an upcoming heist, the gang forged an order of rare alloys from [[Enskjeld Foundry]]. Using forgery and telepathic manipulation, the bandits were able to steal the materials they needed without stepping foot inside the foundry.
* **Surrender Surrounder.** After spying on [[Kriv Dilmerev]] in [[Beltijna]], the Stormriders learned that the Dilmerev's gang planned to rob a convoy of magic items given as surrender terms to end the War of the Hunt. Instead of robbing the convoy directly, the Stormriders waited for Dilmerev's gang to finish with the guards before stealing the prize from them. This sparked a bitter rivalry that would lead to both gangs collapsing.
* **Closing Sail.** The gang broke into [[Glaumdr Palace]] to rescue Taeko Vostok, escaping the city in Count Lathlin's personal barge. The sight of Clamour biting through the chains of the naval portcullis became a symbol of revolutionary sentiment among the tiefling slaves of Highgale.

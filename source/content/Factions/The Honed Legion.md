Among the [[Five Hosts]] of [[Maglubiyet]], the Honed Legion has a history of selling its services against peasant uprisings.

While the legion has a fairly typical distribution of [[Goblinoid|goblinoids]] among its ranks, its attacks and external relations are usually only carried out by [[Hobgoblin|hobgoblins]]. [[Goblin|Goblins]] are relegated to supporting or servile roles and their scarce few [[Bugbear|bugbears]] are used in covert missions, such as assassination on night time terror attacks, that would seem unbecoming of the Legion’s reputation.

In battle, the Legion is best known for their cataphracts, heavily armoured cavalry with lances. Against undisciplined foes or deployed as a second wave after an enemy formation has broken, this force uses [[Worg|worgs]] instead of horses. [[Worg|Worgs]] are slower and tire faster, but their natural viciousness inflicts further brutality on enemy troops and morale.

When battling in cities or other close environments, the legion relies mostly on pavisier crossbowmen.

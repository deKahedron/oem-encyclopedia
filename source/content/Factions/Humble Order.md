### History
The Humble Order formed in 3338 in response to the [[Great Humbling]]. As waves of religious fanaticism began to turn public opinion against [[Arcane Magic|arcane]] practitioners, the order of Unbound Wings renamed itself the Humble Order and all swore a new oath to pursue all uncontrolled practitioners of magic and bring them to justice.

In 3433, a team of elite Humble Order knights was dispatched to eliminate [[The Six Liches|the Six Liches]] of [[University of Arcaxius|Arcaxius]]. While they did not directly kill any of the liches, the sacrifice of [[Laubrey Lafay|Sir Laubrey Lafay]] proved instrumental in the death of [[Zaledrix Almeyar]] and the destruction of their [[The Broken Minute|phylactery]]. Nevertheless, the broader Humble Order claimed full credit for the deed.

In the [[The Glamoran Civil War|Glamoran Civil War]], the Humble Order acted as the main enforcers of the orthodox Sacrum Sophia.
### Imagery
The Humble Order is immediately recognizable for the white and orange colour of their armour and barding.

Their shields and banners bear the ancient symbol of the Order's past: a pair of disembodied orange wings on a white field.
### Philosophy
Despite being indoctrinated to loathe spellcasters, the knights of the Humble Order live in the constant hypocrisy of being spellcasters themselves. They are trained to accept the idea that they are uniquely suited to wield magic because of the immense guilt they internalise about doing so.

The main beliefs of the order are summarised in the tenets of their oath:
* **Withhold your ambitions.** You are a conduit of judgement, not a dealer of it.
* **Protect the meek.** Those who do not aspire higher than they belong must be shielded from those who do.
* **Shine the path.** All creatures are tempted by power. The weak give in to those temptations and must be guided back to their place.
* **Remember your place.** You are worthy of your power only because you have the virtue to not use it freely.
### Training, tactics and equipment
Humble Order knights receive extensive mental conditioning to be resistant to charms and illusions. They are able to sense the presence of nearby spellcasters.

Knights of the order work in groups of three. When engaging a suspected spellcaster, one of the three will always refrain from approaching until the others have made contact with the target, so that the caster is unable to incapacitate the entire squadron with a single concentration spell.

Each knight in the order carries handcuffs and a gag in their person to disable casters from performing somatic or verbal components. In more senior squadrons, at least one of the knights will carry a *Pilum of Silence* and a *Spellbreaker Shield*.
### Ranks, roles and numbers
The Humble Order fields around 300 knights, plus twice as many squires and again as many confessors and a few dozen exorcists. The most senior third of the knights serve as captains, each leading a squadron. When a squadron loses a member, a squire will often be added to fill the vacancy.

Confessors serve as both interrogators and psychological support to the knights. Exorcists are specialised priests who are able to shatter the enchantments within [[Magic Item|magic items]].

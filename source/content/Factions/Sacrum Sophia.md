The Sacrum Sophia is the central authority of [[Sophism|Sophic religion]]. It traces its origins to the [[Forest Gnome|forest gnomes]] of [[Chelió]]. During the [[Nadiria|Nadirian]] expansion, it spread across the Umaran Empire with a small following. After the [[Great Humbling]], it became the main religious institution in the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]].

The [[Humble Order]] are elite mage-hunting inquisitors that act as the Sacrum's enforcers.

The current [[High Cognizant]] of the Sacrum is [[Humbert Theofritt]], an old [[Gnome|gnome]].
### Temples
Within every Sacrum temple to a god of this pantheon, there is a small, closed-off section dedicated to [[Oghma]] himself, called the sacellum.
### Organization
The Sacrum decides what knowledge is safe to be disseminated and what [[Magic|magic]] is legal to practice.

The Orthodox branch of the Sacrum Sophia strongly maintains the values that helped the nation survive the chalican invasion. The last century exacted a heavy toll on the people of the Manilands.

Within the Sacrum, everything is hierarchical. People must do as they are told by people who know more than them. The wise rule over the ignorant, ever expanding their knowledge.

Sacrum texts use lots of big words and overly-complex terminology, which makes their messages sound more inscrutable and intimidating to the common folk.

The Sacrum portrays itself as the intelligent elite who interface with this all-knowing entity directly, while the lesser gods of the pantheon represent smaller aspects of [[Oghma]], fields of knowledge simple enough for “normal people” to comprehend. These aspects of [[Oghma]] take more familiar forms.
### Schism
During the [[The Glamoran Civil War|Glamoran Civil War]], the Sacrum used the national treasury to hire the support of the [[The Honed Legion|Honed Legion]].
#### Extremists
Orthodox extremists burn Glamoran tutors and worshippers in blue pyres of sulphur that leave behind only a melted red substance.

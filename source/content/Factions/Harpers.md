The Harpers are a guild of adventurers that operate all across Oem. They specialise in fighting monsters and are sworn to protect the lives of people while remaining neutral in other matters.

### Founding
The Harpers were founded by three adventurers known collectively as the Greaves. During the [[Aurora Rain]], these adventurers, as well as many others, had been stretched thin trying to attend to crisis after crisis in different points across the continent, overlapping with each other at some sites and collectively failing to attend to others.

In response, the three adventurers took influence from historic figures like [[Hel-Dhao]] and began sowing trust in the idea of classic heroes, embodying the very archetypes they uplifted. As they met more adventurers, they recruited more to their cause, eventually becoming an official guild with a headquarters in [[Bathannot]].

The name Harpers was chosen in honour of [[Lineeckah|Lineeckah's]] epithet, partially as a way to ingratiate the nobility of [[Hiamyth]], among whom Hel-Dhao remained a controversial figure.
### Political involvement
Within the first years of their activity, the Harpers were involved in helping organise the riots that led to the overthrowing of the county of Bathannot and the creation of the pseudo-democratic barony in its place.

In response to many other nobles placing pressure on the guild to assist them in their own power struggles, High Harper Claravela Sixpins moved the headquarters to the small mountain village of [[Newhearth]], in the [[Guong Range]]. This in itself was considered a charged decision, as Newhearth was founded by Hel-Dhao.

Since then, the Harpers' official stance is that they do not select contracts based on political affiliation. While their members are free to fight in any conflict they wish, the guild will not assign jobs against regional governments nor against their citizens.
### Responsibilities and protections
Most often, a city government or noble sends a contract to Newhearth, where the High Harper assigns a team of adventurers to tend to the issue and claim the bounty.

The expertise and authority of the Harpers is respected across the whole of the [[Umaran Empire]] and parts of [[Alheuffikh]]. While on an assignment, travelling Harpers are expected to lend their aid to ailing commoners, to uphold the honour of their guild. 

In return, the adventurers are expected to be allowed to keep most treasure they acquire as a direct result of dealing with the threat.

Additionally, Harpers are generally treated with greater leniency by local law in regards to any collateral damage they cause as a direct result of their intervention. Non-guild adventurers are rarely afforded such treatment.
### Guild dues and benefits
In a very dangerous and inconsistently lucrative line of work, the guild aims to provide some safety and stability to its members.

At the end of an assignment, Harpers are required to pay the guild 40% of the treasure they looted. In return, the adventurers are given free lodging in Newhearth, upkeep on their gear, consistent access to appropriately-dangerous work and a basic salary (paid only when they complete assignments that don't yield additional loot).

Perhaps most valuable of all, the Harpers guild hall gives adventurers a place to rally, form adventuring parties and exchange news, strategies and magic items.

Veteran Harpers who retire from field work or suffer crippling injuries have the option of continuing to work in the guild hall.
### Guild leadership
The guild has several key positions within its leadership:
* High Harper. The leader of the guild, in charge of assigning missions to field agents.
* Quartermaster. In charge of distributing and cataloguing the guild's magic items and resources.
* Dispatchers. Specialised conjurers who transport the field agents to and from their most urgent assignments.
### Adventuring parties
Harpers can enlist as a named party, a group of adventurers who are allowed to refuse an assignment unless they are all sent together.
### Relationships with other organisations
The Harpers are a fairly tight-knit group. While they will on occasion have to collaborate with other adventuring groups (such as the [[Imperial Rangers]] or the [[Blood Hunters]] orders), it is something the Harper leadership tends to avoid where possible.

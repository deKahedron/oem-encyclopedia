The Draymorites are a cult of demon worshippers composed of people who were sick of the dithering factions that had fuelled the [[The Glamoran Civil War|Glamoran Civil War]] for years. They want someone in power who will make changes with action instead of words.

Fully embracing all forms of Sacrum-banned magic, they summon demons to sow chaos throughout the country, which only their leaders will be able to rule over.

Their model their goals after the conquest of [[Arcaxius]] by the [[Twin Kings]] of [[House Draymor]].

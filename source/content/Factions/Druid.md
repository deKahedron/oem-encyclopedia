### Egregore network
Every druid is the host to a node of [[Egregore|egregore]]. This fungus subconsciously connects the druid to the full druidic network.

Druids are able to recognise each other instinctively. Wild animals are also able to recognise druids and are more likely to be friendly towards them.

The fungal network also imbues the druid with magical knowledge. Every druid is able to communicate in [[Druidic (Language)|Druidic]], the language of the spores. Many druids also gain the ability to cast [[Druidic Magic|druidic spells]], some of which are only known by the network.

All of the fungal influences on a druid's mind are entirely subconscious. Most druids live their entire lives without knowing what egregore is.
### Death and rebirth
Since egregore can't take root inside living humanoids, all druids are must be resurrected from a dead corpse infected with egregore. Druids don't remember their own deaths. Some of them don't remember anything about their lives before they died, while others do.

Druids know that they are able to create more druids by resurrecting the dead, but most of them don't understand the exact mechanism behind this.
### Wild Shape
The egregore spores have a unique effect on a druid's body, allowing them to transform into the shapes of beasts while maintaining their personality and mind.
### Iron aversion
Druids find the feeling of iron unpleasant on their skin. Touching iron feels like a numbing burn.
### Influence on historical events
Throughout history, the druids have enacted secret plots to sabotage great technological and magical civilizations. Some of the most prominent attacks include:
* Dispelling crucial *Teleportation Circles* to sabotage resource extraction operations.
* The murder of [[Bember Locksilver|Lord Bember Locksilver]], a wealthy nobleman who was trying to tear down a great forest in the [[The Pale Blot|Pale Blot]]. After killing him, he was reincarnated into a new form, from which he was unable to be restored.
* [[Great Humbling|The Great Humbling]]. The druids destroyed the entirety of Nadiria in collaboration with the [[Chalican|chalicans]]. The chalicans alone took the blame for the event.
* Working to undermine the [[The Six Liches|Six Liches]] of [[Arcaxius]], eventually contributing to their downfall.
### D'ruuda
On very rare occasions, the druids have acted in unison to achieve a single goal, coordinating across the continent. Often, these actions have been in response to astral alignments or to perform rituals that seem pointless to outsiders.

Druids that have been asked about these behaviours have described it feeling like a natural instinct, a gut feeling so strong as to be a compulsion.

The druids who participated in the Great Humbling barely remember the events of that day. The entire thing passed them by as an emotional haze, where they barely acted of their own will. This was the only time in living memory that the network has seized direct control of the druids.

There are references to an ancient being from the [[Age of Titans]] named D'ruuda. D'ruuda has never been physically described, but historians agree that the word "druid" likely shares a root with the name of this being.
### Druid pods
##### *Vainas de druidas*
Some druids form small communities, called pods. These communities are usually entirely self-sufficient but will often make deals with nearby settlements, blessing harvests in exchange for allowing areas to be reforested or calling off hunts.

Druid pods will often integrate with villages of [[Grung|grungs]], [[Firbolg|firbolgs]], [[Luruthi|luruthi]] or other isolationist cultures.
### Druid circles
Druids belong to different circles based on the abilities they are granted and their role within the collective. Some known circles are:
* Circle of the Land. Sherpas who guide well-intentioned strangers through their homelands.
* Circle of the Leaf. Protectors and restorers of plant life.
* Circle of the Moon. Scouts who oversee the world at large.
* Circle of the Shepherd. Protectors and restorers of animal life. 
* Circle of the Single Shape. Warriors who guard their home biome at the expense of others.
* Circle of Spores. Wardens of the natural circle of life and death.
* Circle of Stars. Prophets and teachers of the other druids.
* Circle of the Titan. Doomsday weapons, born to terminate the transgressions of a civilization.

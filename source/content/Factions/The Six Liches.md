### The Clocktower Covenant
In the 30th century, the Sovereign University of Arcaxius hosted fourteen archmages. Each of these archmages was allowed to hold their position and pay for as long as they were willing to teach classes on their assigned school of magic.

Professors [[Zaledrix Almeyar]] and [[Yurtris Fenbryte]] believed that Professor [[Andrej Rusakov]] was near a breakthrough on the fundamental understanding of the [[The Weave|Weave]], but that his worsening [[Chronic Divination|mental state]] would kill him before he could. Rusakov, believing that he really would need more than a human lifetime to complete his study, agreed to become a [[Lich|lich]].

The three professors knew that the rituals of lichdom were dangerous and extremely controversial. All known previous liches had had to kill thousands of people in order to complete their ascension. Through researching the rituals, the group discovered that the arcane release of thousands of deaths could be emulated by consuming the soul of one extremely powerful spellcaster.

In order to maximise their odds of success in a possible confrontation, Professor Fenbryte thought best to have half of the professors on their side and use the other half as sacrifices. 
Professor Almeyar saw in [[Wendallia Kirstein]] a similar case to Professor Rusakov. The onomancer had long been researching an alternative method of immortality, but her extreme age was catching up to her.
Professors [[Helem Thumlekh]] and [[Leein Galvanis]] were seen as ambitious enough to be tempted by the offer.
Finally, as the most experienced combatant among the archmages, [[Jaygo Coronal]] was invited to the conspiracy to provide an ensure they would not be stopped if they were discovered.

The seven conspirators swore an oath of loyalty among themselves. In order to prevent any one of them from betraying the others for power, they would all share a single communal [[Phylactery|phylactery]], hidden by Professor Almeyar somewhere almost no one else would ever be able to find: anchored to the passage of time. Almeyar themselves, having the most control over the phylactery, intended to act as an arbiter of the liches. If a time came when the cost of their undeath exceeded the value they were bringing to the world, they would cease to feed any more souls to the phylactery, leaving them all to wither into demiliches or die.
### The Withering
On the second Bloodsday of Autumn, 2910, using the chaos of the start of the academic year as a distraction, the conspirators put their plan into motion. In the early hours of the night, Professors Galvanis, Thumlekh and Coronal assassinated the elderly Professor [[Garthum Bleska]] in the [[Caeran|Library]] and imprisoned his soul in a *Soul Cage*. When the body was found, Professor Rusakov declared the death by natural causes. Bleska's soul was used to reanimate Jaygo Coronal as the first of the liches.

During the morning, the newly-reborn lich [[Jaygo Coronal|The War]] assassinated Professor [[Sora Hiramo]] in her tower, while Kirstein and Almeyar cornered and killed Professor [[Sharna Harrow]]. The souls of both archmages were used to complete the rituals for [[Yurtris Fenbryte|The Plenty]] and [[Andrej Rusakov|The Truth]], respectively.

In the early afternoon, the professor-in-training [[The Nameless Namer|Abakim]] discovered the murders, rallying a small group of archmages, powerful students and university staff to try and intervene.

After Professor [[Mali Abi-Dalzim]] tried to investigate, barely escaping with his life, the conspirators knew they had been found out. With Almeyar and Kirstein dead and awaiting reanimation, the conscious five split into three groups: Coronal would stand watch over the ritual site, while Rusakov and Fenbryte tried to kill Professor [[Jagrad Formin Kaliktis]] and Galvanis and Thumlekh ambushed Professor [[Nyngarinitan]] in his private jungle sanctuary.

Professors [[Ingritte Skårgard]] and Abi-Dalzim, alongside the student [[Yorum Teris]], were able to buy Kaliktis enough time to *Plane Shift* to safety.
Abakim was too late to save Nyngarinitan, whose soul had already been taken back to the ritual site.

The university head of security [[Diana Hexenwelt]] began evacuating all students, researchers and personnel to a secure dungeon at the edge of the jungle. While the process was underway, Professor Galvanis created a *Nystul's Magic Aura* that feigned a massive surge of necromantic magic, forcing the hand of the liches' enemies.

The ensuing skirmish was recorded as the First Battle in the Department of Chronurgy. During the conflict, Thumlekh was killed and Abakim escaped with her soul. Abi-Dalzim's soul was used to reanimate [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation]]. Kell and Skårgard were killed, but Skårgard's soul was liberated before it could be used to power another reanimation.

A grief-stricken Leein Galvanis consumed Nyngarinitan's soul to become [[Leein Galvanis|The Joy]] in an attempt to hunt down Abakim and recover his lover's soul. Deep in the Library, Abakim interrogated Thumlekh before fleeing to the Astral Plane, leaving behind their name and identity to escape the Salvation's onomancy, becoming [[The Nameless Namer]].

After failing to locate Abakim, The Salvation cast *Gate* to capture and kill Kaliktis. Once his soul was used to reanimate [[The Change]], the phylactery was sealed into [[The Broken Minute|the minute 19:51]].

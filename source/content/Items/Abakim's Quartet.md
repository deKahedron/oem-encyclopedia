### Effects
As a Bonus ction, you can wave this conductor's baton to conjure four ghostly, intangible string instruments in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet of you. The instruments play along with your melodies and harmonise to your greatest moments with beautiful countermelodies. When you deal 20 or more points of damage on your turn, you can cause one creature that you can see within 10 feet of one of the instruments to regain 2d8 Hit Points. When you restore 20 or more Hit Points on your turn, you can cause one creature you can see within 10 feet of one of the instruments to make a Wisdom saving throw. The creature takes 4d6 Thunder damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

As a Bonus Action on your turn, you can move each of the instruments to a new unoccupied space within 30 feet of you.

The instruments last for 1 hour. Once you conjure the quartet, you can’t do so again until the following dawn.

### Effects
When you make a ranged attack with this weapon, you can choose to cause a blast that pushes you 15 feet away from the target. The target must then make a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 feet away from you if it is Large or smaller. When you use this property, being within 5 feet of a hostile creature does not incur disadvantage on the attack. Once this property has been used, it can’t be used again until the following dawn.
### Description
The pistol was a standard-issue weapon given to soldiers in the [[Fuegil & the Manilands|Manilands]]. It has since been modified with small runic charms. The crest of the empire engraved on the handle is all but faded.

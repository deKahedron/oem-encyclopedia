### Effects
You gain a +1 to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. Immediately after you make an attack with this weapon on your turn, you can choose to teleport to the nearest unoccupied space to the space where you started your turn.
### Description
The weapon has a fine blade and a basket hilt made of a single long piece of steel curved around the grip in a winding path from crossguard to pommel.

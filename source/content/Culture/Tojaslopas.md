Tojaslopas is a winter feast celebrated by [[Human|humans]] of [[Human#Damaran|Damaran]] culture. The day is celebrated by sacrificing the eggs of dangerous monsters and eating egg-roe coulibiac.

The origins of the custom date back to a time when local adventurers had to raid monster lairs in the winter to exterminate their young while the adults hibernated.

Tojaslopas takes place every year on the sixth Godsday of Winter.

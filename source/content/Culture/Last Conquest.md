Last Conquest is a festival celebrated by the [[Orc#Orcs of Luthic|Luthic orcs]] of [[Korom Sul]] and [[Udak Sul]]. The festival takes place on the second Crownsday of Autumn.

The festival is held in honour of peace of [[Adanis]], whence the [[Myth King]] granted the [[Shadowy Mountains]] to the orcs and named them protectors of the range.

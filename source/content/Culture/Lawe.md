##### *Ley*
Lagu is a board game invented by the [[Giant|giants]] in which two players take turns moving pieces across a chequered board. The rules are identical to real-world chess, though the historical origin is different.

The aim of the game is to capture the opponent's Paragon. 
### Pieces
* **Paragon.** The most important piece. Can move one space in any direction. The game ends if the Paragon is threatened and has no safe moves available.
* **Fount.** Can move any number of spaces in a straight line.
* **Retainers.** Can move any number of spaces diagonally. Said to be based on a frost giant and a fire giant. Since they are locked to spaces of one colour, the spaces are themselves known as Snow (white) and Ash (black).
* **Masks.** Can move two spaces in one direction and then one space perpendicular to that. Can move over other pieces. Said to be based on cloud giants.
* **Discs.** Can move any number of spaces vertically or horizontally. Named after stone giants and their art of throwing flat boulders.
* **Hills.** Eight pieces arranged in front of the rest of the army, which can move one space forward or take another piece one space forward diagonally. Each one can move two spaces forward on its first move. Based on hill giants, the lowest of the true giants.

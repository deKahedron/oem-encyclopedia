Sophism is a religious system that originates from [[Gnome|gnomes]] and contemplates knowledge as a measure of spiritual purity.

According to Sophic beliefs, to hold information about one’s own sphere of influence can bring fulfilment, but only by expanding one’s knowledge beyond their own experiences can one truly achieve enlightenment.

The highest figure of worship is [[Oghma]], the God of All Knowledge.
### The Eight and Three
Sophism places great spiritual importance on eight mundane senses and three mystic senses. While the [[Forest Gnome|forest gnomes]] who founded this religion only had five mundane senses, their ability to communicate with small animals made them aware of the existence of the last three.
##### The Eight Mundane Senses
* **Sight**
* **Hearing**
* **Touch**
* **Smell**
* **Taste**
* **Echolocation**
* **Electroreception**
* **Lodesight (Magnetoreception)**
##### The Three Mystic Senses
* **Alethocula**. Known coloquially as *True Sight*, the ability to see through magical deceptions and transformations.
* **Pansophia**. Also known as omniscience. The ability to glean information from other places.
* **Pronoia**. Also known as foresight, the ability to predict information about future events.
### [[Divine Magic#Gods|Gods]]
Sophism is technically monotheistic. However, eight different aspects of [[Oghma]] are presented as manifestations of different kinds of knowledge. Less prominently, each of these aspects is associated with one of the mundane senses.
### [[Oghma]]
The central, all-knowing deity. Associated with the three mystic senses.
### [[Alti]]
The Goddess of Music and Writing. Associated with hearing.
### [[Bargas]]
The God of Kinship. Associated with smell.
### [[Kelim]]
The God of Tradition. Associated with taste.
### [[Lolth]]
The Goddess of Secrets. Associated with touch.
### [[Milil|The Milil Hive]]
Gods of Craft. Associated with sight.
### [[Solos]]
God of Navigation. Associated with electroreception.
### [[Viana]]
Goddess of Magic. Associated with echolocation.
### [[Zail]]
Goddess of Lost Knowledge. Associated with lodesight.
### The Glamoran Schism
In the year 3432, the sophic religion was torn apart by a schism in the [[The Glamoran Civil War|Glamoran Civil War]].

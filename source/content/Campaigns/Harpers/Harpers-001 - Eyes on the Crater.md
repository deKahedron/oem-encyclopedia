### Debrief
[[Maximilan Rudorof|Maximilan]] has reported on the situation. He and [[Flaakia]] are safe and are currently organising shelter for the [[Iacça|village’s]] survivors.

It seems a [[Oncehaven|colossal palace]] that has fallen from the sky onto the fields of the [[Court of Wine]]. According to [[Herikos]], these flying castles were common in the [[Age of Titans]], when cloud [[Giant|giants]] ruled their fiefs from atop them. Why it's crashed now and whether anyone is still inside, we still don't know.

Lady Vidtner and the mayor are both missing. Worse still, it seems the river water is infected with something. X advises burning the bodies.

Max says they came across a magical creature, possibly from the [[Underdark]], which used a cauldron to attack them with poison. Sounds to me like some sort of hag. It escaped them, so it could still be on the loose in the area. 

This leaves us with two open fronts in Iacça: the surface is still teeming with local monsters and opportunists that threaten the survivors as they rebuild their home. At the same time, we need experienced eyes and hands inside the palace, charting that place with all possible haste. Who knows what dangers and riches might lie within.
-[[Jo Parsley|Parsley]]

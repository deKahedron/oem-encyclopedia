After having escaped from their [[White Bank Heist|heist]] on the [[White Bank]], [[Unnamed StR Party|four adventurers]] arrive at the small village of [[Irrom Calisei]], in the shadow of [[Mount Jeong-Yo]].

The party are greeted at the town's border by [[Lu-zyo Hai]], a noble warrior in service to [[House Noven]]. [[Jenette Meirim]] introduces her companions: [[Kridix Thundersoul]], [[Mercy]] and [[Lode]].

Lu-zyo grants the adventurers refuge within the village. That night, as the livestock is being shepherded into their pens, a wild [[Wyvern|wyvern]] descends upon the village. The wyvern begins tearing into the sheep as Lu-zyo and the adventurers rush out to rescue the shepherds. Before the wyvern can take off with its prey, Kridix cripples it with a *Grasping Shot*, buying enough time for Mercy to transform into her *Behemoth* form for the first time. As the *Behemoth*, Mercy drags the creature away from the flock. As the wyvern struggles, Jenette notices a raw mark around its neck, as if it had been tied to something. Finally, Lode slays the wyvern with an extremely powerful *Witch Bolt*.

Kridix and Lode harvest several parts from the wyvern, including wing leather, venom and a few electrified teeth.

The following morning, Jenette awakes to the sound of a conversation outside their door. An agent of the White Bank is interrogating Lu-zyo about the location of four fugitives, two of them [[Tiefling|tieflings]]. Lu-zyo tells the agent that the travellers passed through the town some days prior, heading East.

As they part ways from the town, Jenette thanks Lu-zyo for his help.

The adventurers then continue their path, up towards the base of Mount Jeong-Yo and then [[Newhearth]].

After having destroyed the [[Orb of Dragonkind]], [[Mercy]] and [[Lode]] are left alone for a time in the back room of [[Cassic and Mograve's Trove of Antiquities]]. Mercy pockets Mograve's cane and Lode steals an old facemask.

Somewhat rattled by the experience, the two claim their payment from [[Ormus Cassic]] and head to the [[Two-Score Strings]] tavern, where they previously saw a [[Tiefling|tiefling]] enjoying herself. They find the tavern in full celebration: [[Candleguard|The Candleguard]] have just cashed in a large bounty for the demon Thyrotrokar, earning a night of free drinks for all the [[Harpers]]. Lode tells [[Hespîr|the barman]] that he is a new Harper. When Hespîr expresses his doubt about that fact, [[Anya Orváes]] intervenes, asking whether Lode intends to join the Harpers and promising to fudge the paperwork to pretend he is already enlisted.

Mercy makes eye contact with [[Byugo of Hallum]], whom she recognises intuitively as a fellow [[Druid|druid]]. Some minutes later, Byugo tries to speak with Mercy, who orders a round for everyone and flees the tavern.

Mercy locates [[The Cloudy Mattress]] inn, books a room for two and runs a hot bath with fragrance stones.

When Lode finally arrives, he finds Mercy collapsed in bed but awake enough to recommend a similar bath. Lode tries a couple of stones that smell of the sea and drifts off to sleep, dreaming of being cradled by the eternal storms of the [[Bay of Grief]].

The following day, Mercy goes to the [[Pantheon (Newhearth)|Pantheon]], hoping to find a shrine to [[Fomorian]]. In its absence, she gives a simple prayer to [[Annam|Annam All-Father]], on behalf of his son. Before leaving, she encounters a [[Gnoll|gnoll]] [[Voice of the Reborn River|priest]], who suggests that her god may not be any less important for lacking a shrine. Using [[Ta'ux#Rebirth as the gnoll god of redemption|her own goddess]] as an example, he posits that the gods of the downtrodden are more likely to take after their followers, eventually becoming the deity their followers need.

As Mercy leaves the Pantheon, Lode arrives. He slowly makes the round of all the shrines, noting all the names and symbols to which his dozens of victims appealed in their final moments. As a small token of apology and remembrance, he places a manticore spine at the foot of each shrine.

That afternoon, the two tieflings discuss the merits of joining the Harpers. Hoping to have some level of security and community, they agree to enlist.

That afternoon, they enter the [[Tower of Sheathed Blades]] to speak with Anya Orváes. After [[Rhea Istaros]] confirms their lack of existing allegiances or unpunished crimes, Lode and Mercy are sworn in as Harpers.

Over the following days, Mercy purchases a [[Sallihhka|sallihhka]] greatsword and a suit of scale armour, then spends some time with the innkeeper [[Larayoctrithia]] learning how to cook [[Ithkrip|ithkrip]]. Lode infuses himself with [[Wyvern|wyvern]] blood and practices his [[Gnomish]] with [[Epidma Hopstilts]].

As [[Unnamed StR Party|the adventurers]] begin to trek [[Mount Jeong-Yo#Pariah's Climb|Pariah's Climb]], [[Kridix Thundersoul|Kridix]] takes note of the inscription at the start of the path, relaying only the general meaning to his companions.

After the first day of climbing, the group encounters a large snowfall, which they circumvent by tying themselves to [[Mercy|Mercy's]] *Behemoth* form. After seeing the beast's chains up close and in daylight, [[Jenette Meirim|Jenette]] questions Mercy as to her comfort and stability in this form. Mercy refuses to open up about the matter, but takes Jenette's advice to confide in someone trustworthy by reaching out to [[Lode]].

That night, the group camps in a small cave with the desecrated grave of a dragonborn adventurer named Ccelec. They are suspicious of a foul-smelling tunnel next to their own. That night, their fears are proven true, as a manticore returns to roost and attacks them after detecting their scent.

Mercy's *Insectoid* form disables the manticore, setting Jenette up for a killing blow. Searching the cave, Lode finds the [[Magic Item|magic rapier]] [[Indecision]], once belonging to the dead dragonborn next door.

That night, a group of ice mephits approach the sleeping party, intent on playing their deadly pranks. Kridix, who is on guard, scares them off. After they wander into the manticore's cave, Kridix follows them to keep an eye on them. The mephits become fascinated by Kridix's weaponry and *Prestidigitation*. He is able to convince them that there is a barrier of heat that will harm them if them leave the cave.

The following day, Jenette, Mercy and Lode question Kridix about the surprisingly large number of mephits in the neighbouring cave, which he handwaves as a non-threat.

Later in the day, as the group is climbing across a dangerous chasm, they hear a gunshot far above them. As a yeti falls down the cliff past them, they hear the sound of an oncoming avalanche.

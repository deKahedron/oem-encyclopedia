[[Kridix Thundersoul|Kridix]] and [[Jenette Meirim|Jenette]] find themselves on the safe side of an ice shelf as the avalanche rolls near, but [[Lode]] and [[Mercy]] remain behind. Lode is able to leap across, helped up by the others. Mercy struggles on the jump, falling down. Kridix pins her to the cliffside with a magical bolt, long enough for Lode to extend her a rope.

The four adventurers reach the next safe ice shelf as the avalanche slides past. Mercy points out that someone must have fired the gun that started the avalanche and they may still be buried under the snow.

As they begin searching the snowbanks, they are beset by an agitated air elemental. While Mercy and Lode desperately try to fight it, a wounded Kridix locates the unconscious and hypothermic [[Epidma Hopstilts]] in the snow. As Kridix carries the gnome away from danger, Jenette remembers the warning she read at the base of the mountain, urging the tiefling to stop fighting the elemental and run away with her.

Once they are safe, Epidma wakes up. She thanks the adventurers for saving her, gifting her pistol to Kridix. She accepts the food and dry clothes she is offered, and tells them about the friends she lost on the climb.

Mercy thanks Jenette for helping her. Despite her own grievances with nobility, and after acknowledging the noblewoman's complete lack of tact, Mercy declares that she believes Jenette to be a good person.

Later, at the summit, the five travellers are greeted into [[Newhearth]] by [[Rhea Istaros]] of the [[Harpers]]. Eppy is stricken with the emotion of having carried the memory of her friends to their final destination. Still, Mercy convinces her to seek medical help before enlisting as a Harper.

The group make their way to [[Cassic and Mograve's Trove of Antiquities]]. On the way, Lode makes note of the fact that [[Tiefling|tieflings]] seem to be accepted here.

As they enter the weathered old antiques shop, Jenette notices a suspicious onlooker and asks Kridix to tail him. Kridix pursues the spy down a few alleys before losing him, realising that the spy must be teleporting.

Within the shop, the group meet [[Ormus Cassic]] who, after realising who they are, allows them into the back of the shop to speak with [[Victor Mograve]]. Mograve is visibly nervous. Jenette takes note of his severed finger, a common punishment for thieves. mercy notices the muting effect of the back room, indicative of Abjuration magic.

Jenette and Lode don't want to give Mograve the orb right away, asking him why he is so jittery. Mograve explains the function of the orb, how it compels the white [[Dragon|dragons]] of [[Bastorin's Spawn]] to remain in [[Liengard]] despite how densely they overlap each other's territories. As an appeal to Lode and Mercy, he describes it as a chain, conscripting generations of dragons to a task they have no choice to leave.

When Kridix arrives with the orb, Mograve places it on the table and raises his cane to smash it. Before he can perform the deed, the front door opens and [[Mother Mint]] arrives with two arcanists, one of whom was the spy Kridix followed. The drow matriarch demands to have the orb returned. To keep her away from the artefact, Mograve leaves to the front of the shop, tailed by Jenette and Kridix. Kridix resists a magical command from Mother Mint asking about the location of the orb. Jenette attempts to stall for time. As Mint approaches the door, Mograve promises her that the orb is already gone.

Within the back room, Lode and Mercy share a moment. Hearing the conversation in the front, they choose to save another race from the fate that befell them. Raising the dragon-claw cane between them, they bring it down upon the orb, shattering it.

In the aftermath of the ensuing shock, a bewildered Mother Mint arrests Mograve and swears that he will help her fix this mess. Threatening them should they break their silence, she allows Jenette and her companions to remain free, as mere pawns in this transgression.

Picking up the pieces of a broken vase, Ormus asks for help cleaning up his shop.

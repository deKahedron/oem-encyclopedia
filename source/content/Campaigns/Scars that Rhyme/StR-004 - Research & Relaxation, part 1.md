After the bankers leave, [[Kridix Thundersoul|Kridix]] and [[Jenette Meirim|Jenette]] help [[Ormus Cassic]] tidy up his shop. In the process, Jenette snoops on some of [[Victor Mograve|Victor Mograve's]] open research. She finds that he has been compiling disparate fragments and rumours of prophecies. In particular, Mograve has been studying various different languages foretelling of some sort of "Master of Dragons". The different interpretations use different words with very different connotations, from "Master" to "Rider" to "Leader".
Additionally, she identifies a bookmarked tome by [[Wanda Humbolt]], recounting the murder of [[Joesyph Rooker]].

Ormus gives Jenette a bag full of [[Lawe]] pieces. Some pieces are missing and he would like Jenette's father to make replacements. He confesses that this was to be the cover story if anyone asked why she had visited the shop. Finally, he says that, should they find anything of note regarding Victor's prophecy research, the [[Cassic and Mograve's Trove of Antiquities|Trove]] would be willing to pay for the information.

Jenette's investigation takes her to the [[Pantheon (Newhearth)|Pantheon]]. Over the course a couple of days, she listens in intently to conversations in the temples of [[Oghma]] and [[Annam]]. In the latter, she overhears a conversation regarding a message from [[Annam's Chisel]] and that something called [[Parodochus]] might be needed to understand the full message.

Hoping to find a library to research this Parodochus, Jenette and Kridix head to the [[Tower of Sheathed Blades]] to consult the [[Harpers]]. They are greeted by [[Rega Anganott]] and [[Krien-Lev Dardassos]] of the [[Candleguard]], among others. Krien-Lev very eagerly shows Kridix some of his own weaponry.

Jenette meets High Harper [[Quentyn Yerrian]], who allows her to visit the top of the tower with [[Quaal|Quaal's]] oversight. 

While Jenette researches in the library, accompanied by [[Maximilan Rudorof]], Kridix pretends to be a new Harper called Karl to get a free training session with [[Anya Orváes]].

Jenette discovers two references to Parodochus: one attributing it with fixing the date of the Ostorian [[Ostorian Calendar|year 0]] before the event came to pass. The second reference lists it as one name among [[Fomorian|Fomorian's]] many companions. With this information, she approaches the priestess of Annam directly and asks about the name. The most she is able to get is that Parodochus was likely a [[Giant|giant]].

###### *A summary of [[Wendallia Kirstein|The Salvation's]] research notes from developing the [[Clone]] spell.*

Versions 1-37: The flesh does not respond to direction. The flesh spoiled sooner than expected. The flesh merged into a single block. 

V-38: The flesh has produced a recognizable artery, a ligament and a piece of nondescript bone. I have labelled these samples as V-38A, V-38B and V-38C. 

Versions 39-44: No response from the flesh, the previous step forward has not been replicated. 

V-45: Small fragments of recognizable organ have sprouted again. The repetition is promising, but my body feels the toll. Attempt count exceeding optimistic expectations. Experiment with alternative sources of body matter. Reduce sacrificed flesh; replace clean water with organic residue.

V-46: The new formula has proven a surprise boon. The mix of different base organisms seems enthusiastic to join into new forms. A piece of notable size had both hair and scales (V-46G). Lesser flesh, but will prove suitable for testing.

47-79: Progressively larger fragments. Most batches produce multiple recognizable organs, though some are definitively marine. The urns no longer fit in my tower. *Scratched out below this is the suggestion to ask [[Yurtris Fenbryte|Yurtris]] for an expansion.*

V78-B: I have been unable to tell if the piece congealed was ever alive. However, *Gentle Repose* confirms that something in there is definitely dead. 

V86-V: It stands. It does not respond to stimulus, but is able to hold balance. Its face is disgusting. 

V92-C: A worm with arms. A half-formed, mockery of my face, babbling incoherently. It cries when illuminated, it cowers when observed. A step forward. Occasional flashes of ectoplasm-like images appear around the creature, but disappear immediately.

V096-J: A stable enough foundation is undeniable. My work is within my grasp and I require only iteration to perfect it. Flashing images, appendages?

V104-D and V104-L: ~~Two separate beings from the same pod. Investigate cause.~~ Correction, drainage revealed the two pieces were connected by a single strand of brain tissue, effectively one creature.

V109-E: This one thrashed about unpleasantly, and spoke. It cried out my name before being silenced. I am glad for their repulsive forms.

V131-A: Subject repeated my exact words from a 50-minute conversation with Andrej yesterday, before coughing up blood and bile and dying.

V150-B: Subject has no nose or eyes, but a tongue with a shelled crustacean face. Tongues revealed this miniature sub-creature to be speaking a dialect of [[Primordial]], though it used vocabulary unknown to me.

V172-C: A replica of a left hand, close to my own in my youth. Inert.

V172-F: A living creature, almost entirely fish-like. Its left fin is contorted and stretched into a humanoid shoulder bone.

V209-F: Malformed, broken. Again this ghostly image appears. [[Andrej Rusakov|Andrej]] compared it to an *Astral Projection*. I disagree. The image has a name.

V284-L: Result stands and moves on its own strength. It responds to verbal commands. Test longevity under working conditions.

V286-J: Stronger than predecessor. Final lifespan: 3 days.

V295-T: Shorter limbs. Slower and more robust limbs. Like [[Gwendolyn]]. Broke down after 12 days. Killed.

V314-N: Apparition again. The ghost has short limbs. An echo of the previous subject? Implied consciousness? The ghost follows version 314-N, mimics its actions, though inaccurately.

[[V331-C]]: A wild, overgrown abomination. Long hairs that discharge lightning. Coated in a membranous layer. Useless for desired purpose, though potentially interesting to explore in the future.

V387-D: Covered in spines, killed by its own movements.

V428-A: Mostly liquid. Unfortunately similar to early failures.

V430: Inert.

V438-I: Lucid, responsive. Final lifespan: 107 days.

V449-K: Animated prematurely; attempted to attack.

[[V451-E]]: Another echo. Several similar recent versions make it difficult to discern what it imitates.

V474-Q: Surprisingly durable. Much like a long-lasting unseen servant. Performed tasks to a varying degree of success.

V561-D: Frail. One broken leg, soft exoskeletal back structure. Greater mental agility than predecessors, able to perform simple operations. Final lifespan: 51 days.

V583-I: Durable and agile. A functional servant. Able to relay messages and understand complex assignments. Learning to read. Final lifespan: 4 years, killed by paranoid researcher [[Marleta Colizza]].

V607-E: Dressed in its predecessor's uniform, was able to stalk the meddler for 29 days before being struck down.

V651-F: Subject has learned how to command the echo that follows it. It uses the echo to torment Colizza, feeding her paranoia. It is most entertaining.

[[V680-A]]: Subject shows very impressive sense of direction and human understanding. Was able to track down target over many miles and kill her in her house. I hear that old Marleta's screams awoke her whole family. They had never believed her. Final lifespan: 19 years.

V745-D: Escaped using echo? Killed 680-A when it was sent to catch it. Must do everything myself it seems.

[[Ashes|V771-M]]: Unintelligent, but agile and obedient. Shows unusual skill with its echo. "Willa" has found a way of telepathically seeing through its echo. Study of a telepathically awakened child has shown no similar behaviours. Not telepathy? The subject has made friends. They call it by a name I never should have given it.

V903-S: I believe I have located the source of the leakage problem. No more undesired lucidity.

V904-B: Failing in the same direction enough times makes one forget her goal. Finally, a true success. Body is not alive, but could be animated. Still, physically deteriorated.

V931-J: Version is within acceptable margins of desired result.

V962-A: Five successes in a row. Spell complete.

[[V972-R]]: A lesson learned from a mistake along the way. Selected for minimal chance of developing independence. Mentally conditioned over time to be obedient. An improved Willa.

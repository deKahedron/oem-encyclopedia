*Sencode*, also known as Sending Code, is a constructed [[Languages|language]] designed to maximise the efficiency of each word. It has an extremely modular etymology, with an extreme wealth of prefixes, suffixes and modes. It was invented to circumvent the word limit on spells like Sending.

A [[Spellcaster|spellcaster]] who knows this language can communicate twice as much information in each casting of Sending, provided the recipient also knows the language.

In order to learn this language, a creature must have an Intelligence score of at least 13.

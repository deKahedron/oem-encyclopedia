Terran is a dialect of [[Primordial]]. It is a percussive [[Languages|language]], whose original speakers could communicate only through physical contact. Modern Terran is very similar to *Morse Code*, consisting of long and short knocks and scrapes. Aside from true earth [[Elemental|elementals]], the main speakers of the language are the [[Lizardfolk]], who can communicate in very precise and powerful clicks produced from their throats.
